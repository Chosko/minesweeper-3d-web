# Task 194 — Best-time chart: axis ticks with the fewest decimals that tell them apart

Target: claude

## Goal
Stop the Records best-time chart's time axis from reading like thousands.
The UX review drift audit (`docs/UX_REVIEW_DRIFT.md` § Defects outside the
numbered items, "Best-time chart ticks read as thousands") found the y-axis
labels read "2.500 s" and "0 s": the thousandths time format of P-46
(`formatTime`) is applied to axis ticks, where "2.500" reads as two thousand
five hundred. P-46's approved solution in `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` puts
thousandths on recorded times (results, Records figures, the viewer), not on
the round values of a chart scale.

Steps to reproduce (from the audit): win a Classic 2D board at least once →
Records → the best-time-by-date chart's time axis shows "2.500 s" (or a
similar three-decimal tick) above "0 s".

## Acceptance criteria
- The best-time chart's time-axis labels use the fewest decimals (0 to 3)
  that keep every label of that axis distinct, trailing zeros dropped, with
  the unit: a 0–5 s axis reads "5 s", "2.5 s", "0 s"; a 0–100 s axis reads
  "100 s", "50 s", "0 s"; a 0–2.5 s axis reads "2.5 s", "1.25 s", "0 s".
- The hover readout, the text alternative and every other time on the
  Records screen keep the thousandths format (`formatTime`).
- The history chart's 3BV/s and efficiency axes are unchanged unless they
  show the same defect, in which case they follow the same rule.
- **Tests first**: tests/records-history.test.mjs pins the tick labels for
  the three example axes above and that the hover readout still shows
  thousandths. It fails on the current build.

## Decisions
- **"Fewest decimals that tell them apart"** is the audit's prescribed rule;
  it is applied per axis (all ticks of one axis compared together), so a
  "1.25 s" tick does not force "2.50 s" on its neighbour.
- Settled without conflict: `.claude/domain/features/records-screen.md`
  § Architecture describes "a time axis from 0" and fixes no tick format;
  the document does not change.
