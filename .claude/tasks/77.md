# Task 77 — Replay viewer screen with the 2D viewer, controls and overlay

Target: claude

## Goal
Add the replay viewer of feature `replay-playback` as a game-shell screen:
it opens a replay or a replay id from the library, verifies it, and plays
Classic 2D replays on the read-only board view with the recorded cursor,
under a playback clock with play, pause, speed, seek and step controls and
an overlay of the replay's figures.

## Acceptance criteria
- The shell routes to the viewer with a replay, or a replay id it fetches
  from the library, and the screen to return to; Back returns there.
- Every replay is verified before playing; on refusal or failed verification
  the viewer says why ("made by a newer version", "can no longer be
  reproduced", "unreadable") and Back still works.
- The playback clock advances replay time at 0.5×, 1×, 2× or 4× while
  playing and drives the simulator once per frame; the controls offer play
  and pause, speed, a seek bar and step to the previous or next action,
  operable by mouse, keyboard and controller.
- The overlay shows the replay clock, mines left, 3BV solved of 3BV and
  3BV/s so far, read from the simulator.
- The 2D viewer draws the Classic 2D board view read-only from the
  simulator's changes, the recorded pointer as a cursor interpolated
  linearly between samples, and the recorded keyboard and controller cursor
  as the focus ring.
- At the end the final board stays with the outcome and recorded time; play
  restarts from the beginning.
- The viewer is a shell screen, not a mode: it reports nothing to records,
  plays no sound, and is never paused into hiding the board.
- Node tests cover the clock, interpolation and control logic; Playwright
  plays a recorded 2D replay from fixture through to the end.
