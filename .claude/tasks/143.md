# Task 143 — Keyboard-only 3D

Target: claude

## Goal
Make the 3D mode playable with a keyboard alone, without changing how it
plays with a mouse or a controller. This is row W-24 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md`
§ Approved scope and work plan (Area: Input, 3D, Size: Medium, Phase 4;
"free keys only, mouse play untouched"). It closes P-40 of that document
under its decision 12 ("Build it without degrading mouse and keyboard play.
D and F are already taken in 3D (strafe, fullscreen), so the task picks free
keys."). Today 3D is unplayable with a keyboard alone: Enter starts and WASD
fly, but no key looks, reveals, flags or chords, and the arrows do nothing;
neither the Controls panel nor the ready card says a mouse or a controller
is required (P-40, Major, keyboard-only players). P-40's proposed solution
— keyboard look on the arrows at a rate tied to Look sensitivity, plus
reveal, flag and chord keys on the aimed cube — is built on the keys still
free in 3D play, since the 2D letters D and F are taken. The work serves
keyboard-only players: those without a pointing device, and those who cannot
use one.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Keyboard look on the arrows** (P-40): during 3D play, holding
  `ArrowLeft` / `ArrowRight` turns the camera left / right and `ArrowUp` /
  `ArrowDown` looks up / down, through the same path as mouse and stick look
  (`FlyCamera.look`), so the pitch clamp holds, the Look sensitivity setting
  scales the rate and Invert Y flips the vertical arrows exactly as it flips
  the mouse and the right stick. The rate at sensitivity 1 starts at 30°/s
  and ramps linearly to 120°/s over 0.5 s of holding an arrow on that axis
  (named constants in `js/input.js`), so a tap aims finely and a hold turns
  quickly; the two axes are independent, so two arrows held together look
  diagonally, and opposite arrows on one axis cancel. Keyboard look works
  wherever mouse look works and nowhere else (not while paused, on the ready
  card, on a failed board's offer or during the 3D end delay), and a held
  arrow keeps the frame loop drawing (render on demand stays idle once no
  arrow is held).
- **Reveal, flag and chord keys** (P-40, decision 12): in 3D play `Enter`
  reveals the aimed cube and `'` (`Quote`) flags or unflags it, both acting
  on the key's release; holding both and releasing either chords, exactly as
  Left + Right does with the mouse. They drive the same `MouseActions`
  instance the mouse buttons and the controller triggers drive — `Enter` as
  button 0, `Quote` as button 2 — so every rule of the release state machine
  (act on release, the chord toggle, a release with no matching press does
  nothing, the selection guard) is shared and the flag key does what a right
  click does, on a number included. Key auto-repeat presses nothing more.
  Holding a key shows what holding the matching mouse button shows. The keys
  act only where a mouse button or trigger acts on the board (3D play with
  pointer lock or lockless, no failed-board offer, not during the end
  delay), and held keys are dropped without firing wherever held mouse
  buttons are (blur, tab hide, lock loss, pause, leaving play).
- **No key leaks out of play**: while 3D play is active, the browser default
  of the arrows (page scroll), `Enter` (activating a focused button) and
  `'` (Firefox's quick find) is prevented; outside play these keys keep
  their current meaning — the arrows' focus moves on menus and cards, and
  `Enter` activating the focused control. The `Enter` that starts the game
  from the ready card does not also reveal: its release reaches play with no
  matching press.
- **Free keys only** (decision 12): no key already bound in 3D play changes
  meaning — W A S D Q E, Shift, Space, Ctrl, H, M, F, Esc, and the keys
  pending tasks claim in 3D (`R` and `Home`, task 125; `X`, task 139; `F2`,
  task 130). Mouse look, the mouse buttons, the wheel, the controller's
  sticks, triggers and buttons, and pointer lock work exactly as before.
- **Bindings source and help** (P-40): `BINDINGS['3d'].keyboard` in
  `js/settings/bindings.js` gains the rows for the arrows (look around),
  `Enter` (reveal the aimed cube, on release), `'` (flag / unflag, on
  release) and `Enter` + `'` (chord), so the Settings page's Bindings panel,
  the controls modal and the in-game help (task 119's bindings component
  where it has landed) list them from that one source. While the ready
  card's hand-written key list exists (`index.html` `.ready-keys`), it gains
  one line for the keyboard: arrows look, `Enter` reveal, `'` flag,
  `Enter` + `'` chord.
- **Replays**: a game played with keyboard look and the keyboard keys
  records and replays like a mouse game (actions by cell, the camera through
  the 3D sampler), with no change to the replay format; the replays in
  `tests/fixtures/replays/` still verify.
- **Tests first**: a new `tests/input-keyboard-3d.test.mjs` pins:
  - keyboard look: the turn after holding an arrow for 0.1 s, 0.5 s and 1 s
    at sensitivity 1 and 2, the ramp's start and cap, Invert Y flipping only
    the vertical arrows, the pitch clamp, diagonal and cancelling arrows,
    and no change with no arrow held;
  - the key to action mapping: `Enter` and `Quote` press and release
    sequences produce `left`, `right` and `chord` on release in both release
    orders, a release with no press and an auto-repeat do nothing, a key
    mixed with a mouse button chords as two mouse buttons do, and
    `releaseAll` drops a held key without firing;
  - **mouse and controller unchanged**: a table of mouse button sequences
    (single clicks, Left + Right in both release orders, a release without a
    press, a reset mid-hold) and of trigger sequences gives exactly the
    action stream it gives before this task (the expected streams written
    before the code changes), and `FlyCamera.look` / `lookAxes` give the
    same pose for the same inputs. `tests/gamepad.test.mjs` passes
    unedited.
  `tests/settings-page.test.mjs` pins the new bindings rows;
  `tests/replay-3d.test.mjs` records a short game played with keyboard look
  and the keyboard keys and checks it replays to the same final state. A
  Playwright check starts a 3D board with `Enter` on the ready card, looks
  with the arrows, reveals, flags and chords with the keys alone, and
  confirms the mouse still looks and acts the same way, checked by eye.
- **What not to break** (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Decisions taken):
  release-to-act with drag-to-cancel in both modes; the reference input
  conventions — 0.2°/px mouse look, the pitch clamp, 50 u/s, picking from
  the screen centre, the Shift / Space / Ctrl view modes; 3BV, click and
  efficiency accounting (a key action counts as the mouse action it
  replaces); replays recorded by earlier builds.
- `README.md`'s § 3D controls table lists the keyboard look and the three
  keys, its release sentence covers keys as well as mouse buttons, and its
  touch-devices line stops implying that 3D needs a mouse.
  `docs/ORIGINAL_SPEC.md` keeps describing the original and gains "Web
  port:" notes, in the form of the one on `Esc` / `F5`, under § Controls on
  the mouse look line (arrow-key look through the same sensitivity and
  invert Y) and on the mouse actions line (`Enter` and `'` drive the same
  release logic as Left and Right).
- `.claude/domain/features/settings.md` states the agreed design change —
  look sensitivity and invert Y apply to keyboard look in 3D as well as to
  mouse and controller look — in every passage that stated the old one
  (§ Scope's Controls item). Nothing else in the document changes.
- `.claude/domain/features/3d-play-flow.md` states the agreed design change
  — the 3D game session's actions come from the existing mouse state
  machine, fed by the mouse buttons, the controller triggers and the
  keyboard's reveal and flag keys — in every passage that stated the old one
  (§ Architecture, the 3D game session). Nothing else in the document
  changes.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction — `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` decision 12 and P-40, approved under
  that decision (row W-24: "free keys only, mouse play untouched").
  - `features/settings.md` § Scope says look sensitivity and invert Y apply
    to mouse and controller look in 3D; they also apply to keyboard look
    (P-40: "at a rate tied to Look sensitivity").
  - `features/3d-play-flow.md` § Architecture says the 3D game session
    drives actions from the mouse state machine and the controller mapping;
    the keyboard's reveal and flag keys feed the same state machine.

  Settled here, without conflict: the 3D mode keeps today's pointer-lock and
  lockless behaviour (`3d-play-flow.md` § Architecture, the mode adapter).
  Both documents are normally `/architect`'s; this task updates them for
  exactly this and for nothing else. A further design decision met at
  implementation time is not covered: leave that passage untouched and name
  it under *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
- **`Enter` reveals, `'` flags, both together chord.** P-40's `F` and `D`
  are taken in 3D (decision 12), and every key the other 3D tasks claim is
  out. With the right hand on the arrows, `Enter` and `'` sit beside each
  other just above them on the common layouts, so one hand looks, reveals,
  flags and chords while the left stays on WASD. A two-key chord mirrors the
  mouse (Left + Right) and the controller (LT + RT) on the same state
  machine, so chord semantics stay identical across devices with no second
  state machine and no new action type, and the 3D action set stays reveal
  and flag, as the planned `input-rebinding` catalogue declares it. Rejected:
  `Tab` (focus navigation; `input-rebinding` reserves it as a key the browser
  keeps), `Backspace` (under the same finger as `Enter`, so the chord is
  awkward) and `/` (farther from `Enter`; Firefox quick find). Keys are
  matched by `event.code`, so the physical position holds on any layout.
- **Keys share the mouse's `MouseActions` instance**, as the controller
  triggers do, so a key and a mouse button held together chord just as a
  trigger and a mouse button do today.
- **"Press shows the pressed state" is read as the mouse's press.** The 3D
  mouse press shows no separate pressed look today (the original acts on
  release with no press feedback), and the mouse path must stay untouched;
  a key press therefore shows what a mouse press shows, and a pressed look
  for keys alone would make the devices disagree.
- **Ramped look rate.** A constant rate is either too coarse to aim at one
  cube with a tap or too slow to turn round; 30°/s rising to 120°/s over
  0.5 s gives both, and both figures scale with Look sensitivity
  (0.25–3×). The controller keeps its 180°/s stick rate.
- **Pointer lock stays the start.** `Enter` on the ready card takes pointer
  lock as a click does; starting keyboard play lockless would leave a mouse
  player who presses `Enter` without mouse look, which decision 12 forbids.
- **No interim "3D needs a mouse or a controller" line.** P-40 proposes that
  line on the ready card only until keyboard look exists; this task ships
  keyboard look, so the ready card lists the keyboard keys instead.
- **Boundary with task 119** (W-01): 119 owns the help surfaces and their
  copy; this task adds its rows to the bindings source and, while the
  hand-written ready card list exists, to it too, so either task can land
  first.
- **Boundary with task 122** (W-05): 122 gives the arrows focus moves on
  menus, the ready card and the pause card; this task gives them look in
  3D play only, so the two never meet.
- **Out of scope:** rebinding and a left-handed preset (P-44, waits for the
  `input-rebinding` feature); the orbit camera (I-15, W-34, after this
  task); camera presets and snap aiming (I-12, I-13, deferred); touch
  controls for 3D (W-23); Classic 2D's keyboard, already complete.
