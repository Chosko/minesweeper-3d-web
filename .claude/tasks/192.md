# Task 192 — 3D ready and results cards: key hints that match the device and the screen

Target: claude

## Goal
Fix two 3D cards whose hint lines name the wrong input. The UX review drift
audit (`docs/UX_REVIEW_DRIFT.md` § Defects outside the numbered items) found:

1. **The 3D ready card on touch names keys.** On a coarse-only device the
   ready card ("Tap to play") swaps its controls rows for touch gestures,
   but its "How 3D works" block still reads "Empty and solved cubes hide
   themselves; hold Ctrl to show them" (index.html, `#ready-rules`), and
   the row under the card still reads "Esc: Back" — touch has a Ctrl button
   on the view-modes toolbar and no Esc key.
2. **The 3D results card's Esc hint says "Back".** The results card shows
   "Esc: Back" (index.html, `#results` `[data-esc-hint]`), but on a 3D
   results card Esc opens *Look at the board* (task 140, P-22 and
   decision 4 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md`).

Both are hint copy on 3D cards that does not follow the device or the
screen's actual Esc action; P-01's approved solution (help fed by the active
mode and device) and P-07's "Esc: Back beside every Back" (a hint only where
Esc does Back) set the rule.

Steps to reproduce (from the audit): (1) 390 × 844 touch emulation → 3D →
any preset → the "Tap to play" card's rules block says "hold Ctrl to show
them" and the Back row shows "Esc: Back". (2) Desktop, 3D → finish a game
(win or loss) → the results card shows "Esc: Back"; pressing Esc opens
*Look at the board*.

## Acceptance criteria
- On a coarse-only device (the touch ready card, `ready--touch`), the
  "How 3D works" line about hidden cubes names the toolbar's Ctrl button
  ("tap Ctrl to show them" or equivalent), and the "Esc: Back" hint beside
  the ready card's Back is hidden. The Back button itself stays. With a
  mouse and keyboard (and with a controller) both read as today.
- On a 3D results card the Esc hint reads "Esc: Look at the board". A
  Classic 2D results card keeps its current hint. Any card or state where
  *Look at the board* is not offered keeps a hint matching what Esc does
  there.
- **Tests first**: tests/shell-first-use.test.mjs (or the test that pins
  `#ready-rules`) pins the touch wording and the hidden Esc hint on a
  coarse-only ready card and the unchanged desktop wording;
  tests/results.test.mjs (or tests/shell-mode-3d.test.mjs) pins "Esc: Look
  at the board" on a 3D results card and the unchanged 2D hint. The tests
  fail on the current build.

## Decisions
- **One task for both**: same files (index.html, js/ui.js), same kind of
  fix — a hint line chosen by device or by screen — and the same tests.
- **Touch wording follows the touch rows already on the card** ("Shift,
  Space and Ctrl on the right: a tap turns each on or off"), so the rules
  line says tap, not hold.
- **The touch ready card's Esc hint is hidden, not reworded**: the visible
  Back button is the touch action; nothing else replaces the hint.
- Settled without conflict: `.claude/domain/features/3d-results-records-screens.md`
  § Interfaces ("Look at the board — ... Back on that screen (Esc, the
  controller's back buttons)") already makes Esc *Look at the board* on the
  3D results screen, and `.claude/domain/features/game-shell.md` puts an
  "Esc: Back" hint only beside a Back that Esc triggers. Neither document
  changes.
- **Out of scope**: "Esc: Back" hints on other screens shown on touch
  devices — the audit names only the ready card.
