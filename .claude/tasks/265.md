# Task 265 — The theme skill reads Hexagon and Triangle board sheets

Target: claude

## Goal
Part of feature `hex-tri-board-art`: `/theme-implement` reads a theme's
Hexagon and Triangle board sheets beside its 2D board sheet, so a later
theme supplies its per-grid gap and radius and its numbers 9-12 from its
canvas, and a theme that fails the per-grid checks is not registered.

## Acceptance criteria
- The skill's reading reference lists the `<Theme> · Hexagon board sheet`
  and `<Theme> · Triangle board sheet` artboards and what each supplies.
- Its mapping reference maps each sheet's values to the board module's
  per-grid shape entries and the 2D numbers 9-12.
- Its verification reference renders hexagon and triangle tiles beside their
  sheets, and its stops say what happens when a sheet is missing.
- The skill body and references follow CLAUDE.md § Editing Discipline.
