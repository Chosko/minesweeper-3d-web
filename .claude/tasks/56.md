# Task 56 — 3D board choice: presets, custom boards and the no-guess switch

Target: claude

## Goal
Build the 3D board choice of feature `3d-play-flow`: the six 3D presets,
Custom with validation, the Random fill and the no-guess switch with its
availability rule, replacing the 3D menu's current size choice and
remembering the last 3D choice through the game shell.

## Acceptance criteria
- The 3D board choice offers double layer Beginner (8 × 8 × 2, 10),
  Intermediate (14 × 14 × 2, 60), Expert (25 × 16 × 2, 130), and cube
  Beginner (6 × 6 × 6, 10), Intermediate (8 × 8 × 8, 40), Expert
  (12 × 12 × 8, 130), plus Custom and Random fill; the flat 3D presets are
  gone.
- Custom validates each dimension 1 to 100 and mines 1 to cells minus one,
  saying which field is out of range.
- The no-guess switch is available on every preset and on a custom board at
  or below the no-guess cell-count limit, one exported constant; above it the
  switch is disabled and "No-guess is available up to N cells" shows above
  it.
- The choice — X, Y, Z, mines, no-guess — is remembered per mode through the
  shell's last-board-choice mechanism and restored on return.
- The old per-size 3D best times are not read or shown by the board choice.
- Keyboard, mouse and controller operate every control.
- Node tests cover validation, preset values and no-guess availability.

## Decisions
- The no-guess limit constant starts at a conservative placeholder; task 58
  sets it from measured generation time. The constant lives in one place so
  only its value changes.
