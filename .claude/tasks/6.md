# Task 6 — Board metrics, click counts and the game summary

Target: claude

## Goal
Add the per-game counts of feature `cell-graph-rules-engine`: the board's 3BV
computed once the mines are placed, the 3BV solved so far, the player's
clicks split into effective and wasted per action kind, and the game summary
handed to `game-summary` at a win or a loss.

## Acceptance criteria
- 3BV: each opening counts once and each safe cell outside any opening's
  reach counts once; computed when the mines are placed. Tests pin it on
  hand-built boards, including a board with no openings and an all-opening
  board.
- 3BV solved tracks the player's progress and is correct after a loss.
- Clicks are counted per kind (reveal, flag, chord) and split into effective
  and wasted by the reference profile's rule for what counts as a click; an
  action outside *playing* counts nothing.
- At a win or a loss the engine exposes a game summary: outcome, board
  dimensions and mine count, 3BV, 3BV solved, click counts. The counts are
  also readable mid-game, for an abandoned game.
- Node tests cover every item above.

## Decisions
- What counts as a click (and whether wasted clicks count) is implemented
  per the most common Minesweeper Online reading and marked in the profile;
  task 7 pins it against a recorded observation, under the version-1 rule
  stated in task 5.
