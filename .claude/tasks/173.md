# Task 173 — Update documentation for feature `shared-screen-layout`

Target: claude

## Goal
Feature `shared-screen-layout` recomposed every non-game screen from the
canvas's *Joyful tiles* artboards: the new kit components and their selected
state, the main menu's nine entries and previews with Play again and the
session strip gone, the board choice screens with their bests, *Insert Board
Code* and Classic 2D's *Random*, the Daily screen listing every mode, the
one-column results screen, Records' Leaderboards tab, the 3D presets without
First box 1 and 2 and Learn 3D's win offering Cube Intro, the Settings, pause,
replay, ready and Learn 3D cards, the placeholder (*Coming soon*) page, the
confirmations, toasts, fatal card, no-JavaScript notice and 404 page, and the
3D renderer's scene panel. The documentation describing those screens still
describes the m1 low-fidelity compositions and needs to reflect the shipped
layout.

## Acceptance criteria
- `.claude/context/app-shell-kit.md` describes the new kit components and
  their catalogue entries, the selected state, the screen frame and its
  bottom bar, and the screens composed in it.
- `.claude/context/app-shell-menu.md` describes the menu's entries, routes
  (Hexagon, Triangle and Surface to the placeholder), opening selection and
  previews, the removal of Play again and the session strip, and the Daily
  screen's composition and won time.
- `.claude/context/app-shell-markup.md`, `.claude/context/app-shell-pause.md`,
  `.claude/context/app-shell-results.md` and `.claude/context/app-shell-3d.md`
  describe the markup, cards and results screen as shipped.
- `.claude/context/rendering.md` describes the renderer's target rectangle
  and the scene panel.
- `.claude/context/classic2d.md` and `.claude/context/mode3d.md` describe the
  board choice screens, their bests, *Insert Board Code* and Random;
  `mode3d.md`, `app-shell-3d.md` and `records-screen.md` describe the four
  cube presets, the First box sizes as custom boards and the Learn 3D end
  card's *Play Cube Intro*;
  `records-screen.md`, `settings-page.md` and `replay-playback.md` describe
  their screens in the frame, Records' Leaderboards tab included.
- `.claude/context/testing.md` describes the changed and added tests;
  `.claude/context/INDEX.md` rows say what each file covers now, with `Last
  updated` advanced.
- `README.md` changes only where it describes the screens' arrangement or
  the menu's entries.
- `.claude/domain/features/shared-screen-layout.md` records the details
  tasks 163–172 and 206–212 settled that hold at ship time (the catalogued
  component, part and slot names, how the menu's entries share the
  preview's height, the board choice summary line, the scene panel's
  tracking). Nothing else in it changes.

## Decisions
- **The m3-on-steam features drawn in this layout** (leaderboard-screen,
  leaderboards-menu-entry, results-steam-rank, input-rebinding) define their
  own screens in it, per `shared-screen-layout.md` § Non-goals; nothing of
  theirs is recorded here.
- **One documentation task for the feature** — this task also covers tasks
  209–212, added when the design changes were integrated, rather than a
  second documentation task beside it; task 212's documentation was task
  208's (skipped) before the First box removal moved to this feature.
