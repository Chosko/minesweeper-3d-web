# Task 142 — Touch Classic 2D and touch-safe 3D entry

Target: claude

## Goal
Make Classic 2D fully playable by touch and stop a touch player from being
trapped in 3D. This is row W-22 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and
work plan (Area: Input, Size: Medium, Phase 4). Decision 11 of § Decisions
taken ("Touch a target? Yes") lets it proceed. It closes four rows of that
document. P-37 (Blocker for touch): touch Classic 2D cannot flag. A tap
reveals, a 700 ms long-press and a two-finger tap do nothing, and there is no
flag button, so every game that needs a flag or a chord is lost. The menu's
warning speaks only of 3D needing a mouse. P-38 (Blocker on phones): a large
2D board cannot be panned by touch. Expert on an iPhone shows the top-left
9 × 14 cells and a swipe does nothing. P-39 (Major): a touch player can tap
"Click to play" into 3D (Chromium grants pointer lock), tap-reveal cubes but
not look, and cannot reach Pause because the scene intercepts the touch. The
only exit is a reload, recorded as a loss. P-45 (Minor): small text and small
targets. HUD labels are 11 px, the pause card's key caps 10 px, and on touch
the switch control is 44 × 24 and sliders 28 px tall. The work serves phone
and tablet players, and low-vision players for the label sizes. The approved
scope's condition holds throughout: "P-45 only where it degrades nothing on
normal pointers". Mouse, keyboard and controller play and every fine-pointer
layout stay exactly as they are.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Long-press flags** (P-37): on the 2D board a touch held 400 ms without
  moving 8 px toggles the flag on the cell under it. It acts at 400 ms, while
  still held. A short vibration (`navigator.vibrate`, where it exists) marks
  it, and the lift that follows does nothing. A long-press on a revealed cell
  does nothing.
- **Tap reveals, tap on a number chords** (P-37): a touch lifted before 400 ms
  that moved less than 8 px acts on its cell on the lift, the same
  release-to-act moment the mouse uses. On a closed cell it reveals, or
  toggles the flag while the Reveal / Flag switch is on Flag. On a revealed
  number it chords, whichever way the switch is set. While the finger is
  down the cell, or a number's closed neighbours, show the pressed feedback
  the mouse shows (`pressFeedback`). A touch that moves 8 px or more acts on
  nothing.
- **Reveal / Flag switch** (P-37): on a coarse pointer (`(pointer: coarse)`)
  a Reveal / Flag switch with a 44 px hit area sits in the 2D overlay bar,
  or on the framed panel's edge if task 134 has landed (see Decisions). It
  sets what a tap on a closed cell does. It starts on Reveal and keeps its
  setting for the page's life. It is not shown on a fine pointer, and not in
  3D.
- **Panning** (P-38): when the board scrolls, a one-finger drag of 8 px or
  more pans it and acts on nothing. A move under 8 px stays a tap. A
  two-finger drag pans whenever the board scrolls. A second finger cancels
  the first finger's pending tap or long-press.
- **Pinch zoom** (P-38): a two-finger pinch sets the tile size anywhere
  between `MIN_TILE_SIZE` and the fit-to-view tile (the tile at which the
  whole board fits the area). The pinch's midpoint stays over the same board
  point, and the scroll is re-clamped. The zoom lasts for the game. A new
  game, restart or Play again starts at the fit rule's tile. A pan or zoom
  changes no game state and records nothing.
- **Mouse unchanged** (P-37, "What not to break"): mouse input keeps its
  release-to-act state machine with drag-to-cancel, Shift+drag panning and
  wheel scrolling, all byte-for-byte as before.
  `tests/classic2d-pointer.test.mjs` and `tests/classic2d-fidelity.test.mjs`
  pass unchanged. A tap no longer reaches the mouse state machine through
  the browser's emulated mouse events, so a tap acts exactly once.
- **Menu note** (P-37): on a coarse-only device the menu's note says 3D needs
  a mouse and keyboard and Classic 2D plays by touch, instead of telling the
  player to open the game on a desktop browser (`NO_MOUSE_MSG`,
  `#touch-note`).
- **3D ready card is a notice** (P-39): on a coarse-only device (the shell's
  `COARSE_ONLY`) the 3D ready card is not a button. It is a notice saying
  3D needs a mouse and keyboard (or a controller), with one Back action that
  behaves as `readyBack`. No path requests pointer lock there: the card,
  resume from the pause card, restart, the failed board's Retry and Standard
  offers, and entering fullscreen. A tap anywhere on the notice or the scene
  enters no play.
- **Pause always tappable** (P-39): during lockless 3D play the overlay bar
  sits above the scene and takes taps. A tap on `#hud-pause` pauses, and the
  scene canvas does not swallow it.
- **Touch targets and labels** (P-45): under coarse-pointer media queries
  only, every `ui-toggle` switch and the Reveal / Flag switch get a 44 px hit
  area, every `ui-slider` thumb a 44 px hit area, labels at least 12 px
  (the HUD labels that use `--font-size-xs`) and key caps at least 11 px.
  On a fine pointer every computed size is unchanged.
- **Tests first**:
  - A new `tests/classic2d-touch.test.mjs` pins the DOM-free touch state
    machine: the 400 ms long-press flag and its single vibration, the tap
    reveal on the lift, the flag-mode tap, the chord on a number in both
    switch settings, the 8 px threshold, one-finger pan only on a scrolling
    board, two-finger pan, the second finger cancelling, and pressed
    feedback.
  - `tests/classic2d-view.test.mjs` pins the zoom range, the anchor point
    and the re-clamp.
  - `tests/classic2d-mode.test.mjs` pins the wiring: the switch, the zoom
    reset on a new game, and input taken only while `ready` or `playing`
    and not paused.
  - `tests/components.test.mjs` pins that the coarse-pointer rules leave
    fine-pointer sizes unchanged.
  - A Playwright run with touch emulation (`hasTouch`, `isMobile`, a
    phone-sized viewport) plays Expert: it flags by long-press and by the
    switch, chords by tap, pans and pinches. It also checks the 3D notice
    with Back, no pointer lock requested, and Pause tapped during lockless
    play. A second run with a mouse at 1280 × 800 checks the 2D overlay
    bar, the menu, Settings and the pause card unchanged.
- The owned documents state the agreed design change in every passage that
  stated the old one. The change: touch is supported in Classic 2D (tap,
  long-press flag, Reveal / Flag switch, chord on a number, touch pan, pinch
  zoom); on a coarse-only device 3D shows a notice and never requests
  pointer lock; the overlay bar stays tappable over lockless 3D play; and
  coarse pointers get 44 px switch and slider targets and 12 px labels.
  They also settle the points listed under Decisions. The documents are
  `.claude/domain/product-design.md`, `.claude/domain/business-model.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/classic-2d-square-play.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/game-shell.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/3d-play-flow.md` and
  `.claude/domain/features/screen-components.md`. Nothing else in those
  documents changes.
- `README.md`'s "Touch devices are not supported" limitation states the
  same change.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12).** This is settled by
  prior instruction: decision 11 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` and P-37, P-38, P-39
  and P-45, approved under § Approved scope and work plan (row W-22, P-45
  with its condition).
  - `product-design.md`, the "Mouse and keyboard first" point ("touch and
    mobile are not"): touch is supported in Classic 2D, and 3D on a
    coarse-only device shows a notice until 3D touch controls (W-23).
  - `business-model.md` ("Deliberately not served: mobile and touch
    players"): the build supports touch input. No paying segment is added,
    and the segment statement is otherwise unchanged.
  - `classic-2d-square-play.md`, the opening paragraph, § Scope
    ("Mouse input…", "Keyboard and controller play") and § Architecture
    ("Board view", "Pointer input"): touch input is added with tap,
    long-press flag, the Reveal / Flag switch and the chord on a number.
    Touch pan and pinch zoom between the minimum tile and fit-to-view are
    added beside scrolling.
  - `game-shell.md` § Architecture, "Pause controller" (3D resume
    re-acquires pointer lock): on a coarse-only device it never requests
    pointer lock.
  - `3d-play-flow.md` § Architecture, "Mode adapter" ("keeping today's
    pointer-lock and lockless behaviour"): on a coarse-only device the
    ready card is a notice with one Back action.
  - `screen-components.md` § Architecture, "Overlay bar": it carries the
    Reveal / Flag switch in 2D on coarse pointers and stays above the scene
    during lockless play. § Interfaces, "Accessibility": 44 px switch and
    slider targets and 12 px labels on coarse pointers.

  Settled here without conflict: the long-press does nothing on a revealed
  cell, and a touch never acts after moving 8 px. These documents are
  normally `/product-design`'s and `/architect`'s. This task updates them for
  exactly this and for nothing else. A further design decision met at
  implementation time is not covered: leave that passage untouched and name
  it under *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
- **Touch is its own input; the mouse machine is untouched.** Touch arrives
  as pointer events with `pointerType` `'touch'` and goes to a new DOM-free
  state machine in `js/classic2d/touch-input.js`, wired like
  `mountPointerInput`. The emulated mouse events a tap produces are
  suppressed, so `createPointerInput` and its profile markers do not change.
  This is how "the release-on-press semantics of the mouse must not change"
  is kept.
- **Which pointers count as coarse.** The gestures work on any touch pointer
  event, on any device. A mouse never produces one. The switch and the P-45
  sizes show under `(pointer: coarse)`, the primary pointer, so a desktop or
  touchscreen laptop driven by a mouse keeps its layout. The 3D notice uses
  the shell's existing `COARSE_ONLY` (coarse and not fine), so a hybrid
  device with a mouse can still play 3D.
- **The switch and the long-press.** The review says "a 400 ms long-press
  toggles a flag". It does so whichever way the switch is set. The switch
  changes only what a tap on a closed cell does. A tap on a number always
  chords, as P-37 states. The switch is not saved as a setting: it keeps its
  value for the page's life and needs no settings key.
- **A move of 8 px or more on a board that does not scroll** cancels the
  touch. This follows the mouse's drag-to-cancel spirit for a finger, which
  has no hover to follow.
- **The pinch range** is the closed interval between `MIN_TILE_SIZE` and
  the fit-to-view tile, whichever is larger. On a board too large for the
  screen at the minimum tile (P-38's case), that zooms out to a whole-board
  overview and back in to the minimum tile. The fit rule's default tile and
  `MIN_TILE_SIZE` themselves do not change. Task 134 owns those, and this
  task reads whatever they are when it lands.
- **Task 134** (W-08) moves the 2D overlay bar onto the framed panel's edge.
  Neither task waits on the other. If 134 has landed, the Reveal / Flag
  switch goes on that edge beside Pause. Otherwise it goes in today's
  overlay bar.
- **A controller on a coarse-only device** keeps today's lockless 3D entry
  (Start on the ready card through `padResume`). It requests no pointer
  lock, so P-39's rule holds, and controller play is not the trap P-39
  describes. The notice's one touch action is Back.
- **Replays.** The session records touch reveals, flags and chords as
  actions, exactly like mouse ones. The 2D movement sampler records the
  mouse pointer as today. A touch has no hover position, so no pointer is
  sampled for it, and pan and zoom are view-only. The replay format and
  viewer do not change.
- **P-45 scope.** P-45's sizes are applied only inside coarse-pointer media
  queries. That is the approved condition "only where it degrades nothing
  on normal pointers", and it keeps every mouse layout unchanged. The
  footer link P-45 mentions is not in its approved solution and is left as
  it is.
- **Out of scope**: touch controls for 3D play itself (the virtual stick,
  look drag, touch reveal, flag and chord in 3D, pinch for spacing and the
  view-modes toolbar) are W-23 (I-14), a later task. Touch pan and zoom in
  the replay viewer are not part of P-38. The accessibility settings
  (P-56 to P-60, I-16) are dropped by decision 17.
