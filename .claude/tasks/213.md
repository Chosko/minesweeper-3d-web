# Task 213 — 3D selection glow line, selected-face tint and confetti colours drawn from the board module

Target: claude

## Goal
Make the 3D renderer draw the selected cube and the win confetti from the
active theme's board module, for feature `themed-board-art-and-motion`: the
glow line inside the selection outline, the selected face's tint and the
confetti colours are board-module values (task 181 writes *Joyful tiles*'
from J-3DSheet), and today the renderer holds its own coral outline, warm
emissive and confetti palette. Nothing reads or tests the J-3DSheet values
until this task does.

## Acceptance criteria
- The renderer takes the selection's glow-line colour and the selected
  face's tint, and the confetti colours, from the board module it receives
  (task 183's hand-off), and holds no colour of its own for them; on a theme
  change the selection uniforms and the confetti colours are rebuilt with
  the atlas, with no state change.
- **Selection** under *Joyful tiles*, as J-3DSheet draws it: the outline in
  the module's outline colour at max(0.06 of the face, 3 px) on screen, a
  1 px line of the module's glow colour inside it, and the selected cube's
  face colour multiplied by the module's tint toward coral; the glow pulse
  keeps today's timing.
- **Confetti** under *Joyful tiles* is drawn in the module's colours —
  coral, yellow, teal, blue, violet, white and orange — cycled over the
  pieces as today; count, motion and timing are unchanged.
- A DOM-free test (`tests/render-3d-selection.test.mjs`) asserts that the
  values the renderer derives for its selection uniforms and confetti
  colours equal the board module's, for *Joyful tiles* and for a fixture
  module with other values, and that no hard-coded selection or confetti
  colour remains in `js/render.js`.
- A browser test in `tests/render-3d-feedback.test.mjs` selects a cube under
  *Joyful tiles* and samples the outline, the glow line and the tinted face
  against J-3DSheet's values, and checks a frame of the win confetti for
  the module's colours.
- 3D readability and feedback tests pass.

## Decisions
- **Owner decision** (2026-10-19 revision): the J-3DSheet selection and
  confetti details get their own task and runbook step, so the renderer
  reads and tests them; task 183 keeps the outline colour, the start-cell
  tint and the atlas.
- **Classic 2D confetti is not in scope**: its colours stay the 2D board
  view's.
