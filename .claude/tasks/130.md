# Task 130 — One-key restart and completed-games stats

Target: claude

## Goal
Let a player restart with one input, and give the players who restart most
a win rate and a streak that still mean something. This is row W-29 of
`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan (Area: Session, Records,
Size: Small, Phase 1). It closes I-06 and P-55 of that document, together,
in the form its decision 8 takes ("Restart still a loss once a one-key
restart exists? Yes, with completed-games stats beside the reference
ones — I-06, P-55 proceed"). Today a restart is Esc, Restart, confirm:
three inputs and a modal, where the reference (Minesweeper Online) restarts
with one key (I-06). And because a restart counts as a loss, a player who
restarts bad openings has a 20 % win rate and no streak, so the two figures
stop meaning anything to the people who look at them most, and some avoid
starting rather than spoil a streak (P-55, Minor). The work serves speed
players and grinders who restart bad openings dozens of times an hour. The
abandon-as-loss rule itself does not change.

## Acceptance criteria
- **One-key restart in play** (I-06): during play, in Classic 2D and in 3D,
  `F2` starts a new board of the same board choice at once, with no modal —
  the same restart the pause card's Restart performs (in 3D with no-guess
  off once the player took a failed board's standard offer, and entering
  play the way restart does today). `R` and `Home` keep the 3D camera's
  "back to start" (task 125), so in play the one key is `F2` only. On a
  controller, `Y` pressed while `Start` is held restarts; `Start` alone
  still pauses and `Y` alone keeps its play meaning (Chord in Classic 2D,
  the 3D hold). `F2` and Start + Y also work while the pause card shows (a
  restart there, Play again once the game has ended), do nothing during the
  3D end delay, while a confirmation or the controls modal shows, or on a
  shell screen with no game, and `F2`'s browser default is prevented.
- **Restart from the results screen** (I-06): on the results screen `R`
  does what Play again does (a new board of the same choice; the game has
  ended, so nothing counts as a loss); `Enter` on the focused Play again is
  unchanged. `R` does nothing on any other shell screen.
- **No modal for restart, one toast** (I-06, decision 8): every restart of
  a started, unfinished game — `F2`, Start + Y, the pause card's Restart,
  the overlay bar's restart — proceeds at once and still reports the game
  abandoned, so it counts as a loss and ends the streak exactly as now. The
  first such restart in a session of the game shows one toast saying the
  game counted as a loss and that the "Always ask" setting brings the
  confirmation back; later restarts in the same session show none. A
  restart before the first click, during generation before the game
  started, or after the end shows no toast.
- **"Always ask" setting** (I-06): a new boolean setting (e.g.
  `confirmRestart`, default off), in the schema, the Settings page (a
  toggle labelled "Always ask before restarting", or close) and the store's
  validation, restores today's confirmation ("Restart this game? It will
  count as a loss.") before every restart of a started, unfinished game,
  from any of the inputs above; `F2` or Start + Y then open the pause card
  with that confirmation showing, and no toast shows. Legacy carry-over is
  untouched (the setting has no legacy key).
- **Back to menu keeps its confirmation** (I-06): Back to menu from a
  started, unfinished game asks exactly as now, whatever the setting; the
  leave-page guard and the in-progress marker are unchanged.
- **"Completed" defined** (P-55): a completed game is one played to a win
  or a loss. A game abandoned by restart, by Back to menu or a replacing
  start, or by leaving the page (the in-progress marker settled at the next
  launch) is not completed. The abandon-as-loss rule is unchanged: such a
  game still counts in games played, the win rate and the streaks as now.
- **Completed counters** (P-55, `js/records/model.js`): every board's
  counters and every mode's overall counters gain the completed games count
  and a current and longest completed streak. A win extends the completed
  streak, a loss ends it, an abandoned game leaves it as it was. The model
  exposes the completed win rate (wins ÷ completed games, null for none) per
  board and per mode overall, beside `winRate` / `overallWinRate`, and
  `counters()` / `overall()` carry the new fields. `rebuildRecords(history)`
  still equals the records maintained game by game, the new fields
  included; no figure combines Classic 2D and 3D.
- **Existing records gain the figures** (`js/records/store.js`): a records
  document saved before this task loads with its completed counters derived
  from the history (which keeps every game's outcome) and is saved back at
  a new records version, which an older build then refuses as newer, as it
  does today. A missing or corrupt records document beside an intact
  history is rebuilt with the new fields.
- **Records screen** (P-55): for the chosen board and for the chosen mode's
  overall figures, in both the 2D and 3D views, the screen shows a
  completed-games win rate and the best completed streak beside the
  reference win rate and longest streak, with labels that say what they
  leave out (e.g. "Win rate, completed games" / "Best streak, completed
  games"), and the same values in the figures' text; the empty state is
  unchanged.
- **Results screen** (P-55): after a won or lost game the results screen
  shows, for the game's board and counting this game, its win rate and
  completed win rate and its longest streak and best completed streak, the
  reference figure first and the completed one beside it, in Classic 2D and
  in 3D. A recording that failed shows no figures line, as it shows no
  comparison.
- **Help lists the keys** (`js/settings/bindings.js`): both modes'
  keyboard rows carry `[F2]` Restart and their controller rows `{start} +
  {y}` Restart, so the pause card's controls, the Controls panel and the
  Settings bindings section show them; the shell's results screen hint or
  help names `R` where such a hint exists.
- **Tests first**: `tests/shell-pause.test.mjs` pins restart proceeding at
  once and reporting abandoned, the confirmation under the setting, Back to
  menu still confirming, and the toast once per session;
  `tests/settings-store.test.mjs` and `tests/settings-page.test.mjs` the new
  setting, its default, validation, its toggle and the new bindings rows;
  `tests/records-model.test.mjs` and `tests/records-model-3d.test.mjs` the
  completed counters, the completed win rate, abandoned games leaving the
  completed streak alone and the rebuild equality;
  `tests/records-store.test.mjs` a pre-task records document gaining the
  figures from its history and the version step; `tests/records-screen.test.mjs`
  and `tests/records-screen-3d.test.mjs` the new figures and their text;
  `tests/results.test.mjs` the results figures and `R`; a test (unit over
  the key handling, or Playwright through `__ms`) that `F2` restarts in both
  modes and that `R` and `Home` in 3D play still return the camera.
- **What not to break** (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Decisions taken):
  release-to-act with drag-to-cancel in both modes; the reference input
  conventions; 3BV, click and efficiency accounting; the three-way best
  comparison; the confirmations that say "It will count as a loss" (Back to
  menu always, restart under "Always ask"); the last-choice memory; the
  honest empty states and storage notices.
- The owned documents state the agreed design change — a restart of a
  started, unfinished game proceeds at once with a one-time toast unless
  the "Always ask" setting restores its confirmation, `F2` / Start + Y in
  play and `R` on results restart, and every board and mode keeps
  completed-games counters (completed win rate, best completed streak)
  shown on Records and results beside the reference figures — in every
  passage that stated the old one, and settle the points listed under
  Decisions: `.claude/domain/product-design.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/game-shell.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/personal-records.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/3d-game-records.md`,

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction — `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` decision 8 ("Yes, with completed-games
  stats beside the reference ones"), I-06's proposed solution ("F2 (R on
  results, Start+Y on a controller) starts a new board of the same choice
  at once; it still counts as a loss, said once in a toast with an 'Always
  ask' setting; keep the confirmation only for Back to menu") and P-55's
  ("Keep the rule and add a completed-games win rate and a best streak of
  finished games beside the reference figures"), approved without
  condition under § Approved scope and work plan (row W-29).
  - `game-shell.md` § Interfaces and contracts, "Restart" ("during a
    started, unfinished game it asks for confirmation, saying the game will
    count as a loss"): restart proceeds at once with a one-time toast;
    the confirmation returns under the "Always ask" setting. § Scope
    ("Pause and resume, restart, and back to menu, from the pause card and
    from the keyboard and controller"): gains `F2`, Start + Y and `R` on
    results.
  - `product-design.md` § the records paragraph ("Win rate and streaks are
    kept per board and per mode … counts as a loss and breaks the win
    streak"): the rule stays; a completed-games win rate and best streak
    are kept beside them.
  - `personal-records.md` § Scope ("Win rate and win streaks (current and
    longest) per board and across Classic 2D") and § Data and state,
    "Counters" ("games, wins, current streak and longest streak"): gain the
    completed games and completed streaks.
  - `3d-game-records.md` § Scope and § Architecture, "Per-mode counters":
    the same for 3D boards and the 3D mode.
  - `records-screen.md` § Scope (the chosen board's and the overall
    figures) and `3d-results-records-screens.md` (the figures panel): gain
    the two completed figures.
  - `results-screen.md` § Scope (outcome, board, time, 3BV, 3BV/s,
    efficiency, comparison) and its "Play again" contract: gain the board's
    reference and completed figures, and `R` as Play again.
  - `settings.md` § Data and state, "Saved settings": gains the "Always
    ask" setting.

  Settled here, without conflict: "completed" means played to a win or a
  loss, not abandoned by restart, menu or leaving (the launch brief's
  definition); an abandoned game neither extends nor breaks the completed
  streak; existing records derive the new counters from history
  (`personal-records.md` § Data and state, "History is the source of
  truth"). These documents are normally `/architect`'s, and
  `product-design.md` `/product-design`'s; this task updates them for
  exactly this and for nothing else. A further design decision met at
  implementation time is not covered: leave that passage untouched and
  name it under *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
- **"Said once" is once per session**: the toast shows on the first
  restart that abandons a game after each load of the game, not once ever,
  so it needs no stored flag and a returning player is reminded once per
  sitting; a grinder restarting dozens of times sees it once.
- **Every restart path follows one rule**: I-06 keeps the confirmation
  "only for Back to menu", so the pause card's Restart and the overlay
  bar's restart lose it too, not only the new key; "Always ask" restores
  it for all of them at once. `CONFIRMATIONS.restart` stays, used under
  the setting.
- **Start + Y on the controller**: `Y` counts only while `Start` is held,
  which leaves `Start` as pause and `Y`'s play meanings untouched. No
  controller restart is added on the results screen, where Play again
  already has default focus. Rebinding these inputs is P-44's
  (`input-rebinding`, tasks 111–114) and out of scope.
- **Results shows both pairs**: the results screen has no win rate today,
  so "beside the reference figures" there means the board's reference win
  rate and longest streak shown with their completed counterparts, one
  line, rather than the completed figures alone.
- **Out of scope**: changing the abandon-as-loss rule; key rebinding
  (P-44); crash durability (P-49, P-50, P-18 dropped); the Records layout
  and pagination (W-30) and richer Records (W-31).
- **Boundaries**: task 128 (row W-27) reworks the results view and flow
  and the Records screen, task 129 (row W-28) the records model, store and
  history entry, and tasks 125, 126 and 127 `js/main.js` and
  `js/settings/bindings.js`. None depends on this task nor this on them;
  whichever lands second merges around the first, and when task 128's
  next-target streak line is present the completed figures sit beside it.
