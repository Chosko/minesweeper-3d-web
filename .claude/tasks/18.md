# Task 18 — In-game overlay bar component

Target: claude

## Goal
Add the in-game overlay bar of feature `screen-components`: timer, mine
counter and pause as one component that sits over either board and stays
readable over the 3D scene's light sky in both themes by carrying its own
themed surface.

## Acceptance criteria
- The overlay bar is a component in `css/components.css` with a documented
  markup pattern: timer and mine counter as stat readouts using tabular
  figures, and a pause button with an accessible name. It is styled plainly,
  low-fidelity, from tokens.
- The timer shows the elapsed time in whole seconds, zero-padded to three
  digits (`047`), over either board, and stops at `999` while the game's
  elapsed time keeps counting; the results, records and leaderboard screens
  still receive the real time. The mine counter shows mines left clamped to
  −99 … 999. Both formats are Node-tested pure functions, including the
  clamps at 999 s and beyond, and mines left below −99 and above 999.
- It carries its own themed surface and meets the contrast contract against
  that surface in both themes, over the 3D scene and over a plain board
  background.
- The existing 3D HUD (`updateHud`, `setBoard` in `js/ui.js`) renders through
  the overlay bar; the 3D game's HUD behaviour is unchanged but for the
  timer, which takes the overlay's three-digit whole-second format in place
  of its two decimals.
- The pause button is reachable by keyboard and controller.
- Playwright screenshots of the overlay over the 3D scene in both themes are
  checked for contrast.

## Decisions
- Low-fidelity pass: the overlay is functional, readable and accessible,
  styled plainly; the high-fidelity visual dressing comes from the approved
  designs in a later pass, and those designs are not an input to this task.
- The timer and mine-counter formats, their clamps included, are the one
  overlay format, over the Classic 2D and the 3D board alike, per
  `screen-components.md` § Architecture (Overlay bar). The clamps copy the
  reference game's displays, recorded in
  `tests/fidelity/minesweeper-online.md` § Overlay time and mine displays.
  The clamp is display-only: the timer value the modes keep is never
  clamped.
