# Task 18 — In-game overlay bar component

Target: claude

## Goal
Add the in-game overlay bar of feature `screen-components`: timer, mine
counter and pause as one component that sits over either board and stays
readable over the 3D scene's light sky in both themes by carrying its own
themed surface.

## Acceptance criteria
- The overlay bar is a component in `css/components.css` with a documented
  markup pattern: timer and mine counter as stat readouts using tabular
  figures, and a pause button with an accessible name.
- It carries its own themed surface and meets the contrast contract against
  that surface in both themes, over the 3D scene and over a plain board
  background.
- The existing 3D HUD (`updateHud`, `setBoard` in `js/ui.js`) renders through
  the overlay bar; the 3D game's HUD behaviour is unchanged.
- The pause button is reachable by keyboard and controller.
- Playwright screenshots of the overlay over the 3D scene in both themes are
  checked for contrast.
