# Task 150 — Replay viewer as a study tool

Target: claude

## Goal
Turn the replay viewer from a player into a tool a player studies a game
with. This is row W-32 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan
(Area: Replay, Size: Medium, Phase 5). It closes three rows of that
document. P-52 (Minor): the viewer shows position and clock in whole seconds
("003 / 013"), has no frame step and no click markers on the seek bar, draws
the pointer as a 14 px black dot that is lost on a busy board, leaves the
previous replay's figures in the bar when a replay fails to load, and in 3D
keeps the game's HUD chips and the "H controls" toast on screen. P-66
(Minor): a replay opens with the first click already applied, so the board
is open at time zero and the click that made it is never seen, and clicks in
general are not visible during playback, only their effects. I-10 (Medium):
a player improves by seeing wasted clicks and comparing against a best, so
the viewer gains click markers, frame step, a Show 3BV overlay, 0.25×, sound
on replays, a free camera with Follow in 3D, *Watch best* beside *Watch
replay*, and download and import of replay files. All three are approved
without condition by § Approved scope and work plan. The work serves
enthusiasts studying a replay and players improving their efficiency, in
Classic 2D and 3D, by mouse, keyboard and controller.

Two neighbouring tasks change the same screen and are not preconditions:
task 129 adds pause spans to the seek bar, and task 134 hides the 2D cursor
ring unless the recording used the cursor (P-52's "keyboard ring parked at
cell 0", which this task does not redo). Tasks 125 (3D spawn framing) and
146 (orbit camera mode) change the 3D camera this task's Free camera flies.
Build on each where it has landed and name it in the commit; where it has
not, build on the current code and leave its part to it.

## Acceptance criteria

### Viewer precision and markers (P-52, I-10)
- **Clock and position in ss.mmm** (P-52): the viewer's clock (`#rp-clock`)
  and position (`#rp-position`) read seconds with three decimals —
  `13.342`, `13.342 / 47.120`; seconds are not split into minutes, as the
  results screen's time is not. `clockText` changes to this format; the
  in-game overlay's whole-second timer (`formatOverlayTime`) is unchanged
  for play. The seek bar's `aria-valuetext` keeps the results time format.
- **Frame step** (P-52, I-10): Comma steps one frame back and Period one
  frame forward, both pausing first. A frame is the replay's movement
  sampling interval (`sampleIntervalMs`: 50 ms in 2D, 100 ms in 3D under
  format version 1), clamped to the start and the end. ←/→ keep stepping to
  the previous and next action. Both are new `COMMANDS` and `REPLAY_KEYS`
  entries and appear in the viewer's key list (below) and README.
- **0.25×** (I-10): `SPEEDS` is `[0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4]`; the speed choice
  shows it and Minus reaches it from 0.5×. The default stays 1×.
- **Click ticks on the seek bar** (P-52, P-66): the seek bar carries one
  tick per recorded action at its time — left (reveal), right (flag) and
  chord each in its own colour. The three colours are design tokens with
  light and dark values (`css/tokens.css`, held by
  `tests/tokens.test.mjs`), distinct from each other and from the bar's
  fill and thumb in both themes. Ticks closer than a pixel merge without
  hiding a different kind beside them. They are decoration: the bar keeps
  its keyboard and pointer behaviour and its accessible name and value.
  Where task 129's pause spans exist, both are drawn and neither hides the
  other.

### First click and pointer (P-66, P-52)
- **Playback starts on the closed board** (P-66). How the format records
  the first click: the session records it as the first action, a reveal at
  game time 0 (a 3D flag before it also carries 0), and the viewer opens at
  time 0, where `seek(0)` applies every time-0 action. The change is in
  playback only — no format version, no recorder change, no fixture
  regenerated, the verifier unchanged — so every stored replay plays the
  new way:
  - the viewer opens on the start position: the closed board, no action
    applied (`seekIndex(0)`), clock `00.000`;
  - the timeline gains a lead-in of `LEAD_IN_MS` (500 ms of replay time)
    before game time 0, on the seek bar too; through it the board stays
    closed and the clock reads `00.000`; at its end the time-0 actions play
    as the first action, with their press rings and, with sound on, their
    cues;
  - Home, the seek bar's start and play from the end return to the start
    position; ← from the first action returns there; → from there goes to
    the first action applied;
  - after the lead-in every seek applies every action with time ≤ t, as
    now. `tests/replay-viewer.test.mjs` holds this on a fixture from
    `tests/fixtures/replays/` recorded by an earlier build, 2D and 3D.
- **A press ring for every click** (P-66): each action that playback
  reaches going forward shows a ring that expands and fades over about
  400 ms of wall time, coloured by button with the seek-bar tick colours.
  In 2D it is centred on the recorded pointer at the action's time, or on
  the acted cell's centre when no pointer sample precedes it (a keyboard or
  controller action, or the first click). In 3D it is centred on the acted
  cell's projected screen position, which in Follow is the crosshair.
  Seeking or stepping to an action shows that action's ring once; a seek
  that crosses many actions shows none for the ones skipped.
- **A pointer that reads on any board** (P-52): the 2D `.replay-pointer`
  has a contrasting outline (a light ring around a dark core, or the
  reverse in the dark theme, from tokens) that stays visible on closed,
  revealed, numbered and flagged tiles in both themes, and a short trail of
  its last 150 ms of replay time that fades out; the trail is cleared on a
  seek and while paused shows nothing beyond the pointer.

### Failure and 3D chrome (P-52)
- **The bar cleared on failure** (P-52): when a replay is loading or fails
  to load, the overlay figures, the position, the seek bar, the ticks and
  the end line show nothing from a replay opened earlier; figures read
  `DASH`. Holds after a replay played, then another failed.
- **The 3D viewer shows replay chrome, not the game's** (P-52): while a 3D
  replay shows, the game's HUD view chips (`#chip-shift`, `#chip-space`,
  `#chip-ctrl`, `#chip-spacing`) and the "H controls" toast are hidden and
  do not appear, and the viewer shows its own key line instead: the replay
  keys (Space/K play, ←/→ action, Comma/Period frame, Home/End,
  Minus/Equal speed, B Show 3BV, C Free camera) with their controller
  buttons where they have one. Leaving the viewer restores the game's chrome
  for the next game.

### Overlays and sound (I-10)
- **Show 3BV** (I-10): a *Show 3BV* toggle (button, and the B key) paints
  the board's 3BV partition over the current frame: each opening — its
  zeros and the numbers bordering it — in one tint from a small cycle of
  token colours, so neighbouring openings differ, and each isolated number
  (a number bordering no opening) in its own mark. A press ring on a cell
  whose region is already solved reads as a wasted click. The partition
  comes from the engine's 3BV definition (`js/engine/metrics.js`, exposed
  as a per-cell region label, tested in `tests/engine-metrics.test.mjs`
  against the 3BV the engine already reports), not recomputed in the view.
  It works in 2D (over the board view) and in 3D (as a cell tint in the
  renderer), off by default, and holds across seeks.
- **Sound on replays** (I-10): a *Sound* toggle in the viewer, off by
  default and kept for the session (not a saved setting). When on, each
  action that playback reaches going forward plays the cue the mode plays
  for that outcome — in 3D the cues of `actionSounds` / `endGame` in
  `js/main.js`, in 2D the same cues as task 135 plays (the same mapping
  where task 135 has landed, 3D's mapping otherwise) — and the end of the
  game plays `explode` or `win` once. Seeking, stepping and speeds above
  2× play no cue for the actions they skip; the master volume and mute
  apply as in play.

### 3D camera (I-10)
- **Free camera with Follow** (I-10): the 3D viewer has a *Follow* / *Free*
  toggle (button, and the C key; Follow by default). Follow plays the
  recorded camera and view mode, as now. Free detaches the camera at its
  current pose and lets the viewer fly it with the game's flight controls
  (`js/input.js` `FlyCamera`: the movement keys, mouse look under pointer
  lock taken by clicking the scene and released by Esc, the controller
  sticks), and task 146's orbit mode where it has landed, while playback
  runs on; the recorded view mode still applies. Back to Follow returns to
  the recorded camera at the current time. No flight key is a replay key;
  Space stays play / pause.

### Files and entry points (I-10)
- **Watch best** (I-10): the results screen, 2D and 3D, shows *Watch best*
  beside *Watch replay* when the board's fastest-time best has a replay
  kept in the library and that best is not the game just shown; it opens
  the viewer on that replay by id, returning to the results screen.
  `tests/results-watch.test.mjs` covers shown, hidden and the route.
- **Download** (I-10): the viewer (*Download*, while a replay plays) and
  each row of the Records library view save the replay's bytes as a file,
  `minesweeper-<board key>-<end date>.msrp` (characters a file name cannot
  hold replaced), byte for byte the stored blob — or the results hand-off's
  blob for a game the library could not save.
- **Import** (I-10): *Open replay file* in the viewer and in the Records
  library view picks a `.msrp` file and opens it in the viewer (Back
  returns to where it was opened from). It is verified like any other
  replay: a newer one, one that does not reproduce and one that cannot be
  read get the viewer's existing failure lines, as does a file over 16 MB,
  refused before it is read. An imported replay is watched, not kept: it is
  not added to the library, pinned or recorded.
- **Not built:** a ghost compare of two replays of the same board, playing
  one over the other, which I-10 names as later. `replay-playback.md` names
  it as a later follow-on, not a slice of this task.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction, P-52, P-66 and I-10 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md`, approved without
  condition under § Approved scope and work plan (row W-32).
  - `replay-playback.md` § Scope and non-goals, playback controls (0.5× to
    4×, step to the previous or next action): 0.25× joins the speeds, and
    frame step, Show 3BV, the sound toggle, Follow / Free, download and
    import join the controls.
  - `replay-playback.md` § Scope and non-goals, overlay, and § Architecture,
    "Playback controls and overlay" (the clock in the in-game overlay's
    timer format): clock and position read ss.mmm; the seek bar carries
    left, right and chord ticks.
  - `replay-playback.md` § Scope and non-goals, non-goals ("Editing,
    trimming or exporting replays — in no milestone's slice"): exporting a
    replay as a file and importing one are in scope; editing and trimming
    stay out. "Playing back sound effects — the viewer is silent": the
    viewer plays the game's cues behind a toggle; the matching Open
    question is closed. A ghost compare of two replays is named as a later
    follow-on.
  - `replay-playback.md` § Architecture, "3D viewer" ("Player camera input
    is ignored") and the Open question on detaching the camera: a Free
    camera flown with the game's controls, with Follow back to the
    recorded one.
  - `replay-playback.md` § Architecture, "2D viewer" (the pointer drawn as
    a cursor): the pointer has a contrasting outline and a short trail, and
    every click shows a press ring coloured by button.
  - `replay-playback.md` § Scope and non-goals (entry points) and
    § Interfaces and contracts, "Open" and "Watch replay": *Watch best*
    beside *Watch replay*; Open also takes an imported file.
  - `replay-library.md` § Scope and non-goals ("Importing, exporting or
    sharing replays — in no milestone's slice") and § Architecture,
    "Library view" (*Watch* and *Pin* / *Unpin*): each row has *Download*
    and the view has *Open replay file*; sharing stays out.
  - `results-screen.md` § Scope (the actions) and § Interfaces, and
    `3d-results-records-screens.md` § Scope and § Interfaces ("Watch
    replay"): *Watch best* beside *Watch replay*.

  Settled here, without conflict: playback opens on the closed board with a
  500 ms lead-in and the first click as the first action, with no format
  change (P-66); a frame is the replay's sampling interval; press rings and
  ticks share three token colours; the 3D ring sits on the acted cell's
  projection; the bar is cleared on failure; the 3D viewer hides the game's
  chips and toast and shows its key line; Show 3BV paints the engine's 3BV
  partition; sound is off by default and session-only; *Watch best* opens
  the fastest-time best; replay files are `.msrp`, capped at 16 MB on
  import, and an imported replay is not kept. These documents are normally
  `/architect`'s; this task updates them for exactly this and for nothing
  else. A further design decision met at implementation time is not
  covered: leave that passage untouched and name it under *Follow-ups* as a
  precise follow-up.
- **The first click without a format change** (P-66): the recording
  already holds the first click as the time-0 reveal; what hid it is that
  the viewer opened at time 0, where `seek(0)` applies it. A lead-in and a
  start position before the time-0 actions show it without touching the
  format, so old replays play the new way and the determinism fixtures
  stand. The 3D camera through the lead-in is the first recorded sample,
  as `cameraAt` already holds it before its time.
- **Frame = sampling interval**: below it nothing but actions is recorded,
  and ←/→ already reach those, so a finer step only repeats interpolation.
- **Which best** for *Watch best*: the results screen's own comparison
  leads with the fastest time and the library pins every best, so the
  fastest-time best is the one replay certain to be kept; it is hidden
  when the game shown is that best, where it would repeat *Watch replay*.
- **Imported replays are watched, not kept**: keeping them would put a
  replay with no summary in the library and its retention, a design I-10
  does not ask for.
- **Keys**: B and C are bound by neither the replay keys nor the 3D game
  (`js/settings/bindings.js`); R is task 125's and M is mute, so neither is
  used. The sound toggle has no key.
- **Scope kept as one task** (W-32 is one row): the criteria are grouped
  so the implementer can land and test them group by group within it.
