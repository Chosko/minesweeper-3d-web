# Task 129 — Pause keeps the clock running, pauses recorded

Target: claude

## Goal
Make the game clock keep running through every pause, in Classic 2D and in
3D, and record each game's pauses in its summary and its replay. This is row
W-28 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan (Area: Session,
Size: Small, Phase 1). It closes P-48 of that document, in the form its
decision 7 takes ("Pause policy: The clock keeps running while paused; pauses
are still recorded — P-48 proceeds in that form"). Today pause stops the
clock and leaves no trace: a Beginner game with a 3 s pause took 7.29 s of
wall time and shows 3.7 s, and nothing on results, in Records or in the
replay says it was paused, so a player can pause on every hard decision and
set clean bests; automatic pauses on blur make the mechanic unavoidable
(P-48, Major, integrity). The work serves enthusiasts chasing bests and any
future leaderboard. Pause behaviour itself does not change: the board stays
hidden and play input is ignored while paused.

## Acceptance criteria
- **The clock runs through pauses** (P-48, decision 7): in both modes a
  started, unfinished game's elapsed time is the wall time since its clock
  started — the board's arrival in Classic 2D, the first reveal applied to
  the generated board in 3D — paused spans included, until the win or loss.
  Every pause source counts the same: Esc, the overlay's Pause button,
  controller Start or Back, pointer-lock loss, focus loss (`blur`), tab hide
  (`visibilitychange` hidden) and controller disconnect. No screen change in
  3D stops the clock either: `sync3D` pausing the session away from
  `playing` (the pause card, the `ready` screen before pointer lock is
  retaken) leaves the elapsed time running. Win or loss still stops it, and
  the clock still never starts before the board arrives or while a failure
  offer shows.
- **Pause behaviour unchanged**: while paused the board is hidden and play
  input is dropped, exactly as now; the session's `paused` flag, `recording`
  (false while paused, so no movement samples), the pause rules of
  `js/shell/pause.js` (nothing before the first click or after the end
  pauses the mode; nothing pauses twice; nothing during the 3D end delay),
  the confirmations and the leave-page guard are unchanged.
- **Running time on screen**: the pause card shows the game's running time
  and updates it at least once a second while it shows, in both modes, for
  manual and automatic pauses alike; the overlay timer and the 3D HUD read
  the running clock too, so on resume they show the time including the
  pause. After the end the card keeps showing the frozen final time.
- **What a pause is**: each transition of a started, unfinished game's
  session from unpaused to paused while its clock runs is one pause; its
  span runs on the game clock from that moment to the session's resume (in
  3D, the return to `playing`), or to the game's end when it is abandoned
  while paused (restart or back to menu from the pause card). A pause begun
  before the clock starts (a 2D pause while generating) counts from the
  clock's start if still paused then, and not at all if it ended before.
  The sessions expose the spans so far (e.g. `pauses()` → `[{start, end}]`,
  the open one ending at the current time).
- **Summary record** (`js/records/summary.js`): `buildSummary` takes the
  game's pauses and the record gains `pauses` (the count) and `pausedMs`
  (total paused time, whole ms), both non-negative integers with `pausedMs`
  ≤ `elapsedMs`, a `RangeError` otherwise. Every summary carries them —
  finished, abandoned, and the in-progress marker's checkpoint at each
  pause — in both modes. `elapsedMs` includes the paused time, so time and
  3BV/s include it; best eligibility is unchanged (a paused game stays
  eligible).
- **History** (`js/records/model.js` `compactSummary`, `store.js`
  `doLoad`'s history check): the compact history entry carries `pauses` and
  `pausedMs`; an entry without them — every game recorded before this
  task — stays valid and reads as 0 pauses and 0 ms, so existing records,
  bests and history load unchanged, with no records format step. Malformed
  values on a new entry are rejected as other fields are.
- **Replay header and game clock** (`js/replay/format.js`,
  `js/replay/recorder.js`): every recorded time is on the running clock —
  the session's elapsed time, floored — so an action after a pause carries
  a time later by the paused span and the action stream has a gap there.
  The header gains `pauses`, the game's pause spans as ascending,
  non-overlapping `{start, end}` game-clock times within 0 .. the check's
  elapsed time; their count and total equal the summary's `pauses` and
  `pausedMs`. The recorder learns the spans from the session (at sealing,
  or as each pause ends) and seals them with the summary. This is a new
  replay format version: a decoder for it, `UPGRADES[1]` turning a version
  1 replay into it with `pauses: []`, its sampling intervals equal to
  version 1's; version 1's decoder is not edited. The replay check values
  and the final-state digest are unchanged.
- **Old replays stay playable**: every version 1 replay, the fixtures under
  `tests/fixtures/replays/` included, decodes, verifies and plays with no
  pauses; the fixtures are kept as version 1 files to prove it, and a new
  fixture or a round-trip test covers a replay with pauses.
- **Seek bar shows pauses** (P-48: "Show pauses on the replay seek bar"):
  the replay viewer (`js/replay/viewer.js`, `#rp-seek`) marks each paused
  span on the seek bar track at its position and width over the replay's
  duration, coloured from a design token with a dark value, and gives the
  bar a text alternative naming the pauses (e.g. "Paused 2 times, 4.1 s
  in all"); a replay with none shows no marks. Playback runs through a
  paused span at the chosen speed with the board as it was (the viewer
  never hides the board), holding the last movement sample as it already
  does where no sample was recorded. Previous and Next still step by
  action; seeking, verification and the 3D viewer are otherwise unchanged.
- **Tests first**: `tests/classic2d-session.test.mjs` pins `createTimer` /
  the session clock running through `pause()` and `resume()`, the spans
  (including a pause while generating and one left open at abandon) and the
  summary's fields; `tests/mode3d-session.test.mjs` and
  `tests/shell-mode-3d.test.mjs` the same for 3D, `sync3D` not stopping the
  clock and the 3D summary's fields; `tests/shell-pause.test.mjs` that
  automatic pauses (blur, hidden, disconnect) and manual ones are counted
  alike and that pause behaviour is unchanged; `tests/records-summary.test.mjs`,
  `tests/records-summary-3d.test.mjs`, `tests/records-model.test.mjs` and
  `tests/records-store.test.mjs` the new fields, their validation and an old
  history entry loading as 0 pauses; `tests/replay-format.test.mjs` and
  `tests/replay-recorder.test.mjs` the new version, the upgrade from version
  1 and the header spans; `tests/replay-2d.test.mjs` and
  `tests/replay-3d.test.mjs` a recorded game with a pause whose action times
  include the paused span; `tests/replay-viewer.test.mjs` the seek bar marks
  and their text; a test that the pause card's time advances while paused.
- **What not to break** (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Decisions taken):
  release-to-act with drag-to-cancel; 3BV, click and efficiency accounting;
  no-guess boards as separate records; the three-way best comparison;
  auto-pinned best replays; the confirmations that say "It will count as a
  loss"; replay determinism for every recorded game.
- The owned documents state the agreed design change — the game clock keeps
  running through every pause, and each game records its pause count, its
  total paused time and, in its replay, its pause spans, shown on the
  replay seek bar — in every passage that stated the old one, and settle
  the points listed under Decisions: `.claude/domain/product-design.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/game-shell.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/classic-2d-square-play.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/3d-play-flow.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/game-summary.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/personal-records.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/replay-recording.md` and
  `.claude/domain/features/replay-playback.md`. Nothing else in those
  documents changes.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction — `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` decision 7 ("The clock keeps running
  while paused; pauses are still recorded") and P-48's proposed solution
  ("Record pause count and paused time in the summary and replay header …
  Show pauses on the replay seek bar either way"), approved without
  condition under § Approved scope and work plan (row W-28).
  - `product-design.md` § Design decisions, "Pausing hides the board in
    every mode": "The clock stops … so pausing never buys free study time"
    becomes: the board is still covered, the clock keeps running and the
    pauses are recorded.
  - `game-shell.md` § Scope and non-goals ("While paused the board is
    hidden in every mode and the timer is stopped") and § Interfaces and
    contracts, mode contract ("`pause()` stops the mode's timer and stops
    it accepting board input") and pause rules ("without a timer to
    stop"): pause hides the board and stops board input; the timer keeps
    running.
  - `classic-2d-square-play.md` § Architecture, "Timer" ("paused while the
    game is paused") and § Interfaces and contracts, "Pause" ("Pause
    freezes the timer"): the timer runs through pauses.
  - `3d-play-flow.md` § Interfaces and contracts, "Pause" ("the timer
    stops"): the timer keeps running.
  - `replay-recording.md` § Architecture, "Recorder" ("paused time is
    absent from a replay, as it is from the timer"): the game clock
    includes paused time; § Data and state, "Header": gains the pause
    spans, with a new format version.
  - `game-summary.md` § Data and state, "Summary record": gains the pause
    count and total paused time.
  - `personal-records.md` § Data and state, "History document": the
    compact summary gains the same two fields, since the history is where a
    recorded summary persists ("pauses are still recorded").
  - `replay-playback.md` § Scope and non-goals, playback controls: the seek
    bar shows the paused spans.

  Settled here, without conflict: replay times follow the running clock,
  so a paused span is a gap in the action stream and playback runs through
  it (`replay-recording.md` § Architecture); old replays upgrade to no
  pauses and old history entries read as 0 (`replay-recording.md` § Data
  and state, `personal-records.md` § Data and state). These documents are
  normally `/architect`'s, and `product-design.md` `/product-design`'s;
  this task updates them for exactly this and for nothing else. A further
  design decision met at implementation time is not covered: leave that
  passage untouched and name it under *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
- **Out of scope, not taken under decision 7**: P-48's alternative of
  making a game paused after its first click ineligible for bests
  ("Paused · not eligible" on results and a marker in the lists), and its
  variant that keeps the clock running only through manual pauses on
  standard boards. No new settings.
- **Recorded, not yet shown on results or Records**: decision 7 asks that
  pauses be recorded; showing them on the results screen or in the Records
  lists is left out of this task, so the fields are data a later task can
  surface (the results figures belong to row W-27, task 128).
- **Spans in the replay, totals in the summary**: the replay header keeps
  each span because the seek bar needs positions; the summary and history
  keep only the count and total, which is all a record needs.
- **Boundaries**: task 128 (row W-27) also edits `js/records/summary.js`,
  `js/classic2d/session.js`, `js/classic2d/mode.js`, `js/main.js` and the
  replay viewer tests; task 126 (row W-19) edits `js/main.js`. Neither
  depends on this task nor this on them; whichever lands second merges
  around the first.
