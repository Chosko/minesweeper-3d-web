# Task 128 — Results precision, figures and hierarchy

Target: claude

## Goal
Give the end of every game the precision and the figures a competitive
player reads first, and a card that ranks the game above its context. This
is row W-27 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan (Area:
Results, Size: Medium, Phase 1). It closes P-33, P-46, P-47, P-51, P-65 and
I-08 of that document. Today one path shows three time formats (overlay
"011", pause card "Time 11.64 s", results "4.6 s", replay bar "004"; P-33,
Minor, everyone), and times are truncated to a tenth on results, Records and
the replay end line, so two games 40 ms apart cannot be told apart and
deltas are tenths too (P-46, Major, enthusiasts). The results screen lacks
click counts and the game's rank on its board, and a loss shows no
comparison at all, so a 90 % loss on best pace looks like a first-click death
(P-47, Major, enthusiasts after every game). Efficiency is a whole
percentage, so 94.7 % → 95.0 % reads "New best · +0 %" and a tie reads
"0 %" (P-51, Minor, everyone). The "Your best on this board" panel outranks
the game's own stats, deltas are uncoloured and a new best is barely marked
(P-65, Minor, everyone after a game). Nothing on the card pulls the player
forward (I-08, the enthusiast loop). Both modes share the results screen,
the overlay bar and the formats, so Classic 2D and 3D both get every change.

## Acceptance criteria
- **One time format outside the live bar** (P-33, P-46): `formatTime(ms)`
  shows seconds to the thousandth, truncated (13 343 ms → "13.343 s", 47 380
  ms → "47.380 s"), and every time shown outside the live bar goes through
  it: the results screen, the Records bests, recent-games list and replays
  list, the replay viewer's end line and seek-bar text, and the pause card
  ("Time 11.643 s · 12 mines left"). `formatTimeDifference` shows the same
  precision, signed, truncated toward zero ("−1.270 s"). The overlay bar's
  timer and the replay viewer's running clock keep whole seconds
  (`formatOverlayTime`, unchanged). No precision setting is added. `fmtTime`
  in `js/ui.js` is gone or reduced to `formatTime`, so no second time format
  remains.
- **Efficiency to one decimal everywhere** (P-51): `formatEfficiency` shows
  one decimal ("94.7%"), on results, Records, the replays list and the
  history chart's labels; `formatEfficiencyDifference` matches.
- **Differences carry a sign only when real** (P-51): for every best
  difference (time, 3BV/s, efficiency), a difference that is zero at the
  stat's display precision reads "= best", with no sign and no number; any
  other difference carries + or −. A new best whose difference is zero at
  display precision reads "New best" alone.
- **Second stat row** (P-47): under the game's own stats, a second row shows
  "Clicks 183 (L 183 · R 0 · D 0 · wasted 0)" — the counted clicks, then the
  reveal, flag and chord clicks (effective plus wasted each) and the wasted
  total, from the summary's `clicks` — on wins and losses alike; on a win it
  also shows "#3 of 12 on this board", the game's rank by time among the
  board's won games, this one included. Any figure that is not available
  shows a dash. The card shows no IOE.
- **Rank** (P-47): a won game's rank is 1 + the number of earlier won games
  on the same board key whose time is at or below this game's; "of N" counts
  the board's won games including this one. The results flow derives it
  from the records store's existing `history(board)` query after recording;
  the records model, its documents and its comparison are unchanged. A
  no-guess board ranks only among its own games, since it is its own board.
- **Comparison on a loss** (P-47): a lost game shows the bests panel too,
  greyed (muted ink, no green or red), labelled "Not eligible — only won
  games set bests", with each standing best and the game's difference from
  it, never "New best". The time tile shows the pace: the projected time at
  this game's rate, elapsed × 3BV ÷ 3BV solved ("Pace 47.912 s"), and its
  difference from the best time; with 3BV solved at 0 the pace is a dash.
  A board with no best yet shows dashes. A recording that failed still
  shows no comparison, as today.
- **Hierarchy** (P-65): the card reads top to bottom: outcome title and
  board, the new-best banner when there is one, the game's own stats (the
  largest figures on the card), the second stat row, the next-target line,
  then the "Your best on this board" panel, then the notes and actions. The
  results-steam-rank panel's place below the comparison is unchanged.
- **Coloured deltas** (P-65): on a win, a difference that is better than
  the best shows in `--color-success` and a worse one in `--color-danger`
  (better is lower for time, higher for 3BV/s and efficiency); "= best"
  shows in the muted ink. Colour is never the only signal: the sign and the
  "New best" text stay. Both themes take their values from the tokens, and
  the text meets the contrast contract in both.
- **New best is a visible event** (P-65): when the game sets one or more
  bests, a banner under the title names them ("New best time and 3BV/s!"),
  and each improved figure in the game's stat row carries a highlight. No
  animation, sound or effect is added here.
- **Next-target line** (I-08): one line under the stats. After a win it
  reads "Beat 48.213 s for a new best · streak 3" — the board's best time as
  it stands after this game and the board's current streak. After a loss
  that ended a streak of at least 1 it reads "Streak ended at 3 · best 7" —
  the streak this loss ended and the board's longest streak. After a loss
  with no streak to end it reads "Beat 48.213 s for a new best", or "Win
  this board to set a best" when the board has no best time. The streak
  before the game is read from the records store's `counters(board)` before
  the flow records the summary, and the screen still shows only after
  recording.
- **Live click counter** (P-47): the overlay bar shows a "Clicks" readout
  beside the timer and the mines counter, in both modes, fed from the same
  engine click counts as the summary: 0 before the first click, every
  counted click (wasted included) as it is made, frozen with the timer when
  the game ends, written only on change. The replay viewer's bar is
  unchanged.
- **What not to break** (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § What not to break): 3BV,
  click and efficiency accounting; no-guess boards as separate records; the
  three-way best comparison and the replay pinned on a new best; record
  before show; the stored milliseconds and record and history formats.
- Tests come first: the formats, `resultsContent` (second row, rank, loss
  comparison and pace, "= best", delta direction, banner, next-target line
  in each of its forms), the flow's rank and streak data, the
  overlay bar's click readout and the modes' feed, the pause line, and the
  Records, replays-list and replay-viewer format assertions; the full suite
  passes.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction — `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` P-33, P-46, P-47, P-51, P-65 and I-08,
  approved without condition under § Approved scope and work plan (row
  W-27).
  - `results-screen.md` § Architecture, "Results view", shows time to the
    tenth and efficiency as a whole percentage; times go to the thousandth
    (P-46) and efficiency to one decimal (P-51).
  - `results-screen.md` § Scope and non-goals lists the screen's stats and
    "On a win, the comparison"; the screen adds the second stat row with
    clicks, and the rank on a win, the next-target line and the new-best
    banner, shows the comparison on a loss greyed and not eligible with the
    pace, and puts the game's figures above the bests panel with coloured
    deltas (P-47, P-65, I-08); § Architecture, "Results flow", also reads
    the board's streak before recording and its history after.
  - `3d-results-records-screens.md` § Scope, "the three-best comparison on
    a win" and the listed contents: the 3D results screen takes the same
    new contents and the comparison on a loss (P-47).
  - `screen-components.md` § Architecture, "Overlay bar" (timer, mine
    counter, pause), and `classic-2d-square-play.md` § Interfaces, "Overlay
    feed" (timer and mines left): the overlay gains a live click counter
    and the feed carries the click count (P-47).
  - `records-screen.md` § Architecture ("Records view", "Recent games
    list"), `replay-library.md` § Architecture ("Library view") and
    `leaderboard-screen.md` § Architecture ("Entry list") show times to the
    tenth (47.3 s); they follow the one format to the thousandth (P-46).
  - `results-steam-rank.md` § Scope and non-goals gives "a local rank would
    repeat the personal-best comparison" as the reason for no rank panel in
    the web build; the web results screen now carries a local rank (P-47),
    so the reason goes and the non-goal stays.

  These documents are normally `/architect`'s; this task updates them for
  exactly this and for nothing else. A further design decision met at
  implementation time is not covered: leave that passage untouched and name
  it under *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
- **No precision setting.** P-46 offers a setting only "if tenths are wanted
  for casual readers"; the approved row asks for thousandths, so one format
  is kept and no setting is added.
- **IOE dropped (director decision, 2026-10-12; `docs/UX_REVIEW.md`
  P-47):** the results screen shows no IOE, and nothing takes its place.
  P-47 lists IOE among the second row's figures, but under this project's
  click rule IOE always equals efficiency as a ratio, so it would repeat a
  figure the card already shows. No IOE derivation is added, and
  `game-summary.md` is not touched.
- **Rank by time, ties to the earlier game.** Time is the reference ranking
  stat; ranking a tie behind the earlier game matches the records' rule that
  a tie keeps the earlier holder. Deriving it from the existing history
  query keeps the records model and its documents unchanged; a local top-10
  (I-05) is W-31's.
- **Pace is the projected time.** "Pace" in P-47 is read as the time this
  game would have finished in at its own rate, compared with the best time,
  which is what tells a 90 % loss on best pace from a first-click death.
- **"= best" at display precision, uniformly.** P-51 is about efficiency,
  but the rule "a sign only for real differences" applies to every delta,
  so time and 3BV/s follow it too.
- **Streaks are the board's.** The next-target line names the board, so its
  streak and longest streak are the board's counters, not the mode's.
- **Out of scope:** the board code on results (P-47's seed as a board code,
  I-04) is W-33's; the end-of-game celebration that P-65 pairs with the new
  best (P-64) is W-11's, and the banner here stands on its own; Records
  layout and lists beyond their time and efficiency formats are W-30's; the
  local top-10 is W-31's; click markers in the replay viewer are W-32's.
- **Task 104 follows the one format.** Task 104 (leaderboard screen)
  reuses `formatTime` and states thousandths (director decision,
  2026-10-12); the `leaderboard-screen.md` passage it cites is this task's
  to update, as listed above.
