# Task 177 — 3D game overlay in the in-game layout

Target: claude

## Goal
Fit the 3D game's overlay to the in-game layout of feature `in-game-layout`,
as J-Game3D-xl/-l/-m/-p/-ls and J-Game3D-states draw it: the full-window
scene keeps its place; the flags counter, Restart and the timer sit at the
top centre; on the right the figures panel is headed by a bar with the title
and Pause (Esc), the live figures below, the panel — and the H help panel
beside it — centred vertically; each part on its own themed surface so it
stays readable over any sky.

## Acceptance criteria
- The overlay is composed from its artboards using catalogued components
  only (task 175's counter strip and figures panel with its head bar).
- **Title shortening:** the title drops the difficulty when the full name
  does not fit on one line in the panel's bar ("3D" for "3D · Cube
  Intermediate"), the full name kept as the element's tooltip and
  accessible name; it is measured, not decided by window size, so it holds
  at every desktop size the artboards draw.
- **Phone landscape** (J-Game3D-ls): the title as a pill at the top left,
  the counters and Restart at the top centre, Pause as an icon button at
  the top right. **Phone portrait** (J-Game3D-p): the title and Pause bar on
  top, the counters below. Both phones keep the full name.
- The touch hint strip wraps onto a second line instead of overflowing.
- The crosshair and aim readout, the off-screen arrow, the view chips, the
  HUD key strip, the hint strip, the toast, and the touch stick and Shift /
  Space / Ctrl toolbar take the places and looks J-Game3D-states draws;
  pointer lock, picking and the end sequence are unchanged.
- The live figures come from task 174's model, fed from the 3D game's
  counts, with bests read once at game start.
- Every readout meets the contrast contract against its own surface over the
  brightest and darkest sky the theme can draw.
- Existing 3D tests are updated first where they assert the old arrangement
  (and for the title shortening and the phone layouts), and pass.

## Decisions
- **Owner decisions** (DESIGN-CHANGES §4.5): counters at the top centre, the
  panel headed by the title and Pause, vertically centred, and the title
  shortening rule. These replace the head strip this task first carried.
- **Its artboards exist** — J-Game3D-* on the canvas — so the manual
  intervention this task carried while the artboard was undrawn is gone and
  the task is `claude`.
