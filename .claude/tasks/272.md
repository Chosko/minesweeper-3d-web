# Task 272 — Records model and summaries for Hexagon and Triangle

Target: claude

## Goal
Part of feature `flat-grid-records-replays`: Hexagon and Triangle games
count toward personal bests, stats history, win rate and streaks per board
and per mode, never combined with Classic 2D or 3D. The records' list of
modes grows by `hexagon` and `triangle`, and a records format step adds
their overall counters.

## Acceptance criteria
- The shared summary builder accepts a hexagon or triangle board identity,
  with 3BV, 3BV/s, efficiency and best eligibility unchanged in rule. An
  identity whose grid does not match its mode (a `hexagon` board on the
  `tri` grid) is a `RangeError`.
- `MODES` includes `hexagon` and `triangle`. A game counts on its board and
  its own mode's overall only, so a hexagon loss never moves a Classic 2D
  streak.
- The records format version steps up once. An earlier document loads with
  the two modes' counters at zero and everything else carried over
  unchanged; a newer document is refused as today. Rebuilding from history
  rebuilds the new counters.
- Boards played by mode, for each new mode: its standard boards without then
  with no-guess, then custom boards by last played; no Daily board is
  listed.
- A standard board's label is its grid's standard name ("Expert ·
  no-guess"). Screens listing boards from several modes lead with the mode's
  name.
- The board-key format version does not change.
