# Task 156 — Dark theme: menu-screen backdrop and context-lost card

Target: claude

## Goal
Finish the dark theme on the two light tints task 120 (Dark theme
completion) missed. Task 120 themed the four scrims laid over the scene, but
two more light surfaces remain. First, the gradient behind `#menu`,
`#board-choice`, `#coming-soon`, `#c2d-choice`, `#settings` and `#records` in
`css/style.css` is a hard-coded light blue, so in the dark theme it lays a
pale haze over the dark sky behind every menu screen and partly undoes
`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` P-06. Second, the context-lost card
(`#ctx-lost .ready-card`) stays near-white in the dark theme while its text
is light, so it is unreadable there (the defect P-03 described for other
cards), and `.ready-back` with its hover state keeps white fills. The
backdrop takes a themed token pair following task 120's backdrop-scrim
pattern; the card and the Back button take the kit's surface tokens.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Themed menu-screen backdrop:** `css/tokens.css` declares two colour
  tokens for the menu screens' gradient, beside `--color-backdrop-scrim`,
  `-soft` and `-strong`. Their light values equal today's literals,
  `rgba(200, 225, 255, 0.15)` and `rgba(200, 225, 255, 0.35)`. Their dark
  values are declared under `:root[data-theme="dark"]` as translucent dark
  placeholders that lay no light haze over the dark sky. The
  `#menu, #board-choice, #coming-soon, #c2d-choice, #settings, #records`
  rule in `css/style.css` reads them and keeps no literal colour. The
  `body.no-webgl2` rule is unchanged.
- **Token names respect the hue rule:** neither new name contains a hue word
  matched by "token names are semantic, never named after a hue" in
  `tests/tokens.test.mjs`. "sky", "blue" and "white" are hue words there.
- **Context-lost card and Back on surface tokens:** `#ctx-lost .ready-card`
  takes `--color-surface-raised`. `.ready-back` takes `--color-surface`, and
  `.ready-back:hover` takes `--color-surface-raised`. No literal colour
  remains in those three rules.
- **Token test:** `tests/tokens.test.mjs` pins that the two new tokens have a
  translucent light value and a dark value, with the light values equal to
  the original tints. Its literal-free check covers the menu-screen backdrop
  rule, `#ctx-lost .ready-card`, `.ready-back` and `.ready-back:hover`. This
  test is written in the same style as the existing backdrop-scrim tests.
- **Contrast in both themes:** a browser test in the style of the existing
  contrast tests in `tests/components.test.mjs`, using the same `check`
  helper, shows `#ctx-lost` in both themes. It measures the card's text and
  its "Main menu" `.ready-back` against what they sit on, requires at least
  4.5:1 for every run, and takes a screenshot per theme. The test fails on
  today's near-white card in the dark theme.
- **No haze behind menu screens:** a Playwright check screenshots the menu
  screens in both themes, as the existing theme tests do. It shows the dark
  theme's backdrop carrying no light haze (the computed gradient stops are
  the dark tokens, not the light tint), and the light screenshot shows the
  backdrop unchanged.
- **Light theme:** the menu-screen backdrop is pixel-equal to today's. The
  context-lost card and the Back buttons may shift slightly in the light
  theme, because they move from the old literals to the kit's surface tokens.
- `.claude/context/app-shell-kit.md` (and `.claude/context/testing.md` for the
  test list) describe the menu-screen backdrop tokens and the context-lost
  card and Back on surface tokens. `.claude/context/rendering.md` changes
  only if it names the scrim tokens.
- `.claude/domain/features/design-tokens-and-themes.md` states that the
  menu-screen backdrop tint is a themed token with a light and a dark value,
  like the other tints laid over the scene, and settles nothing else.
  Nothing else in the document changes.

## Decisions
- **Skipped** (2026-10-13): obsolete — it themes the dark look that
  milestone m2b-themes removes; task 158 retires the light and dark looks
  and gives the menu screens' backdrop and the context-lost card *Joyful
  tiles* tokens.
- **Surface tokens over pixel equality for the card and Back (user decision,
  2026-10-13):** the card takes `--color-surface-raised`. `.ready-back` takes
  `--color-surface`, and `--color-surface-raised` on hover. Exact light-theme
  equality is required only for the menu-screen backdrop. The light card
  (white at 0.96 opacity becomes opaque white) and the light Back button
  (white at 0.55 becomes 0.78) may shift slightly. This matches what task 120
  did for the Controls panel card.
- **`.ready-back` is shared:** the class also styles the click-to-play card's
  Back (`#ready-back`), not only the context-lost "Main menu" button. The fix
  applies to both, since the click-to-play Back has the same white fill
  under dark-theme text.
- **Settled design detail, not a design change:**
  `design-tokens-and-themes.md` already requires a light and a dark value for
  every colour token. This task settles that the menu-screen backdrop tint is
  one of them; it states no new design. `.claude/domain/features/*.md` is
  normally `/architect`'s. This task updates that document for exactly this
  and for nothing else. A further design decision met at implementation
  time is not covered: leave that passage untouched and name it under
  *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
- **Dark values are placeholders**, per the feature document's rule for
  visual-design judgements: translucent dark tints that keep every overlay
  over the menu screens inside the contrast contract.
