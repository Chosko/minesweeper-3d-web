# Task 239 — Replay viewer to the canvas

Target: claude

## Goal
Bring the replay viewer (2D and 3D) in line with the *Joyful tiles*
canvas (Claude Design artifact
https://claude.ai/artifact/LeP32jxNxEnHLUtRwJ3FNC, page "Joyful tiles").
A design review found the frame, overlay bar, body, figures panel, seek
bar, toolbar, key line, end line, 3BV tints, recorded pointer, 3D
composition and phone layouts drift, and the free-camera notice is not
built. Part of the canvas drift fixes; intended feature:
`shared-screen-layout`. Drift ids: H2-1, H2-2, H2-5, H2-6, H2-7 (use),
H2-9, H2-11, H2-12, H2-14, H2-15, H2-17, H2-18, H2-19, H2-21, H2-22,
H2-23, the replay part of K33, P5. (Frame head steps H2-3/4 are task
223's, the framed fitted board H2-8 task 227's, the failure card H2-20
task 223's, button sizes H2-13 task 218's, key caps H2-16 task 218's.)

## Acceptance criteria
- **Frame (H2-1).** Inset on the backdrop like Settings: xl left 140, top
  66, 1640×940; l 32/24, 1302×712; m 20/16, 984×728 — not the full window.
- **Back (H2-2).** Arrow-left icon (18px) before the label, gap 10.
- **Overlay bar (H2-5).** A strip spanning the full body width: radius 16,
  `inset 0 4px 0` shade-soft plus `inset 0 0 0 2px` border (no drop),
  padding 8px 14px, gap 28; items inline with no labels — clock JetBrains
  Mono 30px/700, a flag icon with "024" at 20px, "3BV 54 / 118" and "3BV/s
  2.70" in mono 17px. (Shipped: a content-width pill of labelled stat
  tiles.)
- **Body (H2-6).** Padding 16px 22px (12px 16px at l and m), stack gap 12,
  board area to panel gap 18.
- **Figures panel (H2-9).** Stretches (flex 1) to the board area's bottom.
- **Seek bar (H2-11).** No "Position" label and no readout; full width.
  Track 8px --color-line; played part filled --color-ink up to the thumb;
  thumb 24px with an inset 3px ink ring and a 3px edge drop; ticks 3×9px,
  radius 2, sitting above the track (top 2, track top 11); pause marks
  hatched `repeating-linear-gradient(45deg, replay-pause 0 3px,
  transparent 3px 6px)`.
- **Icons (H2-12).** Play / Pause / Play again: play, pause, restart; Show
  3BV grid; Sound speaker; Free camera camera; Download and Open replay
  file download and upload.
- **Files placement (H2-14).** Download and Open replay file at the right
  end of the tools row (`margin-left:auto`) at xl and l; their own
  right-aligned row above the key line at m; right-aligned at p.
- **Key line (H2-15).** One line at 13px, gap 14: "Space play / pause · ←
  → action · , . frame · - = speed · B 3BV · Esc back" (3D adds "C
  camera"); keyboard keys only, lower case; controller glyphs appear only
  while a pad is connected. No wrap at l and m.
- **End line (H2-17).** In the tools row after Sound: a won badge (12px,
  .06em, padding 3px 8px, success-fill) plus the time in mono 17px/700
  ("48.213 s"); the play button reads "Play again" with the restart icon.
- **Show 3BV (H2-18).** The pressed toggle is primary (task 218); tints are
  tile-sized rounded squares (radius 4) in --color-confetti-2/3/5 at
  opacity .42, no outline on isolated numbers; the
  `--color-replay-bbbv-*` palette goes (and task 224 retires its tokens).
- **Recorded pointer (H2-19).** Keep the shipped dot pointer and trail;
  rings take the canvas look — 30px, 3px border, the flag ring at opacity
  .6 — while keeping the expanding animation (scale .35 to 1.4 while
  fading).
- **3D composition (H2-21, P5).** The frame keeps its white body and normal
  layout with the 3D scene inside the board area (not a transparent body
  over a full-window scene with floating bar and panel); Free camera is
  primary when on, with the camera icon; the canvas's free-camera notice
  is built.
- **Phone landscape (H2-22).** The right column holds only the strip, the
  seek bar and the transport (Prev, Pause, Next).
- **Phone portrait (H2-23).** Speed, toggles and files each on their own
  row, files right-aligned; the board fills the width; no Position row.
- Replay viewer tests updated; renders match the artboards.
- `.claude/context/replay-viewer-screen.md` and `replay-viewers.md`
  reflect the viewer.
