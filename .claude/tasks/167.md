# Task 167 — 3D board choice screen in the shared layout

Target: claude+human

## Goal
Recompose the 3D board choice screen, for feature `shared-screen-layout`,
inside the screen frame with the same composition as the Classic 2D board
choice (task 166): Learn 3D first, then the named 3D presets of
`PRESETS_3D` in their two groups, First box and Cube, and Custom as board
options, each preset row showing its name, size line, mine density and the
Last played mark; Random fill; the two no-guess switches with their limits;
Play on the right with its key hint beside a non-accent *Records* action;
and the records panel of the selected 3D board, from the 3D records,
following the no-guess switch that governs it. Its *Joyful tiles* artboard
is not drawn yet.

## Acceptance criteria
- The screen is composed from its supplied artboard using catalogued
  components only, sharing the records panel built in task 166.
- Learn 3D is the first option; the presets follow in the First box group,
  then the Cube group, labelled as the artboard draws them.
- Each preset row shows its name, size line, mine density and the Last
  played mark; no typical 3BV, no best and no streak on the row. Selecting
  an option updates the panel at once.
- The panel's title names the selected board and, when the switch that
  governs it is on, "NO-GUESS" ("<BOARD> · YOUR BESTS", "<BOARD> · NO-GUESS ·
  YOUR BESTS"). Bests (time, 3BV/s, efficiency, with when each was set),
  wins, win rate, streak, a Time chart and under it a 3BV/s and Efficiency
  chart over the board's last 30 won games, the recent games and under them
  a Top 5 of its fastest wins all follow that switch, as task 166's panel
  does for Classic 2D. No trend figure anywhere on the screen.
- While Learn 3D is selected, the panel shows what the artboard draws for
  it; Learn 3D keeps no records.
- Play sits on the right; the *Records* action, not accented, opens the
  Records screen on the selected 3D board and switch, Back returning here.
- The panel reads the 3D records through the records store's existing read
  interface and change notification and holds no records of its own; a
  board with no games shows an empty state.
- The 8,000-cell no-guess limit, no no-guess on a side of 2, Random fill,
  custom limits, Learn 3D's start and the last 3D board choice document
  behave as before.
- Every element id, route, default focus and controller navigation target
  survives; existing 3D board choice and returning tests pass, updated only
  where they assert the old arrangement or the old preset detail line.
- No horizontal scroll at 1024 px wide; contrast checks pass under *Joyful
  tiles*.

## Decisions
- **Same pattern as Classic 2D** — rows, records panel, its contents and
  the Play and *Records* buttons follow task 166 (owner's answer:
  "Replicate as 2D does").
- **Learn 3D's panel and the group labels follow the artboard** — not
  invented here (owner's answer: "Let Design decide").
- **Waits on its artboard**, per `shared-screen-layout.md` § Architecture,
  *Design status per screen*.
- **Feature document over artboard.** Where the artboard draws 3BV, a best
  or a streak on preset rows, a trend figure or a different button
  placement, the feature documents win.
