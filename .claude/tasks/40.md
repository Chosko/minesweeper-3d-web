# Task 40 — Settings appliers: theme, audio, look, resolution, fullscreen

Target: claude

## Goal
Apply every setting of feature `settings` through its owner subscribing to
the store: the theme applier, the sound module's volume and mute, the 3D
camera's sensitivity and invert Y, the 3D renderer's pixel ratio, and the
browser's fullscreen state — with the store knowing none of them.

## Acceptance criteria
- Theme: the store's theme value drives `setTheme`, and the theme applier's
  start-up getter is wired so the first screen never shows the wrong theme.
- Audio: `js/audio.js` takes volume and mute from the store and no longer
  reads or writes its own `localStorage` keys.
- Look: the 3D camera's sensitivity and invert Y come from the store;
  `loadLookSettings` and its keys in `js/ui.js` are retired.
- Render resolution: Auto keeps the current adaptive cap
  (`min(devicePixelRatio, 2)`), Sharp uses the full device pixel ratio, Fast
  uses 1; a change applies without reloading.
- Fullscreen: setting it requests or exits fullscreen; when the browser
  leaves fullscreen on its own, the store follows the browser's state.
- Node tests cover the subscription wiring with fakes; Playwright confirms a
  theme change and a resolution change apply at once.
