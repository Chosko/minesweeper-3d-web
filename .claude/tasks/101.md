# Task 101 — Achievement unlocker, catch-up and the end-of-game hook

Target: claude

## Goal
Connect feature `steam-achievements` to Steam: the unlocker that sets the
achievements not yet set and stores, catch-up over `personal-records`'
history at start-up and on reconnect, and the end-of-game hook, all through
the achievements capability of the desktop host's bridge.

## Acceptance criteria
- The unlocker asks the adapter which names are set, sets the rest and
  stores; offline unlocks are left to the Steam client; with Steam
  unavailable nothing is attempted.
- `catchUp()` runs at start-up once Steam is available and on each
  logged-on event, evaluates every won game in the history and unlocks
  what is missing; it is idempotent and stores nothing of its own.
- `onGameFinished(summary)` is called by the end-of-game hand-off after the
  summary is recorded; it returns at once and unlocks in the background.
- With the achievements capability off (the web build), both calls do
  nothing.
- A failed set or store is retried at the next catch-up; nothing
  interrupts play or the results flow.
- Node tests use a fake bridge: catch-up over a history, already-set names,
  offline, unavailable and failures.
