# Task 81 — 3D end sequence and the results takeover

Target: claude

## Goal
Replace the 3D game's end banner for feature `3d-results-records-screens`:
at a win or loss input freezes while today's end effect plays, and after a
fixed end delay the mode releases pointer lock and reports the game
finished, so the shared results screen takes over the 3D scene completely.

## Acceptance criteria
- At a win or loss the 3D session stops the timer, freezes the HUD's end
  state, and ignores board actions, camera movement and view-mode keys; Esc
  and the controller's Start are ignored and pause is not offered until the
  results screen shows.
- The renderer plays today's end effect; after one end-delay constant
  (about one second, shared by win and loss) the mode releases pointer lock
  and reports `game finished(summary, replay)` once; nothing during the
  delay can restart or leave the game.
- The results screen shows over 3D on an opaque surface in the chosen
  theme; the 3D scene is not visible behind it and stops rendering while it
  shows.
- The end banner, its compact form and its best-time line are removed; a 3D
  game is never returned to after the results screen.
- *Play again* starts the same 3D board choice, re-acquiring pointer lock or
  entering lockless play from a controller.
- When the tab is hidden during the delay, the summary is still recorded at
  the end of the delay and the results screen shows when the tab returns; a
  lost graphics context during the delay is reported as a mode failure and
  the summary is still recorded.
- Node tests cover the delay, the ignored input and the hidden-tab rule
  with fakes; Playwright wins and loses a fixed 3D board and checks the
  results screen.

## Decisions
- The end delay is one fixed constant of one second, as the feature's
  architecture states. Its open question on large boards stays open for
  watching real games; settling it changes only that constant.
