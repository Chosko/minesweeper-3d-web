# Task 148 — Records layout, pagination and lists

Target: claude

## Goal
Give the Records screen a reading hierarchy, bounded lists and a layout that
uses a landscape window, and give the Settings page the same layout. This is
row W-30 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan (Area:
Records, Size: Medium, Phase 5). It closes P-53, P-67, P-68 and P-69 of that
document, all Minor. The bests panel (best time, best 3BV/s and best
efficiency with their dates and Watch) is a cramped block of fields with no
margins and no reading hierarchy (P-68, everyone reading Records). The
replay list grows without bound, and the recent-games list pages with Newer
and Older buttons that show even when everything fits one page (P-67,
grinders with many games). Both tables read a bare date such as
"11 Oct 2026" with no time, the recent-games list has no 3BV, a kept replay
cannot be deleted (only unpinned), and the Pinned first switch is forgotten
between visits (P-53, grinders with many games a day). Every screen is a
single column, so Records gets crowded and Settings is a 1,400 px scroll
while most of a 1280 px window stays empty (P-69, desktop players). Both
sides of the Records 2D | 3D switch get every change. Richer stats and the
local top-10 (I-09, I-05) are W-31, which follows this task.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Bests as three stat tiles** (P-68): the chosen board's best time, best
  3BV/s and best efficiency each show as a tile — the label, the figure
  large, and beneath it, small, the date the best was set and its Watch
  button when the replay is kept. A best not yet set shows a dash and no
  Watch. Tile padding, gaps and figure size come from the design tokens
  (`--space-*`, `--font-size-*`), with no literal size or colour; both
  themes follow from the tokens and meet the contrast contract. The tile is
  a kit component (a `ui-stat` modifier or a new class), catalogued in
  `css/components.css`, shown in every state in `dev/components.html` and
  listed in `tests/components.test.mjs`.
- **Figures on their own rows** (P-68): under the tiles, the board's
  counters (games, wins, win rate, streaks) are one row, and the chosen
  mode's overall figures (titled "Classic 2D overall" or "3D overall") are
  a separate row of their own, never mixed with the board's. The empty state
  for a board with no games still hides the tiles, counters and history.
- **Figures from tasks 128 and 130 are carried**: the tiles and rows show
  every figure Records has when this lands — times through the results
  screen's `formatTime` at whatever precision it has (thousandths once
  task 128 lands), efficiency through `formatEfficiency`, and the
  completed-games win rate and best completed streak beside the reference
  figures for the board and for the mode once task 130 lands. Whichever of
  the three tasks lands last keeps every figure the others added; none is
  dropped by the new layout.
- **Recent games: date and time, 3BV** (P-53): the recent-games table gains
  a Date column, first, and a 3BV column (the game's board 3BV from its
  history entry), so its columns read Date, Outcome, Time, 3BV, 3BV/s,
  Efficiency, then Replay when the replay route is registered.
- **Date and time in both tables** (P-53): the recent-games and replays
  tables show each game's end as a date and a local time, for example
  "11 Oct 2026 14:32" (24-hour, minutes), through one shared format beside
  `formatDate`. The bests tiles keep the date alone.
- **Both lists page at ten rows** (P-67): the recent-games list and the
  replays list each show at most ten rows per page, newest first (the
  replays pinned first under the filter, each group newest first). Under
  each list a pager shows First, a run of numbered page buttons and Last;
  the current page is marked (`aria-current="page"`, distinct styling) and
  disabled, and First and Last are disabled on the first and last page.
  The numbered run shows at most five page numbers around the current page,
  so a long history keeps one pager row. The pager is hidden when one page
  holds everything, including an empty list. Newer and Older are gone. The
  pager is one kit component used by both lists, catalogued, in the gallery
  and in `tests/components.test.mjs`, and every button is a native
  `<button>` operable by mouse, keyboard and controller. A pager button that
  becomes disabled while focused hands focus to the current page's button.
- **Paging state** (P-67): `show`, `select` and `setMode` start both lists
  on their first page, and toggling Pinned first starts the replays list on
  its first page; a live redraw (a game recorded, a replay added, pinned,
  unpinned or removed) keeps each list on its page, clamped to the last
  page when the list shrinks. The recent-games caption keeps its range
  ("Games 11–20 of 57"); the replays list gains the same caption.
- **Delete a kept replay behind a confirmation** (P-53): each replays row
  gains a Delete button beside Watch and Pin / Unpin. Pressing it opens a
  confirmation in the replays panel, in the style of the pause card's
  `#pause-confirm`: a message naming the replay (its date, time and
  outcome) and saying it cannot be undone, a confirm button labelled
  "Delete replay" and Cancel; for a pinned replay the message also says it
  is pinned (as a best, by hand, or both). Focus moves to Cancel when it
  opens. Cancel, Esc and the browser or controller Back close it and
  change nothing, returning focus to that row's Delete; while it shows,
  Back closes it first and does not leave Records. Confirming removes the
  replay; focus then goes to the Delete button of the row that took its
  place, else the previous row's, else the Pinned first switch.
- **Library removal** (P-53): the replay library gains
  `remove(id)` → Promise, which removes that entry from the index whether or
  not it is pinned, saves the index, then deletes its blob, and notifies
  `{ kind: 'remove', ids: [id] }`; an unknown id changes nothing, and a call
  before `load()` throws, like `pin` and `unpin`. A blob whose delete fails
  is an orphan the next start-up's reconciliation deletes, as with
  retention. A failed index save follows the library's existing not-saving
  rule. Retention, best pinning on a new best and hand pinning are
  unchanged. After a best's replay is deleted, that best's tile and the
  game's recent-games row show no Watch.
- **Pinned first remembered** (P-53): the Pinned first switch's state is
  kept in its own platform-storage document (for example `records.view` at
  version 1, `{ pinnedFirst }`), registered and loaded before the Records
  screen first shows, saved on every change of the switch, and applied on
  every later visit and after a reload. A missing, malformed or newer
  document gives off; a failed save keeps the choice for the session and
  says nothing more. The document is cleared by task 131's Reset all data
  when that task lands, since its clear removes every document.
- **Records in columns above about 1024 px** (P-69): in a window 1024 px
  wide or more, the Records screen widens beyond `ui-screen--wide` and lays
  out in two columns — the 2D | 3D switch, the board picker, the bests
  tiles, the board's counters and the mode's overall row on one side; the
  history chart, the recent-games list and the replays list on the other —
  with the note line and Back below both. Below 1024 px it stays one
  column, in that order, and at phone width (390 px) nothing scrolls
  horizontally. The chart redraws at its new size through its existing
  resize observer.
- **Settings sections side by side above about 1024 px** (P-69): in a
  window 1024 px wide or more, the Settings page widens like Records and
  shows its sections side by side — the setting sections in columns, and
  the Bindings panel with its mode × device lists side by side rather than
  stacked — with Back, and task 131's Data panel when it has landed, at the
  bottom across the full width. The layout is generated from
  `SETTINGS_SECTIONS` and the bindings source, so a section or setting that
  other tasks add joins a column without layout code of its own. Below
  1024 px it stays one column; at phone width nothing scrolls horizontally.
  The schema, the controls and the default focus are unchanged.
- **The layout is one composition**: the column layout and its 1024 px
  breakpoint are one composition in `css/components.css` (catalogued, with
  its breakpoint stated once), used by both screens; spacing between
  columns and panels comes from the tokens.
- **Navigation holds**: the default focus stays the chosen board in the
  picker (Records) and the look sensitivity slider (Settings); Tab order
  follows the visual order in both layouts. Every new button (pager,
  Delete, confirmation) is reachable by keyboard and controller; when task
  144 has landed, they are stops on its Records walk in DOM order and LB /
  RB / LT / RT keep switching boards and modes in both layouts.
- **What not to break** (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § What not to break): the
  three-way best comparison; auto-pinned best replays; no-guess boards as
  separate records; the honest empty states and storage notices — the
  records not-saved note and the replays-not-saved notice, each told once
  per session as today.
- Tests come first: the tile content, the overall row, the date-and-time
  format, the recent-games 3BV and Date columns, `pageOf` and the pager
  state (the five-number window, First and Last, hidden on one page, clamp
  on shrink) for both lists, the replays caption, Delete and its
  confirmation (message for pinned and unpinned, cancel, focus), the
  library's `remove`, the remembered Pinned first document (load, save,
  malformed, failed save), the kit's new components and composition, and
  the Settings page markup; the full suite passes. A Playwright check at
  1280 × 800, 900 × 800 and 390 × 844, in both themes, confirms the column
  count of Records and Settings and no horizontal scroll.
- `.claude/domain/features/records-screen.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/replay-library.md` and
  `.claude/domain/features/screen-components.md` state the agreed design
  change — date and time and a 3BV column in the recent-games list, the
  remembered Pinned first filter as the screen's one persisted state, the
  player's Delete of a kept replay behind a confirmation as the one removal
  besides retention, date and time in the library view, and the stat tile,
  the pager and the column composition in the kit — in every passage that
  stated the old one, and settle the bests as stat tiles, both lists paged
  at ten rows with numbered pages plus First and Last, and the Records and
  Settings column layouts above about 1024 px
  (`.claude/domain/features/settings.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/3d-results-records-screens.md`). Nothing else in
  those documents changes.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction — `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` P-53, P-67, P-68 and P-69, approved
  without condition under § Approved scope and work plan (row W-30).
  - `records-screen.md` § Scope and non-goals lists the recent games "with
    their outcome, time, 3BV/s and efficiency"; the list adds the date and
    time and a 3BV column (P-53).
  - `records-screen.md` § Data and state says "No persisted state"; the
    Pinned first filter is remembered in its own document (P-53).
  - `replay-library.md` (opening summary: pinned replays "are never
    removed"; § Scope and § Architecture "Library view": *Watch* and
    *Pin* / *Unpin*; § Interfaces and contracts: pin / unpin) — the player
    can delete any kept replay, pinned or not, behind a confirmation, and
    the library gains the matching removal; retention and storage failure
    still never remove a pinned replay (P-53).
  - `replay-library.md` § Architecture, "Library view", lists a replay's
    date; it shows date and time (P-53).
  - `screen-components.md` § Scope and non-goals lists the kit's
    components; the kit gains the stat tile, the pager and the column
    composition with its breakpoint (P-67, P-68, P-69).

  Settled here, without conflict: the recent-games list's paging form,
  ten rows with numbered pages plus First and Last, hidden on one page
  (`records-screen.md` § Architecture, "Recent games list"; P-67); the
  library view paged the same way (`replay-library.md` § Architecture,
  "Library view"; P-67); the bests as stat tiles and the Records columns
  (`records-screen.md` § Architecture, "Records view"; P-68, P-69); the
  mode's overall figures on their own row
  (`3d-results-records-screens.md` § Architecture, "Records mode switch";
  P-68); the Settings sections side by side
  (`settings.md` § Architecture, "Settings page"; P-69). These documents
  are normally `/architect`'s; this task updates them for exactly this and
  for nothing else. A further design decision met at implementation time is
  not covered: leave that passage untouched and name it under *Follow-ups*
  as a precise follow-up.
- **Delete works on pinned replays too.** P-53's complaint is that only
  Unpin exists; requiring an Unpin first adds a step and protects nothing,
  since Unpin already clears the best pin. The confirmation names the pin
  instead, and auto-pinning of new bests is untouched.
- **Pinned first lives in its own small document.** The settings schema
  generates the Settings page, so a Records view preference there would
  appear as a setting; a one-field document follows the last board choice
  and last mode documents.
- **A five-number pager window.** Ten-row pages over a long history can
  reach a hundred pages; a bounded run of numbers around the current page
  with First and Last keeps one pager row, which is what P-67's "numbered
  pages plus first and last" needs at that length.
- **3BV is the board's 3BV for every game.** The history entry carries it
  for won, lost and abandoned games alike; 3BV solved is not added, so the
  column reads one figure per row.
- **Date and time, 24-hour local.** One fixed format, like `formatDate`,
  so rows line up and tests are stable across locales; the bests keep the
  date alone, as P-68 asks for "its date".
- **Two columns for Records, grouped as P-69 names them**: picker and
  bests beside the chart and lists. Settings takes as many columns as its
  sections fill at the width, from the same composition.
- **Out of scope:** averages, win rate over the last 50, the best-time-by-
  date chart, period and outcome filters and CSV or JSON export (I-09), and
  the local top-10 per board (I-05) are W-31's; the board code that P-53
  mentions for the recent list is W-33's (I-04); replay viewer changes are
  W-32's; records durability (P-49, P-50) is dropped by decision 9.
