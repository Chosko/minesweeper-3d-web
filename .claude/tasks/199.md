# Task 199 — Larger stat labels and key caps on every pointer, 44 px hit areas still touch-only

Target: claude

## Goal
Deliver the low-vision text minimum of UX review item P-45 on every pointer,
not only on touch. The UX review drift audit (`docs/UX_REVIEW_DRIFT.md`,
"Five most important findings" #4, § 1 row P-45 "drift") found that the
12 px stat labels and 11 px key caps sit only in the `(pointer: coarse)`
blocks (`.ui-stat__label` in `css/components.css`, `kbd` in
`css/style.css`), so a mouse player at 1024 × 768 still gets 11 px HUD
labels and about 10 px key caps. The user decided the text sizes apply on
every pointer, while the 44 px hit areas (switches, slider thumbs, the
overlay bar's Pause, the 3D touch toolbar, `#hud-tap`) stay touch-only.

## Acceptance criteria
- On a fine pointer and on a coarse pointer alike, `.ui-stat__label`
  renders at 12 px and a `kbd` key cap at least 11 px (`max(11px, 0.78em)`
  or equivalent). The rules leave the `(pointer: coarse)` blocks and live
  in the top-level rules; the `--font-size-xs` token itself is unchanged.
- Every 44 px hit-area rule stays inside its `(pointer: coarse)` block, and
  on a fine pointer the switch, slider, Pause, touch toolbar and `#hud-tap`
  keep their fine-pointer sizes and display.
- At 1024 × 768 with a fine pointer, in both themes, the Classic 2D overlay
  bar (time, mines, Clicks readouts and Pause on the panel's top edge)
  stays on one line inside the panel and above the board, and the pause
  card fits the viewport with no scrolling and no clipped or wrapped key
  cap row.
- **Tests first**, before the CSS moves:
  - `tests/components.test.mjs`'s "coarse pointers get … inside (pointer:
    coarse) only" and "on a fine pointer every size the coarse rules touch
    is as it was" tests are rewritten: the 12 px label and 11 px key cap
    are asserted on the top-level rules, the 44 px hit areas still only in
    the coarse blocks; the browser test "switches, sliders, labels and key
    caps reach their coarse sizes" gains (or a sibling test adds) a
    fine-pointer page where the label measures 12 px and a key cap ≥ 11 px.
  - A browser test at 1024 × 768 with a fine pointer measures the Classic
    2D overlay bar and the pause card as above.
  These fail until the CSS changes.
- `.claude/domain/features/screen-components.md` states the agreed design
  change — labels at least 12 px and key caps at least 11 px on every
  pointer; only the 44 px touch targets are coarse-pointer only — in every
  passage that stated the old one (§ Interfaces, "Accessibility": "On a
  coarse pointer, and only there, … labels are at least 12 px and key caps
  at least 11 px; a fine pointer keeps every size"). Nothing else in the
  document changes.
- `.claude/context/app-shell-markup.md` ("Coarse pointers" bullet and its
  title/cross-reference wording) describes the new split.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, `2026-10-15`):** the low-vision
  part of P-45 (stat labels ≥ 12 px, key caps ≥ 11 px) applies on every
  pointer, where `.claude/domain/features/screen-components.md`
  § Interfaces, "Accessibility" limited it to a coarse pointer ("a fine
  pointer keeps every size"). The approval condition "degrades nothing on
  normal pointers" is met: one more pixel does not degrade a mouse layout.
  The 44 px hit areas stay coarse-pointer only. `screen-components.md` is
  normally `/architect`'s; this task updates it for exactly this and for
  nothing else. A further design decision met at implementation time is
  not covered: leave that passage untouched and name it under *Follow-ups*
  as a precise follow-up.
- The fit check at 1024 × 768 is the guard for "degrades nothing": if the
  bar or pause card does not fit at the larger sizes, that is a finding to
  report, not a reason to shrink other text.
