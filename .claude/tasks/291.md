# Task 291 — Game windows against the available area

Target: claude

## Goal
For feature `in-game-layout`: the Classic 2D, Hexagon and Triangle game
window measures its board's fit in the available area (task 289) less its
chrome, instead of the window at its largest, so the window keeps hugging
its board when the desktop window is sized to it. 3D play keeps filling the
whole available area.

## Acceptance criteria
- `ui.sizeBoard2D` measures the room the chrome leaves in the available
  area, not in the window; the board takes the board scale's tile at 100 %
  and 125 % and the largest tile the area holds at Fit; a board that does
  not fit keeps the floor and scrolls, as today.
- On the desktop host, a game window laid out against a work area larger
  than the window gets the same board extent it gets in a browser window
  of the work area's size.
- 3D play, the Learn 3D layout and the 3D ready card fill the available
  area, as today in a browser window.
- `.claude/domain/features/in-game-layout.md` § Architecture states that
  3D play's natural size is the available area, and settles nothing else.
- The flat modes' and the 3D layout's existing browser tests pass, with a
  test of the board extent measured in an available area larger than the
  window.

## Decisions
- **3D play fills the available area** (owner decision, 2026-10-26): the
  scene is immersive, so on the desktop the window takes the whole work
  area during 3D play. Settled here: in-game-layout.md leaves 3D play's
  size unstated; this task records it in § Architecture and changes
  nothing else in the document.
