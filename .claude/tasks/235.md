# Task 235 — 3D ready card, HUD readouts and the touch stick

Target: claude

## Goal
Bring the 3D ready card, the in-game HUD readouts and the touch stick in
line with the *Joyful tiles* canvas (Claude Design artifact
https://claude.ai/artifact/LeP32jxNxEnHLUtRwJ3FNC, page "Joyful tiles").
Part of the canvas drift fixes; feature: `shared-screen-layout`
(the ready card) with the HUD parts of `in-game-layout`. Drift ids: G3,
G4, G7, G8, G10, G11, G13, G14, G15, G16, G19, K35 (B33, G27).

## Acceptance criteria
- **Ready card size (G11).** 640 wide at xl, 580 at l, 560 at m, body
  padding 24px 26px; phone 360 wide, padding 16px 18px.
- **Phone landscape (G3).** No Back button; the left column holds the h2
  "Tap to play" (24px), the board line, the Tap to play button and the
  Learn line; the right column holds only the touch rows (no "How 3D
  works" panel); the card is about 240px tall.
- **Icons (G4).** Back arrow-left (18px); Click to play a mouse icon
  (24px), its touch version a hand icon; Learn 3D a cube icon; Play with
  drag-to-look a hand icon.
- **How 3D works (G7).** Padding 12px 14px, gap 6, lines 15px, "hold Ctrl"
  as plain text (no kbd); touch line "hold the Ctrl button to show them".
- **Learn line (G8).** Plain success-fill box, radius 12, padding 8px 8px
  8px 14px, text 15px bold (not a `ui-banner` with a teal stripe).
- **Board line (G10).** JetBrains Mono 14px/700.
- **Drag-to-look (G13).** md button with a hand icon; note "Or connect a
  controller and press A." plain muted 15px, the A as text (no pad kbd).
- **Aimed-cell readout (G14).** Outline hexagon-cube and outline flag
  icons, 14px, `currentColor`; gap 6, padding 4px 9px; placed at left
  calc(50% + 22px), top calc(50% + 18px).
- **Crosshair (G15).** Arms only when a cell is targeted — no ring.
- **Off-screen arrow (G16).** 40×40 svg at the crosshair box's left 44px,
  top -6px.
- **HUD key strip (G19).** Labels in ink, not --color-ink-muted.
- **Touch stick (K35).** Always drawn at rest: base 130px (110 on ls),
  `rgba(255,255,255,.22)` with an inset 3px `rgba(255,255,255,.55)` ring,
  knob 56px, at left 24 / bottom 28 (18 on ls); it follows the thumb
  while used. The gallery stick matches.
- Ready, HUD and touch tests updated; renders match the artboards.
- `.claude/context/app-shell-3d.md` and `app-shell-3d-play.md` reflect the
  card and HUD.
