# Task 82 — Records 2D | 3D switch and the 3D board picker

Target: claude

## Goal
Open the Records screen to 3D boards for feature
`3d-results-records-screens`: a 2D | 3D segmented choice above the board
picker, the picker listing the chosen mode's boards with 3D labels, and the
figures panel showing the chosen board's and the chosen mode's overall
figures.

## Acceptance criteria
- A 2D | 3D segmented choice from the component kit sits above the board
  picker; the picker lists the chosen mode's boards from the mode-filtered
  listing, with 3D boards under their 3D labels.
- The figures panel shows the chosen board's figures and the chosen mode's
  overall figures; the history chart, the recent games list and the library
  view read the chosen board.
- Open takes an optional mode beside the optional board key; a key alone
  sets the switch from the key's mode. Without either, the screen opens on
  the mode and board of the last game played.
- The results screen's *Records* opens on 3D and this game's board for a 3D
  game.
- Mouse, keyboard and controller operate the switch; Node tests cover the
  open rules and the mode switch with a fake records store.
