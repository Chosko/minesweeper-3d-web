# Task 232 — First-use hint strip and the Tap: Reveal / Flag segmented choice

Target: claude

## Goal
The *Joyful tiles* canvas (Claude Design artifact
https://claude.ai/artifact/LeP32jxNxEnHLUtRwJ3FNC, page "Joyful tiles")
overlays the first-use hint strip on the board column without refitting
the board, boxes every input, and draws the touch Reveal / Flag control as
a two-option segmented choice under the board. Shipped refits the board,
uses separators and a single latched toggle. Part of the canvas drift
fixes; feature: `in-game-layout`. Drift ids: K31 (F3, F13, F14,
F15, G17, G18), K4 (B3, F10).

## Acceptance criteria
- **Hint strip (K31).** Absolute inside the board column at bottom 32px,
  over the board; the board keeps its size (no `c2d--hint` refit; tile 42/4
  at xl and 28/3 at l, m with the strip shown, as without it). 15px bold,
  padding 7px 8px 7px 18px, gap 14, a 30px close button with a stroked X
  svg. Every input is a key box — including "Arrows", "D-pad", "Left",
  "Right" — with no " · " or bullet separators. On narrow windows (the
  figures panel under the board) the strip never overlaps the figures
  panel.
- **Touch copy.** 2D: "Tap reveal · Long-press flag · Tap a number to
  chord". 3D: "Tap reveal · Long-press flag · Two-finger tap chord".
- **Tap choice (K4).** `#hud-tap` becomes a two-option segmented radiogroup
  "Tap: Reveal" / "Tap: Flag", 48px tall; the chosen option ink-filled, the
  other transparent on the sunk #fbf1df track; on a phone it sits directly
  under the board. Touch input reads the radiogroup's value.
- Hint-strip, bindings and touch-input tests updated; `[slow]` walks pass.
- `.claude/context/app-shell-help.md`, `settings-bindings.md` and
  `classic2d-input.md` reflect the strip and the Tap choice.
