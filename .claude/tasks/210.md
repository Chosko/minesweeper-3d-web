# Task 210 — 3D ready card, lock states and Learn 3D cards in the shared layout

Target: claude

## Goal
Recompose the cards shown over the 3D scene before and around play, for
feature `shared-screen-layout`, as J-Ready-xl/-l/-m/-p/-ls and
J-Ready-states draw them: the ready card (Click to play with the first-use
"How 3D works" and Learn 3D line, Tap to play on a touch device), the lock
refused and blocked-with-drag-to-look states, the generating and failed-board
cards over the box, the context-lost card, and the Learn 3D prompts and end
cards, won and lost — keeping the content and behaviour `3d-play-flow` and
`game-shell` define.

## Acceptance criteria
- Each card is composed from its artboards using catalogued components only.
- The Learn 3D end card shows the copy and actions task 207 gives it (*Play
  Cube Intro* on a win, *Try again* on a loss, *Back to board choice*), as
  J-Ready-states draws them.
- Pointer-lock requests, the refusal count, lockless and touch entry, the
  Learn 3D prompts' sequence and the card's key hints by device behave as
  before.
- Every element id, default focus and controller navigation target
  survives; 3D shell, first-use and Learn 3D tests are updated only where
  they assert the old arrangement, and pass.
- No horizontal scroll at any supported window size, the phone landscape
  size included; contrast checks pass under *Joyful tiles* over the scene.

## Decisions
- **Gap closed** — the canvas draws these cards (J-Ready-*) and no task
  built them in the shared layout; they are "as delivered" screens of the
  design (DESIGN-CHANGES §4).
- The 3D in-game overlay itself is task 177's.
