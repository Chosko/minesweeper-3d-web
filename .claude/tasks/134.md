# Task 134 — 2D board scale, framed panel, cursor ring

Target: claude

## Goal
Size the Classic 2D board for the window it is played in, frame it, and stop
the keyboard cursor ring from reading as a marked cell. This is row W-08 of
`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan (Area: Classic 2D, Size:
Medium, Phase 2). It closes two rows of that document. P-14 (Major): board
scale is wrong at both ends. `MIN_TILE_SIZE` is 41 px, so Expert needs
1230 × 656 px: at 1366 × 650 three rows are off-screen and the board scrolls
mid-game; at 1024 × 768 six columns are cut flush with no scrollbar, shadow or
hint; the replay viewer puts its controls below the board and scrolls Expert
even at 1280 × 800; and at 1280 × 800 Beginner tiles are 139 px, far from the
24 to 40 px that Beginner grinders play at. P-15 (Minor): the keyboard cursor
ring is drawn on cell (0,0) from the first frame for mouse players, and
through whole replays that never used the keyboard. Decision 10 of § Decisions
taken settles the target: "Smallest window Expert must fit: 1024 px wide,
tile floor about 26 px, a board-scale setting". The work serves enthusiasts
playing Expert on laptops and small windows, Beginner grinders who want small
tiles, and every 2D player (mouse, keyboard or controller), in play and in the
replay viewer.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Tile floor** (P-14, decision 10): `MIN_TILE_SIZE` is 26 px, re-measured
  as the tile an Expert board (30 × 16) needs to fit a 1024 × 768 window
  inside the framed panel described below, with 16 px (`--space-4`)
  margins. A board shrinks its tile down to this floor before it scrolls.
  Every tile state and number stays legible at the floor in both themes:
  the browser legibility check in `tests/tile-skin.test.mjs` runs at the
  new floor and passes (closed and flagged edges keep
  `max(2, round(size / 12))`).
- **Expert fits** (P-14, decision 10): at the default scale, Expert fits
  with no scrolling and no clipped cell at 1024 × 768, 1280 × 800 and
  1366 × 650, and in any window 1024 px wide whose height leaves room for
  16 rows at the floor.
- **Default tile capped near 48 px** (P-14): at the default scale the tile
  is the largest whole size that fits the area, at most 48 px and at least
  the floor. At 1280 × 800 Beginner plays at 48 px tiles, not 139 px.
- **Framed panel** (P-14): in Classic 2D the board sits centred inside a
  fixed, framed panel sized to the board at its tile, centred in the area
  below the window's top margin. The panel carries on its top edge,
  Minesweeper-style, the time, mines left and Pause (`#hud-pause`, its
  click, the controller's Start / Back through `padClick` and its
  `aria-label` unchanged), plus the live click counter of task 128 when
  that task has landed (see Decisions). The board-size label goes with
  them. These readouts keep their formats (`formatOverlayTime`,
  `formatMineCount`, `.negative`). In 2D the full-width overlay bar over
  the board is replaced by this edge; the 3D overlay bar and HUD are
  unchanged. When the board is clipped, the panel fills the area and the
  board scrolls inside it. The panel and its frame are drawn in theme
  tokens, correct in light and dark.
- **Board-scale setting** (P-14, decision 10): a new setting `boardScale`
  in `js/settings/schema.js`, a choice of Fit, 100 % and 125 %, default
  100 %, shown as a segmented control in the Settings page's Graphics
  section, saved with the other settings and so remembered per profile.
  100 % is the capped fit above (cap 48 px). 125 % raises the cap to 60 px.
  Fit removes the cap and fills the area. In all three the tile shrinks to
  the floor before the board scrolls. A change applies at once to an open
  board and to the replay viewer, with a refit and full repaint. Adding the
  key needs no `SETTINGS_VERSION` bump unless the stored shape requires one.
- **Clipped board affordances** (P-14): when a board does not fit at the
  floor (a large custom board, or a window below the Expert size), the view
  draws an inset shadow on each edge beyond which cells are hidden (it
  disappears when scrolled to that edge), and a thin scroll position bar
  for each overflowing axis showing the visible part's size and position.
  The first time a clipped board is shown, a one-time hint "Wheel scrolls ·
  Shift+wheel sideways" appears beside the board. Once dismissed, or after
  the player scrolls, it never shows again: the seen state persists through
  platform storage (see Decisions). A board that fits draws none of the
  three. The wheel, Shift+wheel and Shift+drag panning work exactly as
  today.
- **Replay viewer** (P-14): the 2D viewer fits the board in the area
  between its overlay bar and its controls panel, measured from the
  controls' real height, not a fixed offset (`#replay-board`'s
  `bottom: 200px`), with the same floor, cap and scale setting as play.
  Expert fits with no scrolling at 1280 × 800 and 1024 × 768. The viewer
  never scrolls a board that fits: the follow-the-pointer / cursor
  `ensureVisible` runs only when the layout scrolls. A clipped board in the
  viewer shows the shadow and the scroll bar as in play.
- **Cursor ring on demand in play** (P-15): the ring is not drawn when a
  game starts. It shows on the cursor's cell after the first cursor key
  (a direction, reveal, flag or chord key) or controller input (d-pad,
  stick past `STICK_THRESHOLD`, a mapped button). It hides on the next
  mouse action on the board (a button press, or the pointer moving over
  the canvas), and shows again on the next cursor or controller input. The
  cursor keeps its cell while hidden. Hiding or showing redraws only the
  ring's cell. The ring's colour token (`CURSOR_RING`) is unchanged.
- **Cursor ring on demand in the viewer** (P-15): a replay shows the ring
  only while the recorded player was using the cursor. A game recorded
  after this change records the cursor as none (-1, the movement stream's
  existing "none" value) whenever the ring hides, and its cell when it
  shows, with no format version change. A replay recorded before this change
  (`tests/fixtures/replays/`) shows no ring until its first cursor event
  after the recording's opening one. In both cases the ring state at any
  seek position is the same as in straight playback. A replay of a
  mouse-only game shows no ring at any point.
- **Tests first**: `tests/classic2d-view.test.mjs` pins the floor, the cap
  per scale, the fit at the three window sizes above (Expert) and
  1280 × 800 (Beginner at 48 px), the panel geometry, the clipped edges
  and scroll bar presence, and ring visibility redraws.
  `tests/classic2d-cursor.test.mjs` and `tests/classic2d-mode.test.mjs` pin
  the ring shown by cursor and controller input and hidden by a mouse
  action, the setting change refitting a live board, and the one-time
  hint. `tests/replay-2d.test.mjs` pins the -1 recorded while hidden.
  `tests/replay-viewer.test.mjs` pins the viewer's ring rule (new and old
  recordings, seeking) and no `ensureVisible` on a board that fits.
  `tests/settings-store.test.mjs` and `tests/settings-page.test.mjs` pin
  the new key, its default, validation and its control.
  `tests/tile-skin.test.mjs` pins `MIN_TILE_SIZE` and legibility at it.
  A Playwright run at 1024 × 768, 1366 × 650 and 1280 × 800, in both
  themes, checks Expert unclipped in play and in the viewer, Beginner at
  48 px, the panel edge readouts and Pause, a clipped custom board with
  shadow, bar and hint, and no ring for a mouse player.
- **What not to break** (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Decisions taken, "What not
  to break"): release-to-act with drag-to-cancel, the reference input
  conventions, 3BV, click and efficiency accounting, Shift+drag panning
  and wheel scrolling on a clipped board, the board hidden while paused,
  the replay format's existing replays and fixtures decoding and verifying
  as before.
- The owned documents state the agreed design change, in every passage that
  stated the old one: the 26 px floor measured against a 1024 px wide window,
  the capped default tile and the board-scale setting, the framed panel
  carrying the time, mines and Pause in 2D, the clipped-board affordances,
  the ring shown only after cursor or controller input in play and in the
  viewer, and the viewer fitting above its controls. They also settle the
  points listed under Decisions. The documents are
  `.claude/domain/features/square-tile-skin.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/classic-2d-square-play.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/settings.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/replay-playback.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/screen-components.md` and
  `.claude/domain/features/replay-recording.md`. Nothing else in those
  documents changes.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction, P-14, P-15 and decision 10 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md`, approved
  without condition under § Approved scope and work plan (row W-08).
  - `square-tile-skin.md` § Minimum tile size (41 px, measured for Expert
    at 1366 × 768 and 1280 × 800): the floor is about 26 px, measured so
    Expert fits a window 1024 px wide.
  - `classic-2d-square-play.md` § Architecture, "Board view" (largest tile
    that fits, down to the minimum): the default tile is capped near
    48 px, set by a board-scale setting, with the board centred in a
    framed panel and clipped boards marked by shadow, scroll bar and a
    one-time hint. § Architecture, "Cursor input" (the cursor drawn as a
    focus ring): the ring shows only after cursor or controller input and
    hides on the next mouse action.
  - `settings.md` § Scope and § Data and state (the list of settings): the
    board-scale setting (Fit, 100 %, 125 %) joins Graphics and the saved
    settings.
  - `replay-playback.md` § Architecture, "2D viewer" (the recorded cursor
    drawn as the focus ring): the ring shows only while the recording used
    the cursor; the board fits above the controls and is never scrolled
    when it fits.
  - `screen-components.md` § Architecture, "Overlay bar" (one component
    that sits over either board): in Classic 2D the time, mines and Pause
    sit on the framed panel's edge.

  Settled here, without conflict: `replay-recording.md` § Architecture,
  "Movement samplers" (the cursor's cell whenever it moves) records "none"
  while the ring is hidden. These documents are normally `/architect`'s;
  this task updates them for exactly this and for nothing else. A further
  design decision met at implementation time is not covered: leave that
  passage untouched and name it under *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
- **What the three scales mean**: P-14 caps "the default tile near 48 px"
  and names the setting's values without defining them. Here 100 % is that
  default (cap 48 px), 125 % scales the cap by 1.25 (60 px), and Fit drops
  the cap. In each the tile still shrinks to the floor before the board
  clips. So the setting changes how large a small board grows and never
  makes a board scroll that could fit, which keeps P-14's "Expert fits any
  window 1024 px wide" true at every scale.
- **"Remembered per profile"** is the settings document: the game has one
  local profile, so the scale is saved with the other settings through the
  settings store and needs no storage of its own.
- **The viewer follows the same setting and floor**: it mounts the same
  board view, and P-14 asks it to fit "at the smaller tile". It keeps its
  own overlay bar of replay figures and its controls panel; the framed
  panel is play's.
- **Click counter**: task 128 adds the live "Clicks" readout to the overlay
  bar. Neither task waits on the other. Whichever lands second puts the
  readout on the panel edge beside the time and mines.
- **One-time hint storage**: task 119 owns the first-use hint strips and
  their platform-storage document. If task 119 has landed, the scroll
  hint's seen flag is an entry in its first-use document and uses its
  strip. Otherwise it is a small versioned platform-storage document of
  its own, following `createLastChoice` in `js/classic2d/board-setup.js`,
  in a new `js/classic2d/scroll-hint.js`. A storage failure shows the hint
  once per session.
- **Mouse action that hides the ring**: a button press on the board or the
  pointer moving over the canvas. A pointer resting still sends nothing,
  so a keyboard player's ring stays up.
- **Ring visibility in recordings**: no new movement type and no format
  version change. The cursor event already carries -1 for none, so the
  recording states visibility by the event it already has.
- **Out of scope**: touch panning and pinch-to-zoom on the 2D board belong
  to W-22 (P-37, P-38, P-39, touch Classic 2D); the first-use hint strips
  themselves are task 119's; the click counter's figure is task 128's; the
  3D mode, its overlay bar and HUD are untouched; the replay sampling
  interval measured at the old minimum tile is not re-measured.
