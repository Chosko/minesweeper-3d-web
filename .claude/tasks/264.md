# Task 264 — The tile painter draws hexagon and triangle tiles

Target: claude

## Goal
Part of feature `hex-tri-board-art`: the Classic 2D tile painter paints a
state, number and depth for a cell of any flat geometry, through the
geometry's outline (task 250), the recipe's outlines (task 262) and the
grid's shape entries in the board module (task 263). A triangle board caches
both orientations. The cell motion is the Classic 2D motion, played around
the tile's centre.

## Acceptance criteria
- On a hexagon or triangle geometry the painter draws each state in the
  grid's outline with that grid's gap and radius, and its glyphs in the
  geometry's glyph box.
- The cache holds a hexagon board's tiles and both orientations of a
  triangle board's.
- A number outside its grid's range, an unknown state or a missing module
  paints the visible placeholder.
- Square tiles are unchanged. A browser test renders a hexagon and a
  triangle board in every state at each grid's floor.
