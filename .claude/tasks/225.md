# Task 225 — Board module motion tracks: opacity, flip, scale and brightness

Target: claude

## Goal
The *Joyful tiles* canvas (Claude Design artifact
https://claude.ai/artifact/LeP32jxNxEnHLUtRwJ3FNC, page "Joyful tiles",
artboard J-BoardSheet motion rows) animates cell motions with opacity, a
face flip, scale and brightness; the module contract and the cell-motion
player only have offset and depth, and depth draws nothing in the flat
tile style. Add the tracks and author the canvas keyframes. Part of the
canvas drift fixes; intended feature: `themed-board-art-and-motion`.
Drift ids: K28 (C7, C8, C9, C10, C11).

## Acceptance criteria
- The module contract and the cell-motion player support opacity, flip
  (the closed face shows until the flip's cut, then the after-face), scale
  and brightness tracks beside offset; offset is visible in the flat
  style.
- The *Joyful tiles* module authors the canvas keyframes (offsets as a
  fraction of the tile; 3px at a 56px tile ≈ .054):
  - **Press** 90 ms easing-out: 45 ms closed face with its band,
    translateY .04, scale .98, brightness .97; 90 ms pressed face,
    translateY .054, scale .96.
  - **Release** 140 ms easing-spring: from the pressed pose (translateY
    .054, scale .96); 90 ms translateY -.02, scale 1.03, brightness 1.04;
    rest at 140 ms.
  - **Reveal** 160 ms: 0 ms pressed pose; 60 ms opacity .6, scale .82,
    brightness 1.12; 120 ms opacity 1, brightness 1.04; rest.
  - **Flag** 180 ms: brightness 1.08 at 80 ms (offsets and scales as
    shipped). **Unflag** 120 ms: brightness 1.06 at 60 ms.
  - **Chord press** 90 ms: 0 ms closed face; 90 ms pressed face,
    translateY .054, scale .96, brightness .96.
  Durations, easings and cut times stay as shipped (they match).
- A brightness value above 1 lifts toward white, below 1 darkens.
- 3D cells honour the same tracks where the 3D renderer supports them, or
  the closing report names what 3D ignores and why.
- Motion contract and player tests cover each track; the `[slow]` motion
  flavour tests pass.
- `.claude/context/classic2d-tile-motion.md`, `classic2d-board-motion.md`
  and `app-shell-board-module.md` describe the tracks.
