# Task 251 — Cursor moves and end effect on the grid geometry

Target: claude

## Goal
Part of feature `flat-grid-geometry`: the keyboard and controller cursor
asks the geometry for the cell one step up, down, left or right, and the
Classic 2D end effect spreads its win and loss waves by distance between
cell centres, with its confetti over the board's extent. Pointer and touch
inputs reach cells only through the view's hit-testing (task 249). Classic 2D
plays exactly as it does now.

## Acceptance criteria
- The cursor input moves by the geometry's neighbour query, stops where it
  answers none, and keeps its bindings, auto-repeat, focus ring and rumble
  cues.
- The end effect's waves are timed by the distance between cell centres from
  the geometry, and its confetti covers the geometry's extent. On the square
  geometry every reach time and confetti piece equals today's.
- Pointer and touch inputs read no column or row arithmetic of their own.
- The Classic 2D cursor, pointer, touch and end-effect tests pass unchanged.
