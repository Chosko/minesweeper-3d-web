# Task 294 — Frame size steps follow the root area's height as well as its width

Target: claude

## Goal
For feature `steam-desktop-host`: the desktop game window — the screen's
frame at its natural size — shrinks in size steps that the root area's
width alone decides, while the theme's compact title bar (and the in-game
readouts and the Classic 2D window's padding) also switch to compact when
the root area's height drops below 820 px. On a wide but short work area the
window is therefore a desktop-step frame wearing a compact title bar. The
frame's size steps and the title bar must agree: the frame's steps take the
root area's height into account too, so a short work area gets the compact
step's frame, not only its title bar.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every `@container root` rule that picks the frame's compact step — the
  screens' natural sizes (menu, board choices, Daily, Records, Settings,
  replay viewer, results, pause card, ready card, placeholder) and the frame's
  own metrics — takes it when the root area is below the compact step's
  width **or** below 820 px high, the same condition the theme's compact
  title bar already uses, so no frame shows the desktop step's size with the
  compact step's title bar.
- At a root area of desktop-step width and under 820 px of height, every
  screen's frame is its compact-step natural size and fits the area; at a
  root area of desktop-step width and 820 px or more of height nothing
  changes.
- The width-only steps below the compact one (the 1199 px step and the
  narrower layouts) are unchanged.
- A test asserts that the frame's compact natural sizes and the compact
  title bar switch on the same height condition.
- `.claude/domain/features/shared-screen-layout.md` § Architecture,
  **Size steps**, states that the compact step is chosen from the available
  area's height as well as its width, and settles nothing else. Nothing else
  in the document changes.

## Decisions
- **Kept for later, now a backlog task** (owner decision): the mismatch was
  found while fixing the desktop window's size and corners and deliberately
  left out of that fix.
- **Direction: the frame follows the title bar**, not the reverse — the item
  as approved says the frame's steps account for height too; the title bar's
  820 px height condition stays.
- Settled here: `shared-screen-layout.md` § Size steps says the desktop and
  compact steps are chosen from the available area without saying along
  which dimension; this task settles that the compact step is taken on
  height too. `shared-screen-layout.md` is normally `/architect`'s; this task
  updates it for exactly this and for nothing else.
