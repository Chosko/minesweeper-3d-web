# Task 52 — 3D rule profile: revealed-cell flagging, chord, auto-hide and win

Target: claude

## Goal
Add the 3D rule profile of feature `3d-board-graph` to the engine's
rule-profile table: right-click on a revealed cell flags or unflags its
neighbours, chord on a satisfied number, auto-hiding (unlinking) of solved
cells, and the 3D win rule — with the 3D engine's rule tests ported to it.

## Acceptance criteria
- The 3D profile ships as its own entry, version 1, in the engine's profile
  table; the reference profile's behaviour is unchanged.
- Toggle flag on a revealed cell flags every unrevealed neighbour, or
  unflags them all when every one is already flagged.
- Chord acts on a revealed cell whose flagged-neighbour count equals its
  number, revealing every unflagged, unrevealed neighbour; a zero cell's
  flood fill stops at a cell with a flagged neighbour.
- Auto-hide: a revealed, unflagged cell whose number is satisfied and whose
  every neighbour is revealed, flagged or hidden becomes hidden; a zero cell
  with no flagged neighbour is hidden at once; unflagging a cell un-hides its
  neighbours. Hidden is a per-cell state the profile maintains after every
  action and reports among the changed cells; the reference profile never
  sets it.
- Win when every safe cell is revealed and no safe cell is flagged; flags on
  mines are not required.
- No action changes anything after the game ends.
- Counts: a right-click on a revealed cell is one flag click, however many
  neighbours it flags; 3BV and 3BV solved are computed over the box graph.
- Every rule case in `tests/logic.test.mjs` is ported to the 3D profile on
  fixed mine sets; the cases the feature document lists as deliberate
  differences (post-game actions, mine cap) are rewritten to the new rule.
- Actions on a large box allocate nothing per cell; flood fill and hide
  propagation are iterative.
