Question:
The no-guess limit was meant to stop custom 3D boards that are too big to generate a no-guess board in time. Measuring it (dev/measure-noguess-3d.mjs, Node 22) showed the premise does not hold: at the presets' densities (up to 16 %) bigger boards need no more tries than small ones — every request from 10 × 10 × 10 up to 70 × 70 × 70 succeeded within a few dozen of the 2000 allowed tries; only small dense boards (4 × 4 × 4 to 6 × 6 × 6 at 15 %) need hundreds. What grows with size is time: the slowest successful request is about 0.3 s at 1,000 cells, 0.5 s at 8,000 (20 × 20 × 20), 1 s at 27,000 (30 × 30 × 30), 2–3 s at 125,000–343,000. A request that fails (too dense) waits out all 2000 tries: 2–40 s at 1,000–125,000 cells, about 5–6 minutes at 1,000,000. So "a board at the limit answers within the attempt budget" holds at every size, and the limit has to be set by a wait instead.

A second finding: no box with an axis of exactly 2 cells can ever be a no-guess board — two stacked cells share every neighbour, so a mine in one is always a coin flip (8 × 8 × 2: 0 of 50 seeds, at every density). That includes the three double-layer presets, which the design says offer no-guess; a no-guess game on them always ends in the failure offer after seconds of waiting.

Q1. What should the no-guess cell-count limit be set by?
  a. A short successful wait: 8,000 cells (20 × 20 × 20), the slowest request at preset densities about 0.5 s.
  b. A one-second successful wait: 27,000 cells (30 × 30 × 30).
  c. No limit: no-guess on every custom board (1,000,000 cells), relying on the generating state and the failure offer — the disabled switch never shows; a design change to the feature document.
  Recommendation: a.

Q2. What about boards with a 2-cell axis, double-layer presets included?
  a. Leave them to a follow-up (`/architect amend feature=3d-play-flow`) and keep this task to the cell-count limit.
  b. Also disable no-guess on any board with a 2-cell axis in this task — a design change beyond the task's scope.
  Recommendation: a.
