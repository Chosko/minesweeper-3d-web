# Task 78 — 3D replay viewer

Target: claude

## Goal
Play 3D replays in the viewer of feature `replay-playback`: the 3D renderer
over the simulator's 3D state view, with the camera placed from the recorded
position and direction, interpolated between samples, and the recorded view
mode applied.

## Acceptance criteria
- A 3D replay opens the 3D scene in the viewer, drawn from the simulator's
  3D state view; the scene's look is unchanged.
- The camera follows the recorded position, yaw and pitch, interpolated
  between samples, and applies the recorded view mode; player camera input
  is ignored.
- After the point where the replay's movement was capped, the camera holds
  its last recorded position.
- A lost graphics context is reported as `game-shell` reports a mode's
  failure; Back still works.
- Node tests cover the camera interpolation and the cap hold; Playwright
  plays a recorded 3D replay from fixture.
