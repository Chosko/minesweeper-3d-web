# Task 252 — 2D movement sampler and replay viewer in board units

Target: claude

## Goal
Part of feature `flat-grid-geometry`: the 2D movement sampler records
pointer samples in the geometry's board units, and the 2D replay viewer
draws the pointer, its trail, the cursor ring, press rings and Show 3BV
tints through the geometry. It rebuilds the geometry from the replay's board
rather than assuming a square grid. On the square geometry board units are
pitches, so every recorded Classic 2D replay plays back unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria
- `boardPoint` and the sampler quantise board units from the view's
  geometry. On the square geometry the samples are byte-identical to
  today's.
- The 2D viewer's pointer pixel, trail, press rings, cursor and Show 3BV
  tints come from the geometry's centres, outlines and board units. The
  tints fill each tile's outline.
- The recorded replays in `tests/fixtures/replays/` decode, verify and draw
  as before; replay tests pass unchanged.
