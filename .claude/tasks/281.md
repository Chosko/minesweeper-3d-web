# Task 281 — Button colour rule: go and stop buttons

Target: claude

## Goal
For feature `shared-screen-layout` (§ Scope and non-goals, **Button
colours** and the button variants): give the kit the green *go* and red
*stop* primary buttons and apply the one colour rule on every screen — a
button that confirms or goes forward is green, never red; red is only for
Quit and the destructive confirmations; Pause is always the white secondary
button; an on/off toggle shows its on state with the pressed look. Exact
sizes and colours come from the canvas
https://claude.ai/artifact/LeP32jxNxEnHLUtRwJ3FNC — J-Components *Buttons*,
"confirm (primary go)", "Confirm · j-btn--primary j-btn--go" and the
title-bar Quit — and every screen artboard.

## Acceptance criteria
- The kit catalogue has the go and stop variants of the primary button,
  with their parts and states, styled by *Joyful tiles*: both rest in a
  stronger pastel and take their full colour when selected; the theme
  conformance tests cover them.
- Green: Play (every board choice and Daily), Play again, Resume, Restart
  on the pause card, Retry, Try again, Reload, Click / Tap to play, Play
  Cube Intro, Play with drag-to-look and the replay viewer's Play.
- Red only for Quit and the destructive confirmations — leave the game,
  restart, delete a replay, reset all data.
- Pause is the white secondary button everywhere, the replay viewer's
  play / pause button included while it shows Pause.
- The latched toggles — Swap clicks, the touch view modes and the 3D Space
  latch, Show 3BV, Free camera, the panel toggles — are quiet when off and
  show the pressed look when on, no longer a primary look.
- No confirm button carries an *Enter* key label; *Esc* on Resume, *Space*
  on the replay controls and the controller glyphs of the controller-hints
  frames stay. The large confirm buttons are slimmer, as the canvas draws
  them.
- Contrast of each new variant's ink on its rest and selected colours meets
  the contrast contract; the component gallery draws the new variants
  beside the canvas's components artboard.

## Decisions
- **First of the four layout tasks** — the title bar's Quit and the board
  choice's Play use these variants, so they land first.
