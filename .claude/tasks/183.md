# Task 183 — 3D cubes, numbers and sky drawn from the active theme's board module

Target: claude

## Goal
Draw the 3D scene in the active theme, for feature
`themed-board-art-and-motion`: the tile atlas is built from the board module's
palette and style — every state, number 0–26 and the sunk closed face the
depth ingredient blends towards — and rebuilt on a theme change; the sky
gradient, fog and clear colour come from the board module's sky; the selection
outline and the marked start cell's tint from its palette. The
`--color-scene-*` tokens retire.

## Acceptance criteria
- The renderer takes a board module and rebuilds its atlas and sky on
  receiving one; it never imports a theme or reads a stylesheet; it holds no
  palette of its own.
- The atlas has one layer per state and per number 0–26 plus the sunk closed
  face, drawn through the recipe with the theme's style; the ground grid keeps
  deriving its ink from the horizon.
- Fog defaults to the horizon when the module gives none.
- `js/shell/scene-palette.js` hands the board module's sky instead of reading
  tokens; the `--color-scene-*` tokens are removed from every theme stylesheet
  and the conformance required-token list, and the tests that pinned them
  follow.
- End-of-game waves, confetti and shake, and the flag-click flash keep their
  behaviour, drawn in the theme's palette.
- A browser test switches theme in a 3D game and sees the atlas and sky change
  with no state change; 3D readability and feedback tests pass.
