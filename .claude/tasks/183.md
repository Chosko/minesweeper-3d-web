# Task 183 — 3D cubes, numbers and sky drawn from the active theme's board module

Target: claude

## Goal
Draw the 3D scene in the active theme, for feature
`themed-board-art-and-motion`: the tile atlas is generated from the board
module's palette and 3D style — for *Joyful tiles* the bevel cubes of
J-3DSheet — every state, number 0–26 and the sunk closed face the depth
ingredient blends towards, and rebuilt on a theme change; the sky gradient,
fog and clear colour come from the board module's sky; the selection outline
and the marked start cell's tint from its palette. The `--color-scene-*`
tokens retire.

## Acceptance criteria
- The renderer takes a board module and rebuilds its atlas and sky on
  receiving one; it never imports a theme or reads a stylesheet; it holds no
  palette of its own.
- The atlas has one layer per state and per number 0–26 plus the sunk closed
  face, in the layer order it has today, drawn through the recipe with the
  theme's 3D style — never a shipped picture; the ground grid keeps deriving
  its ink from the horizon. Under *Joyful tiles* closed and flagged cubes show
  the edge and four lit sides, revealed cubes are flat inside their edge, and
  a test compares sampled atlas pixels with J-3DSheet's values. The slope is
  painted into the texture, so it reads lit from the same corner on every
  face, top faces included.
- Fog defaults to the horizon when the module gives none.
- `js/shell/scene-palette.js` hands the board module's sky instead of reading
  tokens; the `--color-scene-*` tokens are removed from every theme stylesheet
  and the conformance required-token list, and the tests that pinned them
  follow.
- End-of-game waves, confetti and shake, and the flag-click flash keep their
  behaviour, drawn in the theme's palette.
- A browser test switches theme in a 3D game and sees the atlas and sky change
  with no state change; 3D readability and feedback tests pass.

## Decisions
- **Owner decision** (DESIGN-CHANGES §3.4, §5): "Bevel 5 %" cubes replace
  the first delivery's closed tile; the game generates the atlas, the canvas
  PNG being the reference; the painted slope's lighting on top faces is
  accepted.
- **Settled design detail**
  (`.claude/domain/features/themed-board-art-and-motion.md` § Architecture,
  *3D tile atlas and scene*): the scene palette module's new input. The
  feature document is not edited here; task 187 records what holds at ship
  time.
