# Task 259 — Triangle geometry

Target: claude

## Goal
Part of feature `triangle-grid`: the triangle implementation of the flat
grid geometry contract (task 248). Alternating triangles sized by their side
sit a half side apart along a row and one triangle height apart between rows
(plus the gap), each outline pointing the cell's way.

## Acceptance criteria
- The geometry answers every query of the contract for a `tri` board: the
  board's extent, each cell's centre, outline with its orientation (up when
  c + r is even) and glyph box centred on the centroid, the cell at a board
  point, and the floor and the tile size at each board scale.
- Hit-testing follows the triangle outline: every point inside the board's
  outline belongs to exactly one cell, and a press in a gap goes to the
  nearest tile.
- The floor is the feature document's value a side. The tile size at each
  board scale gives a glyph matching the square's at the floor and at the
  cap.
- Cursor moves: left and right along the row; up and down to the same
  column in the row above or below; none at the board's edge.
- Registered under the `tri` kind in `js/geometry/`, DOM-free, tested in the
  fast tier.
