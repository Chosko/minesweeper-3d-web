# Task 287 — 3D game overlay without a title: floating Pause and live-figures toggle

Target: claude

## Goal
For feature `in-game-layout` (§ Scope and non-goals, **3D game** and
**Learn 3D**; § Architecture, **3D overlay**): the 3D game shows no title
anywhere — the mode and difficulty label is removed. Pause floats at the
top left of the scene and the live-figures toggle at the top right, the
counters and the icon-only Restart stay at the top centre, and the live
figures panel is hidden by default and appears on the right when opened,
under the same stored preference as Classic 2D's. Exact sizes, colours and
positions come from the canvas
https://claude.ai/artifact/LeP32jxNxEnHLUtRwJ3FNC — J-Game3D and J-Ready,
each at -xl, -l, -m, -p, -ls and -states (each states board's second frame
shows the panel open).

## Acceptance criteria
- No mode or difficulty label in the 3D overlay, its figures panel head
  bar or its phone layouts; the title-fitting rule goes with it.
- Pause floats at the top left and the live-figures toggle (task 286's
  component, sunk while open) at the top right; the flags counter, the
  icon-only Restart and the timer stay at the top centre.
- The live figures panel is hidden by default and appears on the right
  when opened, its open state the stored preference task 286 adds; the
  help panel keeps its place beside it.
- On a phone, in landscape and in Learn 3D, only the floating Pause
  remains of those controls; Learn 3D keeps its mines-and-Restart strip and
  prompt strip.
- The 3D ready card matches J-Ready at every size.
- Hooks and focus keep their ids or move with their owners; keyboard,
  controller and touch reach Pause and the toggle.
- The 3D game moving inside the window with the title bar is not part of
  this task.
