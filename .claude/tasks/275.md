# Task 275 — Playback of Hexagon and Triangle replays

Target: claude

## Goal
Part of feature `flat-grid-records-replays`: Hexagon and Triangle replays
play back in the replay viewer on their own grid. The simulator and verifier
run over the new graphs, and the 2D viewer mounts the board view over the
geometry it rebuilds from the header's graph description.

## Acceptance criteria
- `openReplay`, seeking with engine-state snapshots and the verifier work on
  `hex` and `tri` replays, deterministically.
- The 2D viewer draws the board through the grid's geometry and tiles: the
  pointer and trail, the cursor ring, press rings, cell motion on played
  frames and none on a seek, Show 3BV tints in the tile's outline, and the
  end line. The viewer screen's title and strip name the mode.
- The replay library keeps retention, best and hand pinning and the library
  view by board key for both modes; the Records screen's Replays tab watches
  them.
- Browser tests record a Hexagon and a Triangle game, then watch, seek and
  verify each.
