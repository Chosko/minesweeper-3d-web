# Task 62 — 3D summaries and the 3D mode's summary hand-off

Target: claude

## Goal
Let `game-summary`'s builder take a 3D board choice for feature
`3d-game-records`, with 3BV/s, efficiency and best eligibility derived by the
unchanged 2D rules, and switch the 3D mode adapter to report summaries built
by it.

## Acceptance criteria
- `buildSummary` accepts the 3D board choice and the engine's summary or
  counts over the box graph, the elapsed time, the seed and the generator
  version; invalid 3D input (dimensions, mines, counts that do not fit the
  board) throws a range error.
- 3BV/s, efficiency and best eligibility use the existing derivations
  unchanged; the 3D profile's right-click on a revealed cell enters the
  counts as the one flag click the profile records.
- The 3D mode adapter reports `game finished` and `game abandoned`, and
  answers `summary()`, with the summary from the builder, replacing its
  interim summary fields.
- Node tests cover won, lost and abandoned 3D summaries and a started
  game's summary so far, including a 3D efficiency case with a right-click
  on a revealed cell.
- `.claude/domain/features/3d-game-records.md` records, in place of its open
  question, that 3D efficiency applies the pinned 2D click rule to the 3D
  profile's actions and counts a right-click on a revealed cell as one flag
  click. Nothing else in the document changes.

## Decisions
- Settled here: the feature document's open question on whether the 3D
  right-click on a revealed cell counts as a click — it counts as one flag
  click, as `3d-board-graph` already states for the profile's counts, and
  efficiency applies the 2D rule pinned by task 7 to it.
  `.claude/domain/features/3d-game-records.md` is normally `/architect`'s;
  this task updates it for exactly this and for nothing else.
