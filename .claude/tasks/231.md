# Task 231 — Classic 2D window layout and HUD density

Target: claude

## Goal
Bring the Classic 2D game window in line with the *Joyful tiles* canvas
(Claude Design artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/LeP32jxNxEnHLUtRwJ3FNC,
page "Joyful tiles"). Shipped fills the viewport and floats the board in a
wide white area with a separate figures card; the canvas hugs the content,
left-aligns the board under the counter strip and draws the figures as a
flat column, with compact and touch type steps for the HUD (2D and 3D).
Part of the canvas drift fixes; feature: `in-game-layout`. Drift
ids: K34 (F1, F2, F5, F8, F11, F12), K8 (F7, F30, G21, G22, G23, G28,
G29), B29.

Sizes: xl 1920×1080; l 1366×768 and m 1024×768 compact; p 390 and ls
844×390 touch.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Window (F1).** The window hugs its content and centres: 1086×964 at
  left 417 (xl), 793×691 (l), 761×687 (m); the board column is 784 / 531 /
  527px wide and the board fills it. No fill-the-viewport width or 1600
  max-width.
- **Board placement (F2).** The board starts 12px under the counter strip,
  left-aligned, at the strip's width (740px at xl) — no centring with
  empty bands.
- **Figures column (F5).** Flat, no surface, after a 2px --color-line
  divider; width the 1fr remainder (300px at xl); padding 22px 26px (xl),
  16px 20px (l), 14px 18px (m).
- **Paddings (F8).** Main padding 16 (l) and 14 (m); counter strip padding
  8px 14px.
- **Phone (F11).** Board viewport 358×450 directly under the counter strip
  (12px gap), the Tap switch right under it, spare room at the bottom.
- **Phone landscape (F12).** Board viewport 450×286 at the left edge
  (x 12); the right column fills the rest (about 370px): mines, then time
  (clock icon before the digits), then Restart full width, then the Tap
  switch.
- **HUD density (K8).** Counter digits 34 (xl) / 28 (compact) / 26
  (touch); figure values 23px at compact including the 3BV value; figure
  padding 8px 0; figures title 15px at compact; phone titles 16px (p) and
  15px (ls pill); touch sm buttons 15px, padding 7px 13px, min-height 44
  (Restart 44 tall, not 38 with a hit band); touch view-mode buttons
  min-width 76. Applies to the 2D window and the 3D HUD.
- **Negative counter (B29).** The negative mine counter stays red
  (#b42a2a).
- Classic 2D view and HUD tests and the `[slow]` 2D walks pass; renders
  match the artboards.
- `.claude/context/app-shell-2d.md`, `classic2d-board-view.md` and
  `app-shell-3d.md` (HUD sizes) reflect the layout.
