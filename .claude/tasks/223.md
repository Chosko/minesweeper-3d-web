# Task 223 — Kit frames: head and foot steps, white action foot, message cards, scrims, status cards

Target: claude

## Goal
The *Joyful tiles* canvas (Claude Design artifact
https://claude.ai/artifact/LeP32jxNxEnHLUtRwJ3FNC, page "Joyful tiles";
classes `j-head`, `j-foot`, `j-card`, `j-scrim`, `jt--compact`,
`jt--touch`) sizes the frame head and foot per window size, draws a white
action foot on the screens (keeping the yellow foot for the game window),
and lays out message cards, scrims and board status cards differently from
the shipped kit. Fix the kit frame parts. Part of the canvas drift fixes;
intended feature: `shared-screen-layout`. Drift ids: K6 (A7, A8, A9, B30,
F6, F18, H2-3, H2-4), K7 (D22, D23, E1, E2, H1-19), K22 (A2, A3, A11,
A12, G31, H2-20), K24 (G5, G6, H1-6, H1-7, F28), K23 (B31, F19, F20,
G30).

Sizes: xl 1920×1080; l 1366×768 and m 1024×768 are the canvas's compact
step (`jt--compact`); p 390 phone portrait and ls 844×390 phone landscape
are its touch step (`jt--touch`).

## Acceptance criteria
- **Head and foot steps (K6).** Head min-height 64, padding 10px 16px, end
  gap 10 at xl; compact: 56, 8px 14px, title 18px; touch: 60, 8px 10px,
  title 17px and the title on one line (no wrap). Foot padding 8px 22px at
  15px. On touch sizes sm and md buttons have min-height 44. The game
  window's own head overrides (`css/style.css`) follow the same steps.
- **White action foot (K7).** A foot variant for screens (board choices,
  Daily, results): white surface, `border-top: 2px solid` --color-line,
  padding 14px 24px 18px (10px 20px 14px compact; the results foot is
  16px 28px 20px, 14px 22px 18px compact), summary 16px/700. On phones a
  fixed white bottom bar 92px tall with the 2px line on top and
  `0 -10px 20px -12px` shade-press shadow, actions right-aligned. The game
  window keeps the yellow foot.
- **Message cards (K22).** Card title 28px/800/line-height 1.1 (the
  Coming-soon placeholder title 36px); text ink 16px/1.4 (not muted); body
  padding 24px 26px with a 14px flex gap between icon, title, text and
  actions (instead of 16px margins). The context-lost card is 480px wide,
  "Click to reload" carries a refresh icon and Main menu has no Esc key
  hint. The replay failure card keeps the frame title (for example
  "Replay · Classic 2D · Intermediate") and leaves the speed choice, Show
  3BV and Download enabled, with figures and ticks kept.
- **Scrims (K24).** The scrim is plain --color-backdrop (.55) with no
  `backdrop-filter`; results over 3D use the same .55 (not
  --color-backdrop-strong .72). Behind the 3D ready card, the pause card
  and results, the HUD, figures panel and window chrome are hidden — only
  the board (2D) or the scene (3D) and the window's head title show.
- **Status cards (K23).** The generating and failed board cards centre on
  the board column, not the viewport; spinner 44×44 with border 5 and a
  16px gap to the title; progress line JetBrains Mono 15px muted; failure
  text ink 16px, 14px under the title; Retry with a refresh icon and Back
  with an arrow icon (task 220); in 3D, a scrim over the scene and no HUD,
  figures or hint strip behind.
- Gallery and tests updated; screens using these parts still pass their
  tests.
- `.claude/context/app-shell-kit.md`, `app-shell-ui.md` and the 3D context
  files reflect the frame steps, the foot variant and the hidden HUD.
