# Task 176 — Classic 2D game window: counter strip, side panel and foot line

Target: claude

## Goal
Build the Classic 2D game window of feature `in-game-layout` from the S3D-Game
artboard, replacing the m1 overlay bar: inside the screen frame, the head with
the mode and board title and Pause with its key hint; the counter strip with
mines left, Restart and the timer; the board in its themed frame; the side
panel of live figures with the controls reminder; and the foot line naming the
board size, mine count and no-guess.

## Acceptance criteria
- The window is built from catalogued components only.
- The counter strip's timer keeps its format and limits. The mine counter
  shows three digits with a negative count's minus in the first digit (−05),
  down to −99 and staying −99 below that (`screen-components` § Overlay bar);
  the shared formatter changes, so the 3D overlay and the replay viewer show
  the same. Restart is the shared restart with its confirmation rule
  unchanged.
- The side panel shows the live figures from task 174's model, fed whenever
  the game's counts change and on the timer tick only for the figures that
  include elapsed time; the board's best time and best 3BV/s are read from the
  records store once at game start; a DOM write happens only when a figure's
  text changes.
- The controls reminder lists the board's main bindings for the active input
  device from the bindings source and follows a device change.
- The board area keeps the fit rule, `MIN_TILE_SIZE` and scrolling; the
  window's chrome reduces the area the fit rule works in; when the window is
  too narrow for board and panel side by side, the panel moves under the
  board, and on a narrow or mobile window the panel may be hidden so the
  board keeps its room.
- The foot line names the board size, mine count and no-guess, and never a
  cell.
- The existing hooks (time, mines and clicks readouts, Pause, the board-size
  label) keep their ids or move with their owners in this change; existing
  Classic 2D tests pass, updated only where they assert the old arrangement.
- No horizontal scroll at 1024 px wide with an Expert board; contrast checks
  pass under *Joyful tiles*.
- A browser test plays a few moves on a board with a best and checks the
  figures and pace update.

## Decisions
- **Settled design detail** (`.claude/domain/features/in-game-layout.md` §
  Architecture, *Game window composition*): the narrow-window breakpoint, and
  the narrower one at which the side panel hides, chosen from the artboard,
  the 1024 px floor and phone widths. The feature document is not edited
  here; task 179 records what holds at ship time.
- **Feature document over artboard.** Where the S3D-Game artboard names the
  cursor's cell in the foot line ("cursor I5"), the feature document wins: no
  cell name.
