# Task 228 — Main menu: mode entries, scene panel, per-size layout, build label and scroll

Target: claude

## Goal
Bring the main menu in line with the *Joyful tiles* canvas (Claude Design
artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/LeP32jxNxEnHLUtRwJ3FNC, page "Joyful
tiles"; classes `j-entry`, scene panel, `jt--compact`, `jt--touch`). A
design review found the column layout, mode-entry metrics, scene panel,
phone note and build label drift from it, and the menu does not scroll on
short windows. Part of the canvas drift fixes; feature:
`shared-screen-layout`. Drift ids: K10 (B20, B21, D3, D4, D5), K25 (B23,
D10, D12, D13), the menu part of K9 (D2), D1, D9, P7 (D29), D8, D7, A20,
A19.

Sizes: xl 1920×1080, l 1366×768, m 1024×768, p 390 phone portrait.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Layout (D1).** Fixed per-size values, not fluid clamps: xl entries 590px
  wide at x=96, preview 964×804 at x=860 (174px gap); l padding 56,
  entries 450 wide, preview 748 wide, gap 56, entries top 118 and 580 tall;
  m padding 36, entries 390, preview 526, gap 36, top 106, 596 tall.
- **Mode entries (K10, D2).** Rest: min-height 70, padding 6px 20px.
  Selected: padding 10px 24px, min-height 101 at xl, height from flex
  1.45 (1.4 at l and m, 1.35 at p); remove the dead `min-height: 92px` or
  let it win over `#menu-entries > .ui-mode-entry {min-height:0}`. Label
  selected / other: 30/24 xl, 27/22 l, 25/20 m, 24/19 p. Icon box 56/42
  xl, 48/36 at l, m and p; glyph 30/24 xl, 26/20 l, 24/20 m and p. List
  gap 12 at xl, 10 at l, m, p; an extra spacer between blocks of 12 xl,
  10 l and m, 8 p (blocks 36/30/30/28 apart).
- **Scene panel (K25).** Radius 30; tag at left 26, top 22; a veil dot grid
  over the sky (`radial-gradient(circle, var(--veil) 1.4px, transparent
  1.6px)`, 22px spacing) on card previews; badge 102×102, radius 22,
  icon 50% (glyph 100px, stroke 2 on the large card); card gap 18; note
  18px/700 in --color-scene-ink; the locked badge keeps its
  `inset 0 4px 0` highlight.
- **Phone touch note (D9).** One line, 14px/700 ink, after a 20px hand icon
  with a 10px gap (shipped 16px warning colour, no icon, wraps).
- **Build label (D8).** Canvas style: JetBrains Mono 12px, weight 700,
  letter-spacing .15em, uppercase, muted; content stays the shipped version
  ("WEB · V0.2.0" once uppercased).
- **Locked Quit (D7, A20).** The locked Quit entry shows only "Quit" and
  the lock, with no reason line; other locked entries (3D without WebGL 2)
  keep their reason line.
- **Scroll (P7).** The menu scrolls on windows too short for its content
  instead of clipping.
- Menu tests and the `[slow]` focus walks pass; screenshots at xl, l, m, p
  match J-Menu-xl/l/m/p.
- `.claude/context/app-shell-menu.md` reflects the layout and entry rules.

## Decisions
- Canvas artboards to check against: J-Menu-xl, J-Menu-l, J-Menu-m,
  J-Menu-p, J-Menu-extra (focus cards), J-Menu-states (No WebGL 2),
  J-Other-states ("No WebGL 2 · the menu without the cube").

  content (no build hash).

  on other locked entries.
- Owner decision 6 (A19): a fresh profile without WebGL 2 keeps Classic 2D
