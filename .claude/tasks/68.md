# Task 68 — 3D capture: recorder and camera sampler

Target: claude

## Goal
Record every 3D game for feature `replay-recording`: the 3D session creates
a recorder and feeds it each action, a sampler records the camera position,
yaw and pitch and the view mode, and the 3D adapter reports the sealed
replay beside the summary.

## Acceptance criteria
- The 3D session creates a recorder when the box opens, feeds it at the
  point it calls the engine — flags placed before the first reveal carry time
  0 — and discards it with the session.
- The 3D sampler records the camera position in cell units and its yaw and
  pitch, quantised, about 10 times a second, and the view mode (Shift, Space,
  Ctrl) whenever it changes, only while the game is started, unpaused and
  unfinished.
- The 3D adapter reports `game finished(summary, replay)` and `game
  abandoned(summary, replay)`.
- Node tests cover the sampler and a recorded 3D game sealed through the
  session with a fake clock.
