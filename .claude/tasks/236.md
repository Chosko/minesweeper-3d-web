# Task 236 — Learn 3D play layout and end cards

Target: claude

## Goal
The *Joyful tiles* canvas (Claude Design artifact
https://claude.ai/artifact/LeP32jxNxEnHLUtRwJ3FNC, page "Joyful tiles")
gives Learn 3D its own play layout and end cards; shipped reuses the full
game HUD and a bare end card. Part of the canvas drift fixes; intended
feature: `in-game-layout`. Drift ids: G32, G33.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Play layout (G32).** A top-left column: a head surface "Learn 3D"
  (18px) with Pause and its Esc hint, then a strip with mines and Restart
  only; no figures panel (no 3BV, Clicks, Flags or reminder). The prompt
  has its own strip: padding 9px 20px, gap 12, the step "1 / 4" in
  JetBrains Mono 13px (not the standard hint strip).
- **End cards (G33).** A "Learn 3D" label above the title; body text in
  ink. Win: "Ready for a real board? …" (the canvas's line). Loss: "The
  lesson box is always the same. Try it again." (shipped hides the line on
  a loss). Play Cube Intro / Try again with a play icon; "Back to board
  choice" md with an arrow-left icon.
- Learn 3D tests updated.
- `.claude/context/mode3d-learn.md` reflects the layout and copy.

## Decisions
