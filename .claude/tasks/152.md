# Task 152 — Cube preset re-tune and first-box presets

Target: claude

## Goal
Re-tune the 3D presets so each step up is a real step, and give the
first-time 3D player two small boxes to learn depth on before the cubes.
This is row W-17 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan
(Area: 3D, Size: Small, Phase 1). It closes P-20 and I-02 of that document,
under decision 2 ("May the cube presets change? Yes — P-20 proceeds, I-02
too") and decision 16 ("3D tutorial before the campaign? Yes — I-01, I-02
proceed"), decision 16 as amended by the director (see Decisions). P-20
(Major; the newcomer using Beginner as the on-ramp, the enthusiast moving
up): the cube presets are mis-tuned. With no-guess, Cube Beginner's first
click reveals 80 to 90 % of the board and the game lasts ten seconds; the
3BV curve is 1 : 5 : 21 against 2D's 1 : 4 : 11, and Cube Expert
(12 × 12 × 8 · 130) is a 500-click board at a density where cascades almost
never happen. Its approved solution: re-tune so each step is about ×2.5 to
×3 in 3BV — Beginner 6 × 6 × 6 · 16 (3BV about 47; with no-guess, the
default since decision 6, the first click opens about 60 %), Intermediate
8 × 8 × 8 · 40 as is, Expert 10 × 10 × 10 · 100 (3BV about 350) — keeping
6 × 6 × 6 · 10 as a labelled Intro board; records are keyed on size and
mines, so old boards keep their records. I-02 (the first-time 3D player):
Cube Beginner is one layer too many to read as a slab; first-box presets
before Beginner, spawned in the three-quarter view at spacing 1.5, one
preset-table entry each.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Preset table** (P-20, I-02): `PRESETS` in `js/mode3d/board-choice.js`
  holds six presets in this menu order:
  1. `box-1`, family `box`, "First box 1": 4 × 4 × 3 with the mine count
     measured below (about 4);
  2. `box-2`, family `box`, "First box 2": 5 × 5 × 3 · 6;
  3. `cube-intro`, family `cube`, "Cube Intro": 6 × 6 × 6 · 10;
  4. `cube-beginner`, "Cube Beginner": 6 × 6 × 6 with the mine count
     measured below (16 to 20);
  5. `cube-intermediate`, "Cube Intermediate": 8 × 8 × 8 · 40;
  6. `cube-expert`, "Cube Expert": 10 × 10 × 10 · 100.

  `FAMILIES` holds `box` ("First box") then `cube` ("Cube"). The
  12 × 12 × 8 · 130 board is no longer a preset.
- **First box 1's mine count is measured** (decision 16 as amended): a dev
  script (for example `dev/measure-first-box-3d.mjs`, in the style of
  `dev/measure-noguess-3d.mjs`) generates no-guess 4 × 4 × 3 boards through
  `generate` with the first click on the marked start cell, the box's
  centre cell (`floor(X / 2)`, `floor(Y / 2)`, `floor(Z / 2)`, task 138's
  definition; computed directly when task 138's helper is not in the tree).
  It runs 100 seeds for each mine count from 3 to 6. For each count it
  prints: the requests that met the attempt budget, the median share of safe
  cells the first click opens, the median 3BV left after the first click,
  and the seeds the first click wins outright. The preset takes the count
  nearest 4 that meets all three conditions: every seed meets the budget,
  the first click wins outright on at most 5 % of seeds, and the median 3BV
  left after the first click is at least 3. Between two equally near counts,
  the one with more 3BV left wins. The same script confirms 5 × 5 × 3 · 6
  meets the budget on every seed from its centre. The script's head comment
  and the preset's comment name the chosen count and the figures behind it.
- **Cube Beginner's mine count is measured** (P-20; director decision
  2026-10-12): the same script generates no-guess 6 × 6 × 6 boards the same
  way, with the first click on the marked centre cell, 100 seeds for each
  mine count from 16 to 20, and prints the same figures for each count. The
  preset takes, among the counts where every seed meets the attempt budget
  and the median 3BV left after the first click is at least 40, the one
  whose median share of safe cells opened by the first click is nearest
  60 %. The review's 16 is the starting candidate. The chosen count is
  written into this body's Decisions and the preset table, the script's head
  comment and the preset's comment name it and the figures behind it, and
  the records key Cube Beginner on it.
- **Records follow** (P-20): `PRESETS_3D` in `js/records/board.js` holds
  the same six presets with the same labels, and the test that keeps it
  equal to the 3D mode's `PRESETS` still holds. Labels: "First box 1",
  "First box 2 · no-guess", "Cube Intro", "Cube Beginner",
  "Cube Expert · no-guess". The 3D board listing orders the six presets
  in table order, each without then with no-guess, then custom boards by
  last played. The Records 3D board picker offers the same six presets in
  that order.
- **Old boards keep their records** (P-20): no record, history entry or
  replay is migrated, and board keys are unchanged. Records on
  6 × 6 × 6 · 10 read as "Cube Intro". Records on 12 × 12 × 8 · 130 read and
  list as a custom board ("12 × 12 × 8 · 130 mines"), with their bests,
  history and kept replays intact, the treatment former double layer boards
  already get.
- **Board choice screen**: `#board-choice` shows a "First box" group with
  its two presets, then the "Cube" group with Intro, Beginner, Intermediate
  and Expert, each item's `data-preset` its `presetKey` and its size line
  from `presetLabel`, then Custom and the Random fill. Every preset offers
  no-guess (no side is 2). `DEFAULT_CHOICE` stays `cube-beginner`, which is
  now 6 × 6 × 6 at the measured count. A remembered choice naming a preset
  id opens on that id's current board, and one naming no current id still
  falls back to cube Beginner.
- **Start spacing per preset** (I-02): each preset carries its start
  spacing, 1.5 for the two first-box presets and `SPACING_START` for the
  four cubes. One pure, DOM-free helper gives a board's start spacing: the
  matching preset's spacing when its X, Y, Z and mines match a preset (the
  way the records recognise a preset, a custom board of that size included),
  else `SPACING_START`. Every 3D start path that sets the spacing today uses
  it: a new game, Play again, Restart, Retry, Play a standard board, and the
  debug hook's start. Where task 139's spacing readout and its reset at a
  new game exist, the readout shows the start spacing (1.5) and the reset
  goes to it. The `X` toggle's values stay task 139's.
- **Three-quarter spawn** (I-02; task 125 owns the view): a first-box
  preset starts at the spawn pose task 125's pose function computes for
  that box at its start spacing, so the box is framed whole and the
  crosshair rests on the near face's centre cube. This task does not
  redefine the pose. Where that function is not in the tree yet, the start
  spacing still applies to whatever pose every 3D start uses.
- **Typical 3BV table** (task 133): when the typical-3BV table task 133
  introduced (keyed by size and mines, with `dev/measure-typical-bbbv.mjs`
  behind it) is in the tree, the script is re-run and the table holds the
  values it printed for every current named board: the six 3D presets and
  Classic 2D's three standard boards. The 12 × 12 × 8 · 130 row is gone, and
  task 133's test that every named board has an entry passes. When the
  table is not in the tree yet, this criterion does not apply, and task
  133's own measurement covers the new presets.
- **The curve is reported**: the commit body or the table's comment gives
  the measured median 3BV of the six presets and the no-guess first-click
  opening on Cube Beginner at its chosen count. If Beginner's opening is
  still far from about 60 %,
  or a cube step falls well outside ×2.5 to ×3, the presets still ship as
  decided, and the gap is named as a follow-up.
- **Tests first**: node tests pin the six presets (ids, families, names,
  sizes, order), `presetByKey('12,12,8,130')` returning null, the default
  choice, a remembered `cube-expert` choice opening on 10 × 10 × 10 · 100,
  no-guess available on every preset, and the start-spacing helper (both
  first-box presets 1.5, cubes and a non-preset custom board
  `SPACING_START`, a custom board with First box 2's size and mines 1.5).
  They also pin the 3D labels of all six presets and of 12 × 12 × 8 · 130 as
  a custom board, the 3D board listing order with an old 12 × 12 × 8 · 130
  record among the customs, and the Records 3D picker's six presets. Where
  task 125's spawn tests exist, the two first-box boxes at spacing 1.5 join
  their framed-spawn cases. A Playwright run with SwiftShader starts First
  box 1 and checks the spacing is 1.5 and the whole box is on screen, then
  opens Records on 3D and checks the picker's six presets.
- **What not to break** (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Decisions taken): no-guess
  boards as separate records, the last-choice memory, the live custom-form
  validation, the honest empty states and storage notices.
- The owned documents state the agreed design change in every passage that
  stated the old one: the 3D presets are First box 1 (4 × 4 × 3 · measured
  count), First box 2 (5 × 5 × 3 · 6), Cube Intro (6 × 6 × 6 · 10), Cube
  Beginner (6 × 6 × 6 · measured count, 16 to 20), Intermediate
  (8 × 8 × 8 · 40) and Expert
  (10 × 10 × 10 · 100); the first-box presets start at spacing 1.5; records
  on 12 × 12 × 8 · 130 read as a custom board. The documents also settle
  the points listed under Decisions. The documents are
  `.claude/domain/features/3d-play-flow.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/3d-game-records.md` and
  `.claude/domain/product-design.md`. Nothing else in those documents
  changes.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction: P-20 and I-02 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` under decision 2 (the
  cube presets may change) and decision 16 (I-02 proceeds), row W-17.
  - `3d-play-flow.md` § Scope and non-goals ("Board choice: three
    presets — cube Beginner (6 × 6 × 6, 10 mines), Intermediate
    (8 × 8 × 8, 40) and Expert (12 × 12 × 8, 130)"): six presets, two
    first-box presets before four cubes, with the sizes above.
  - `3d-play-flow.md` § Scope and non-goals, the double layer non-goal
    ("the preset labels and listing in `3d-game-records` recognise only
    the presets listed here"): 12 × 12 × 8 · 130 joins the former boards
    read as custom.
  - `3d-game-records.md` § Scope and non-goals ("the three cube presets
    are recognised from their exact size and mine count") and § Architecture,
    "Board listing" ("the three presets in the order cube Beginner,
    Intermediate, Expert"): six presets, in table order.
  - `product-design.md` § 3D mode ("Three presets — Beginner, Intermediate
    and Expert cubes — plus custom boards"): two first-box presets, then
    the Intro, Beginner, Intermediate and Expert cubes, plus custom boards.
  - `3d-play-flow.md` § Scope and non-goals ("The 3D scene's look, the
    camera, view modes and controls — kept as they are"): the first-box
    presets start at spacing 1.5 in task 125's three-quarter view (I-02).

  Settled here, without conflict: the start spacing is a per-board value
  matched by size and mines, with no new control or setting
  (`3d-play-flow.md` § Data and state, which names no spacing). These
  documents are normally `/architect`'s and `/product-design`'s; this task
  updates them for exactly this and for nothing else. A further design
  decision met at implementation time is not covered: leave that passage
  untouched and name it under *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
- **Decision 16 as amended (director, 2026-10-12):** the first-box
  presets are 4 × 4 × 3 with about 4 mines, the count measured at
  implementation so that a no-guess first click from the marked centre
  leaves work to do, and 5 × 5 × 3 · 6. The review's 4 × 4 × 2 · 3 proposal
  (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` I-02) was replaced because a box with a side of 2 can
  never be no-guess: two cells stacked along it share every neighbour
  (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan, last paragraph). The
  selection rule in the acceptance criteria turns "leaves work to do" into
  a check the script can apply.
- **Cube Beginner's count is measured (director decision, 2026-10-12;
  `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` P-20):** P-20's 6 × 6 × 6 · 16 is a measured
  candidate, not a fixed count. The implementer picks the count from 16 to
  20 by the rule in the acceptance criteria, which turns P-20's "the first
  no-guess click opens about 60 %" into a check the script can apply, with
  16 as the starting candidate. Chosen count: recorded here at
  implementation.
- **Preset ids name the tier, not the board**: `cube-beginner` and
  `cube-expert` keep their ids with the new sizes, so a remembered choice
  and the menu's "Last played" follow the tier. The Intro board gets a new
  id (`cube-intro`). Records follow size and mines, not ids, so the old
  Beginner's records become Cube Intro's.
- **Labels**: the first-box family reads "First box" and its presets "1"
  and "2" (records labels "First box 1", "First box 2"), so the two read as
  a sequence before the cubes. The Intro board reads "Cube Intro".
- **Start spacing follows the board, not the menu item**: the helper
  matches size and mines as the records do, so Play again, Restart, a custom
  board of a preset's size, and a board from a daily code or a challenge all
  start the same box at the same spacing.
- **Default unchanged**: the default and fallback choice stays cube
  Beginner. Steering a first-time player to First box belongs to the Learn
  3D guided board (W-21, I-01).
- **Replays and fixtures unchanged**: the 3D camera sample is in cell units
  and the viewer draws at its own spacing, so a per-preset start spacing
  changes no replay format. The `3d-cube-beginner-lost` fixture is a
  6 × 6 × 6 · 10 game: `dev/measure-replays.mjs` pins its size
  (`size: [6, 6, 6, 10]`, as the double layer entry does) so a regeneration
  reproduces it. No fixture file is rewritten.
- **Out of scope**: the cube daily board (task 151 chose 8 × 8 × 8 · 40 by
  dimensions, unaffected); the neighbourhood wireframe I-02 mentions and all
  readability work (W-18, task 141); the Learn 3D guided board (W-21, I-01),
  which comes after this task; the spawn pose itself (task 125); the
  spacing toggle and readout (task 139). The Steam leaderboard catalogue and
  achievements (`steam-leaderboards.md`, `steam-achievements.md`) name
  Beginner, Intermediate and Expert by name and stay as they are.
