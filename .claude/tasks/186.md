# Task 186 — 3D cell motion: motion mesh, event sources and the solved pulse as a cell event

Target: claude

## Goal
Play the theme's cell motion on the 3D cubes, for feature
`themed-board-art-and-motion`: animating cubes are drawn by one more instanced
mesh with per-instance scale, offset, rotation, brightness, opacity, face
shown and depth blend, the cube's ordinary instance hidden while it animates;
events come from the engine's change lists and the renderer's own
number-to-hidden detection; the current solved pulse becomes the solved-pulse
cell event played from the theme's description.

## Acceptance criteria
- One instanced motion mesh draws every animating cube from the motion
  player's values; offsets map x and y to the camera's screen axes and z
  toward the camera.
- Reveal, flag and unflag come from the engine's change lists; the solved
  pulse from the renderer's number-to-hidden detection, with its rules
  unchanged (visual only, the cube already hidden to picking, dropped on a
  loss and on a seek).
- Picking uses the logical cell, never the animated position; the game, timer
  and replay are identical with motion on or off.
- The replay viewer plays the same events from its frames and drops motion on
  a seek.
- Motion drops on a new board, a pause hiding the board and a theme change;
  nothing reads the operating system's reduce-motion preference.
- The flag-click flash and end effects keep their behaviour.
- `tests/render-3d-motion.test.mjs` covers the mesh's instances, the hidden
  ordinary instance, the solved pulse from the theme description and the
  drops; existing 3D feedback tests pass, updated only where the pulse's
  source moved.
