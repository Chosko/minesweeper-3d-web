# Task 180 — Tile recipe drawing styles: flat and bevel, with corner radius and depth

Target: claude

## Goal
Grow the shared tile recipe `js/tile-art.js`, for feature
`themed-board-art-and-motion`, from one fixed look into named drawing styles
with parameters: **flat** (the closed face with a light top band and a dark
bottom edge, the revealed face flush with a hairline — *Joyful tiles*' 2D
tiles) and **bevel** (a raised face framed by a thin edge and four sloped
sides, each side its own shade, lit from one corner; the revealed face flat
inside its edge — *Joyful tiles*' 3D cubes, and, with today's parameters,
today's look). Each style draws the closed, sunk and revealed faces and the
number, flag, mine, exploded-mine and wrong-flag glyphs from the palette and
parameters it is given, at any size, at any depth between flat and fully
raised, with an optional corner radius as a fraction of the size.

## Acceptance criteria
- The recipe exports a style table and a draw entry point taking a context, a
  size, a style name, its parameters, the palette entries it uses and a depth
  (0 flat … 1 fully raised); it reads no theme and no token.
- **flat** — parameters for the top band, the bottom edge, the revealed
  hairline and the corner radius (a fraction of the tile, so it scales);
  depth changes nothing it draws; closed and revealed faces differ by more
  than colour.
- **bevel** — parameters for the edge width and the slope width (fractions
  of the face), the four side shades (top, left, right, bottom) and the
  revealed edge; closed and flagged faces take the slope, revealed faces do
  not; the flag and glyphs draw over the face as in flat.
- **bevel** with today's parameters and palette draws pixel-equal to today's
  tiles at depth 1 (closed) and at the sunk depth, so the 2D board and the
  3D atlas look unchanged after this task.
- An unknown style draws the first shipped style rather than nothing, and
  reports the error once in development.
- Every face and glyph stays legible at `MIN_TILE_SIZE` in both styles (a test
  samples each state at that size).
- `tests/tile-art.test.mjs` covers both styles at several sizes, depths and
  radii, the bevel's four shades and the fallback; `tests/tile-skin.test.mjs`
  and the 3D atlas tests pass unchanged.

## Decisions
- **Today's look stays one setting of bevel**, so this task changes no pixel
  on screen; themes switch style in task 181 onward.
- **Owner decisions** (DESIGN-CHANGES §3.3, §3.4): *Joyful tiles* draws flat
  2D tiles with a corner radius that scales with the tile, and bevel 3D
  cubes ("Bevel 5 %") — four lit sides inside a thin edge, revealed cubes
  flat. This replaces the plain no-relief flat style and the band-based
  bevel this task first carried; the values are task 181's, from
  J-BoardSheet and J-3DSheet.
- **Settled design detail**
  (`.claude/domain/features/themed-board-art-and-motion.md` § Architecture,
  *Tile recipe with drawing styles*): the depth scale 0–1 and the style
  table's form. The feature document is not edited here; task 187 records what
  holds at ship time.
