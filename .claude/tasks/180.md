# Task 180 — Tile recipe drawing styles: bevel and chunky raised, with depth

Target: claude

## Goal
Grow the shared tile recipe `js/tile-art.js`, for feature
`themed-board-art-and-motion`, from one fixed look into named drawing styles
with parameters: **bevel** (today's raised-and-sunk cube face, its band
widths, light and dark edges and gloss as parameters) and **chunky raised** (a
flat face with a light top band and a deep darker bottom edge, the *Joyful
tiles* look). Each style draws the closed, sunk and revealed faces and the
number, flag, mine, exploded-mine and wrong-flag glyphs from the palette and
parameters it is given, at any size, at any depth between flat and fully
raised.

## Acceptance criteria
- The recipe exports a style table and a draw entry point taking a context, a
  size, a style name, its parameters, the palette entries it uses and a depth
  (0 flat … 1 fully raised); it reads no theme and no token.
- **bevel** with today's parameters and the current palette draws pixel-equal
  to today's tiles at depth 1 (closed) and at the sunk depth, so the 2D board
  and the 3D atlas look unchanged after this task.
- **chunky raised** draws a flat face, a light top band and a deep darker
  bottom edge whose height follows depth.
- An unknown style draws the first shipped style rather than nothing, and
  reports the error once in development.
- Every face and glyph stays legible at `MIN_TILE_SIZE` in both styles (a test
  samples each state at that size).
- `tests/tile-art.test.mjs` covers both styles at several sizes and depths and
  the fallback; `tests/tile-skin.test.mjs` and the 3D atlas tests pass
  unchanged.

## Decisions
- **Bevel reproduces today's look exactly**, so this task changes no pixel on
  screen; themes switch style in task 181 onward.
- **Settled design detail**
  (`.claude/domain/features/themed-board-art-and-motion.md` § Architecture,
  *Tile recipe with drawing styles*): the depth scale 0–1 and the style
  table's form. The feature document is not edited here; task 187 records what
  holds at ship time.
