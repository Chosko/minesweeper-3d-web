# Task 69 — Replay determinism tests and the measured recording constants

Target: claude

## Goal
Prove the determinism contract of feature `replay-recording` on recorded 2D
and 3D games, and settle its open questions by measuring real games: the
sampling rates, quantisation steps and movement cap, and whether the blob is
compressed.

## Acceptance criteria
- A Node test applies each recorded action stream to a game created from
  the header's graph, mine set, profile and rules version and reproduces the
  check values exactly, for 2D and 3D games including a win, a loss, an
  abandon and a game with wasted clicks.
- A measurement script reports replay sizes and movement smoothness over
  recorded games at candidate rates and quantisation steps, with and without
  the platform's compression stream.
- The recorder's sampling rates, quantisation steps and movement cap, and
  the format's compression choice (recorded in the format version), are set
  from the measurement.
- `.claude/domain/features/replay-recording.md` records the chosen constants
  and the compression choice in place of its open questions. Nothing else in
  the document changes.

## Decisions
- Settled here: the feature document's open questions on sampling rates,
  quantisation, the movement cap and compression, by measurement.
  `.claude/domain/features/replay-recording.md` is normally `/architect`'s;
  this task updates it for exactly this and for nothing else.
