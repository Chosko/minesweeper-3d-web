# Task 121 — No-guess default, switch copy, generating flow

Target: claude

## Goal
Make no-guess the board a new player meets, say what it is where it is
switched, and give the wait for a board and its failure a way out. This is
row W-04 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan (Area: Shell,
Size: Small, Phase 1). It closes P-05 and P-11 of that document, under its
decisions 6 ("No-guess on by default? Yes") and 18 ("Cube Expert no-guess
budget: no measurement; no-guess is the default"). Today no-guess is off by
default in both modes and unexplained, while a standard board is solvable by
logic alone in only 3 to 7 % of 2D Expert boards and 4 to 27 % of Cube
Expert boards, so most games end in a forced guess the newcomer reads as "I
lost for no reason"; the switch sits under the custom panel in 2D and under
the presets in 3D (P-05, Major, first-time player and newcomer to 3D). And
"Generating the board…" has no spinner or Cancel, Esc opens the pause card
over it, a failure then renders the No board found offer under the pause
card, the offer has no Back to menu, and a hopeless no-guess density lets the
player loop Retry forever (P-11, Minor, players starting large or dense
no-guess custom boards). The work serves first-time players and newcomers to
3D, and players of large or dense no-guess custom boards.

## Acceptance criteria
- **No-guess on by default** (P-05, decision 6): the Classic 2D and the 3D
  `DEFAULT_CHOICE` carry `noGuess: true`, so a profile with no stored last
  board choice opens either board choice with no-guess on (in 3D both the
  presets' and the custom board's switch). A stored last choice is honoured
  as stored, no-guess off included, and every later start saves the choice
  through the existing last board choice documents, so the player's setting
  is remembered from then on. The 3D fallback to cube Beginner for a stored
  choice that does not normalise opens with no-guess on. No budget
  measurement is added (decision 18).
- **Helper line and same placement** (P-05): on both board choice screens
  the no-guess switch sits directly under the presets menu, before the
  custom panel — Classic 2D's switch moves there from below its custom panel
  to match 3D's presets switch — with one `ui-text--muted` helper line under
  it reading exactly "Solvable by logic alone, no 50/50 guesses. Separate
  records." The 3D custom board's switch stays inside the custom panel with
  its availability reason line. Keyboard and controller order on both
  screens follows the new visual order.
- **Spinner and Cancel on the generating banner** (P-11): the "Generating
  the board…" card of both modes (`#c2d-status`, `#m3d-status`) shows a
  spinner — a kit class added to `css/components.css` and its catalogue
  comment, styled from tokens, animated only while the card shows —
  and a Cancel button. Cancel cancels the pending generation through the
  generation client and returns the session to its ready state: the closed
  board awaiting its first click, nothing recorded and nothing reported (a
  game left before its first click reports nothing); 3D flags placed before
  the reveal stay. Cancel takes focus when the card shows. The card's show
  delay is unchanged (`GENERATING_DELAY_MS`).
- **Esc cancels generation instead of pausing** (P-11): while a board is
  being generated — the session's `generating` state, whether or not the
  card has appeared yet — Esc and the controller's back button cancel it
  exactly as Cancel does, and do not open the pause card. In pointer-locked
  3D play the Esc the browser consumes to release the lock counts as that
  Esc. The overlay's pause button, the controller's Start button, focus loss
  and tab hide still pause as they do today.
- **Failure offer on top, with Back to menu** (P-11): the No board found
  offer of both modes always renders above the pause card and takes focus,
  including when the failure arrives while the game is paused, and gains a
  Back to menu button beside Retry and Play a standard board. Back to menu
  leaves the mode with no confirmation and records nothing, since no game
  has started. Retry and Play a standard board behave as today.
- **Second-failure focus** (P-11): after a second failure in a row in the
  same game (a Retry that fails again), the offer's default focus is Play a
  standard board instead of Retry; a first failure focuses Retry as today.
  Controller navigation over the offer follows the same default.
- **Density hint** (P-11): when no-guess is on and the custom board's
  density (mines ÷ cells) is above 25 %, a `ui-text--muted` hint shows under
  the custom no-guess switch in 3D, and under the custom panel's info line
  in Classic 2D, reading "Above 25 % mines, a no-guess board may not be
  found." It updates live with the custom fields like the info line, and
  never disables the switch or refuses the board. The 25 % threshold is one
  named constant.
- **Tests first**: session tests in both modes pin Cancel and back-key
  cancellation from `generating` back to `ready` (the client's cancel
  called, no started or abandoned report, a later first click requesting a
  fresh board) and the consecutive-failure count behind the focus default;
  board choice tests pin the `noGuess: true` default, a stored `false`
  surviving, the helper line and the density hint threshold; shell tests pin
  that Esc during generation does not pause and that the offer sits above
  the pause card with Back to menu; the contrast test in
  `tests/components.test.mjs` covers the generating card with its spinner
  and Cancel and the offer with its three buttons, in both themes. Existing
  tests that assumed a `noGuess: false` default are updated to state their
  choice explicitly.
- **What not to break** (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Decisions taken): no-guess
  boards stay separate records; the last-choice memory; the confirmations
  that say "It will count as a loss"; the live custom-form validation; the
  3D no-guess availability limits (8,000 cells, no side of 2) and their
  reason lines.
- `.claude/domain/features/game-shell.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/board-generation.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/classic-2d-square-play.md` and
  `.claude/domain/features/3d-play-flow.md` state the agreed design change —
  while a board is being generated Esc and the controller's back button
  cancel the generation instead of opening the pause card, and the failure
  offer is Retry, Play a standard board or Back to menu, shown above the
  pause card, defaulting to Play a standard board after a second failure in
  a row — in every passage that stated the old one, and settle no-guess on
  by default with the last choice remembered after that, the generating
  card's Cancel back to the closed board, and the density hint that warns
  without refusing. Nothing else in those documents changes.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction — `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` P-11, approved without condition under §
  Approved scope and work plan (row W-04); P-05 under decisions 6 and 18.
  - `game-shell.md` § Interfaces and contracts, "Pause rules", says that
    before the first click the pause key opens the pause card; while a
    board is being generated, Esc and the controller's back button now
    cancel the generation instead (the overlay's pause button, Start, focus
    loss and tab hide still pause).
  - `board-generation.md` § Failure offer says the player is offered Retry
    or Play a standard board; the offer gains Back to menu, renders above
    the pause card, and after a second failure in a row defaults its focus
    to Play a standard board.
  - `classic-2d-square-play.md` § Interfaces and contracts, "Generation
    failure", states the same two-option offer; it changes the same way.

  Settled here, without conflict: no-guess on by default in both modes for
  a profile with no stored last choice, remembered after that
  (`classic-2d-square-play.md` § Data and state, "Last board choice";
  `3d-play-flow.md` § Data and state, "Board choice"); the generating
  card's Cancel returning to the closed board (`3d-play-flow.md` §
  Architecture, "3D game session"; `classic-2d-square-play.md` § Scope, the
  start-of-game flow); the density hint, which warns and never refuses, as
  `board-generation.md` § Failure offer's "do not refuse the no-guess switch
  up front" requires (`3d-play-flow.md` § Interfaces and contracts, "No-guess
  availability"). Those four documents are normally `/architect`'s; this task
  updates them for exactly this and for nothing else. A further design
  decision met at implementation time is not covered: leave that passage
  untouched and name it under *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
- **The review's 2D fallback is not taken.** P-05 allowed keeping Classic 2D
  off by default if it had to keep the reference default; decision 6 settles
  it as on in both modes, so only the helper line is common copy, not a
  difference between modes.
- **Cancel goes back to the closed board.** P-11 names Cancel without a
  destination; returning to the ready state is the least surprising undo of
  the first click, records nothing, and leaves Restart and Back to menu one
  more Esc away on the pause card a not-started game already offers.
  Cancel-to-a-standard-board belongs to W-13's status card (P-34), not here.
- **The back button joins Esc.** `game-shell.md`'s navigation contract makes
  Esc and the controller's back button one Back input, so both cancel; the
  pause inputs that are not Back keep pausing.
- **One switch placement, one helper line.** "Same placement on both
  screens" is read as the 3D layout: the switch directly under the presets
  with the helper line beneath it. Classic 2D has a single switch, so its
  density hint sits in the custom panel under the info line, the place its
  custom density is shown; 3D's sits under its custom switch.
- **Hint copy and threshold.** "About 25 %" becomes a fixed 25 % constant
  (mines ÷ cells) with the hint copy above; the copy names the risk, not a
  time estimate, since P-11's estimate is not in the approved solution.
- **Boundaries.** W-13 (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` P-17, P-34, I-11: synchronous
  standard placement, warm worker, background pre-generation for a marked
  start cell, the status card's show timing, its progress line and
  reliability) is out of scope, so `GENERATING_DELAY_MS` and the generation
  client are untouched beyond calling `cancel`. Task 119 (row W-01) owns the
  " · no-guess" readout on the ready card, HUD and pause card, and also edits
  `index.html`, `css/style.css`, `js/main.js` and `js/ui.js`; this task does
  not touch that readout, so either can land first. P-19 was dropped by
  decision 1.
