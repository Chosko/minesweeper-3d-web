# Task 227 — Board frame and framed previews

Target: claude

## Goal
The *Joyful tiles* canvas (Claude Design artifact
https://claude.ai/artifact/LeP32jxNxEnHLUtRwJ3FNC, page "Joyful tiles";
class `j-board`) frames every 2D board in a plain white frame sized from
the module's gap. Shipped gives the frame the panel look with a fixed 9px
inset, and the menu preview, Daily preview and replay viewer mount boards
with no frame at all (the replay at the 100% board scale, clipped). Fix
the frame and mount every board inside it. Part of the canvas drift
fixes; feature: `themed-board-art-and-motion`. Drift ids: K26
(C3, F4, D11, D16, H2-8).

## Acceptance criteria
- `.ui-board-frame` is white with only `inset 0 0 0 2px` --color-border, no
  drop edge and no elevation; padding equals the module's tile gap (9% of
  the tile: 4px at 48, 3px at 28, 2px at 26); radius tile×.3. The fixed
  `PANEL_INSET = 9` goes; the inset comes from the module.
- On a phone the clipped (scrolling) board is a radius-14 viewport with no
  frame.
- The menu preview (Classic 2D and Daily focus) mounts the board inside
  the frame (Classic 2D tile 41 / gap 4 at xl, 27 / 2 at l, 23 / 2 at m;
  Daily tile 27 / gap 2, about 872px wide).
- The Daily screen's Classic 2D preview is the framed board on the white
  body with no dark scene panel (tile 17 / gap 1 at xl, 16 / 1 at l,
  15 / 1 at m).
- The replay viewer mounts the board inside the frame and fits the whole
  board in the board area instead of drawing at the 100% board scale
  (Intermediate at a 34px tile with 3px gap at xl, about 24px at l and m,
  about 19px at p); no clipping or scroll bars at l, m and p.
- Board-view, menu-preview, Daily and replay tests updated.
- `.claude/context/classic2d-board-view.md`, `app-shell-menu.md`,
  `app-shell-daily.md` and `replay-viewers.md` describe the frame.
