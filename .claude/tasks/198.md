# Task 198 — Classic 2D no-guess switch back under the presets, with the D-pad walk to match

Target: claude

## Goal
Put the no-guess switch in the same place on both board choices. The UX
review drift audit (`docs/UX_REVIEW_DRIFT.md`, "Five most important
findings" #3, § 1 row P-05 "drift") found that on the Classic 2D board
choice the switch `#c2d-noguess` and its helper line sit **below** the
custom form (index.html, after `#c2d-custom`), moved there by task 144 so
the D-pad walk of P-41 would reach it, while on the 3D board choice
`#bc-noguess` sits under the presets. P-05's approved solution in
`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` asks for "Same placement on both screens", and task
121's acceptance criteria put both directly under the presets. The user
chose: under the presets in both modes; the 2D switch moves back and the 2D
D-pad walk changes to match.

Steps to reproduce (from the audit): main menu → Classic 2D → the No-guess
switch and "Solvable by logic alone…" line come after the Custom panel;
main menu → 3D → they come right after the Cube presets, before Custom.

## Acceptance criteria
- On the Classic 2D board choice, the No-guess switch and its helper line
  ("Solvable by logic alone, no 50/50 guesses. Separate records.") sit
  directly under the presets menu (Beginner, Intermediate, Expert), before
  the Custom panel — the 3D board choice's arrangement. The density hint
  stays with the custom panel. Default, remembered state, labels and the
  board each choice starts are unchanged.
- The 2D D-pad and arrow-key walk: from Beginner, DOWN goes Intermediate,
  Expert, No-guess, then the custom form's Width; LEFT / RIGHT walk Width,
  Height, Mines and Start; DOWN from the custom row goes on to the board
  code field and Back; UP retraces the walk. Every focusable on the screen
  is still reached by the D-pad alone (P-41).
- The 3D board choice and its walk are unchanged.
- **Tests first**: tests/shell-pad-walk.test.mjs's 2D walk (the "D-pad walks
  every control of the 2D board choice…" test) is rewritten to the new
  order before the markup moves, and fails until it does; the 3D half of
  that test is unchanged. Any 2D board choice test that asserts the
  switch's DOM position or the old walk is updated to the new placement.

## Decisions
- **Placement under the presets in both modes (user decision, 2026-10-15):**
  chosen over moving the 3D switch below its custom form or keeping the
  difference. It is P-05's and task 121's specification, it puts the switch
  where a player choosing a preset sees it, and the 3D walk stays as is.
- **The walk follows the layout**, as P-41's approved solution ("lay the 2D
  form out so DOWN from the last preset reaches the fields and the switch")
  allows: the switch is reached between Expert and Width instead of after
  the custom row. This supersedes task 144's 2D walk order.
- Settled without conflict: the feature documents
  (`.claude/domain/features/classic-2d-square-play.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/3d-play-flow.md`) do not state where the switch
  sits on the screen; neither changes.
