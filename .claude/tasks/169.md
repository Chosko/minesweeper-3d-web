# Task 169 — Results screen redesigned in the shared layout

Target: claude

## Goal
Rebuild the results screen of both modes, for feature
`shared-screen-layout`, as J-Results-xl/-l/-m/-p and J-Results-states draw
it and `results-screen` and `3d-results-records-screens` now define it: one
column sized to its content — a header band with the outcome, board label and
rank chip; four figure tiles each carrying its own comparison and, where it
applies, a "New best" badge; three detail rows; the board code; the action
bar. The separate new-best banner and bests panel go.

## Acceptance criteria
- The screen is composed from its artboards using catalogued components
  only; no second column.
- **Header:** "You won!" with a trophy on the yellow band, or "Game over" on
  the neutral band; the board label; on the right the rank chip
  ("#3 of 12 wins on this board"), or for a challenge the chip "Challenge ·
  not eligible for bests".
- **Figure tiles:** on a win Time, 3BV/s, Efficiency and 3BV (board
  difficulty); on a loss Time, 3BV solved with a progress bar, 3BV/s,
  Efficiency. Each comparable tile's comparison line under the value per
  `results-screen` — the standing best and the difference, the replaced
  best on a new best with the "New best" badge on that tile, coloured with
  the sign kept; on a loss the time tile says losses set no bests and the
  others show the standing best without a difference.
- **Details:** Clicks (with the reveal / flag / chord / wasted breakdown);
  Next target on a win, Streak on a loss, or the challenge note; Win rate
  (with the completed games' figure and the longest streak).
- **Board code** chip with Copy and Copy link.
- **Action bar:** *Main menu* (Esc) on the left; *Watch replay*, *Watch
  best* (only after a win that set no new best), *Records* and *Play again*
  (Enter) on the right; with *Watch best* present the secondary buttons take
  the small size so the bar stays one row.
- **Over the 3D scene:** *Look at the board* (Esc) in the header band at the
  top right, the rank under the title, and *Main menu* without its Esc
  hint.
- **Phone** (J-Results-p): tiles two by two, the code chip stacked, *Play
  again* full width, then *Watch replay* and *Records* side by side, then
  *Main menu*; the card scrolls.
- Record-before-show, the comparison data, the rank, the 3D takeover, Enter
  and R as Play again, Watch replay / Watch best / Records / Main menu and
  the recording-failure state behave as `results-screen` states.
- Every surviving element id, route, default focus and controller
  navigation target survives or moves with its owner; results tests are
  updated first to the new composition (per-tile comparisons and badges, no
  banner, rank chip, loss tiles, Watch best's condition) and pass.
- No horizontal scroll at any supported window size; contrast checks pass
  under *Joyful tiles*, the difference colours included.

## Decisions
- **Owner decisions** (DESIGN-CHANGES §4.6): the one-column redesign with
  per-tile comparisons replaces the banner and bests panel; `results-screen`
  and `3d-results-records-screens` state the content.
- **Its artboards exist** — J-Results-* on the canvas — so the manual
  intervention this task carried while the artboard was undrawn is gone and
  the task is `claude`.
- **Copy** follows `results-screen`'s wording where it states one (for
  example "= best"); the artboards' sample figures are placeholders.
