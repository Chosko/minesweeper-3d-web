# Task 166 — Classic 2D board choice screen with its records panel

Target: claude

## Goal
Recompose the Classic 2D board choice screen, for feature
`shared-screen-layout`, from the S3D-Setup artboard as the feature document
amends it: inside the screen frame, the board options (name, size line, mine
density, Last played mark), the no-guess switch, Play on the right with its
key hint beside a non-accent *Records* action, and the records panel of the
selected board under the switch's setting — titled "INTERMEDIATE · YOUR
BESTS" or "INTERMEDIATE · NO-GUESS · YOUR BESTS" — with bests, wins, win rate
and streak, a Time chart and under it a 3BV/s and Efficiency chart over the
last 30 won games, the recent games and under them a Top 5 of the fastest
wins. Custom keeps its size fields, revealed when Custom is chosen.

## Acceptance criteria
- The screen is built from catalogued components only (screen frame, board
  option, section label, key hint, stat tile, list row).
- Each board option shows its name, size line, mine density and the Last
  played mark; no 3BV and no best on the row. Selecting one updates the
  panel at once.
- The panel's title names the selected board and, when the switch is on,
  "NO-GUESS" ("INTERMEDIATE · YOUR BESTS", "INTERMEDIATE · NO-GUESS · YOUR
  BESTS"). Bests (time, 3BV/s, efficiency, with when each was set), wins, win
  rate, streak, recent games, the Top 5 and both charts all follow the
  switch.
- The Time chart covers the board's last 30 won games; under it a second
  chart draws 3BV/s and efficiency over the same 30 wins, as the Records
  screen's history chart draws them — lost and abandoned games are not
  charted. Both reuse `history-chart.js`.
- Under the recent games, a Top 5 table lists the board's five fastest wins
  (`topGames` by time); a board with fewer shows what it has, and no wins
  shows an empty state.
- No trend figure ("you shave Xs per game" or similar) anywhere on the screen.
- Play sits on the right; the *Records* action, not accented, opens the
  Records screen on the selected board and switch (`records` screen
  `show({ mode, boardKey })`), Back returning here.
- The panel reads the records store through its existing read interface and
  change notification and holds no records of its own; a board with no games
  shows an empty state.
- Custom shows its size fields only while chosen; validation, the last board
  choice document and no-guess behave as before.
- Every element id, route, default focus and controller navigation target
  survives; existing board choice and returning tests pass, updated only where
  they assert the old arrangement.
- No horizontal scroll at 1024 px wide; contrast checks pass under *Joyful
  tiles*.

## Decisions
- **Top 5 ranking** — fastest time, wins only (owner's answer).
- **Charts count won games only**, as the Records screen does (owner's
  answer).
- **Feature document over artboard.** Where the S3D-Setup artboard draws 3BV
  or Best on preset rows, a trend figure or a different button placement, the
  feature document's owner decisions win.
- **Settled design detail** (`.claude/domain/features/shared-screen-layout.md`
  § Scope, *Board choice screens*): both charts reuse `history-chart.js`. The
  feature document is not edited here; task 173 records what holds at ship
  time.
