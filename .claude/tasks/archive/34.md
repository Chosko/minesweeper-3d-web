## Goal
Add the mouse input of feature `classic-2d-square-play`: a DOM-free
press-and-release state machine over the mouse buttons that reproduces the
reference's feel — press feedback on button down, reveal and chord on
release and a flag on the right press, the chord gestures the reference
accepts, cancelling by moving off a cell before release — wired to the
board view and the session.

## Acceptance criteria
- The state machine takes button down / up and pointer-cell changes and
  emits pressed-cell feedback and reveal / flag / chord actions; it is
  DOM-free and Node-tested.
- The behaviours follow `tests/fidelity/minesweeper-online.md`: left or
  middle held on a closed, unflagged cell shows it pressed and reveals on
  release (a flagged cell shows nothing); held on a revealed number, its
  closed unflagged neighbours show pressed and the release chords; left +
  right chords through the left release; a right press toggles a flag at
  once, also while left is held on a closed cell, and does nothing on a
  revealed cell; moving onto another cell moves the press there, and
  releasing off the board does nothing.
- Which inputs chord, the press feedback and release-off-cell behaviour are
  read from the reference profile's input rules, so task 37 pins them
  without restructuring.
- Wired to the board view: pressed feedback is drawn through the skin's
  pressed state; actions go to the session.
- The context menu is suppressed over the board.
- A Playwright check plays a Beginner game by mouse to a win on a fixed
  board.
