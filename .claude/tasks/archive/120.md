## Goal
Finish the dark theme on the two surfaces it misses, so a dark-theme
player never meets an unreadable panel or a light page behind a dark card.
This is row W-02 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan
(Area: Shell, Size: Small, Phase 1). It closes P-03 and P-06 of that
document. Today the main-menu Controls panel keeps a hard-coded white card
(`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` on `.modal-card` in `css/style.css`) and a
hard-coded light-blue backdrop while its text uses dark-theme ink, so the
term column is invisible in the dark theme (P-03, Major, any dark-theme
player). And the 3D renderer's sky gradient — behind every menu through the
demo board, behind the 3D game, and the fog colour — stays pale blue in the
dark theme, so a dark card floats on a light page (P-06, Minor, dark-theme
players). The work serves every dark-theme player.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Controls panel surface from the kit's tokens** (P-03): the Controls
  modal's card (`.modal-card`) takes its background from the kit's surface
  token and its border from the kit's surface-border token, and the modal's
  backdrop (`.modal`) from a themed backdrop-scrim colour token in
  `css/tokens.css` with a light and a dark value. No literal colour remains
  in the `.modal` and `.modal-card` rules. In the light theme the panel
  looks as it does today.
- **Overlay scrims follow the theme** (P-06): the other scrims in
  `css/style.css` that hard-code the same light-blue tint over the scene —
  `#pause, #ready, #results` and `#ctx-lost` — use the same backdrop-scrim
  token (or a sibling token with its own light and dark value where their
  opacity differs). In the light theme they look as they do today.
- **Controls panel in the contrast test** (P-03): the Playwright test in
  `tests/components.test.mjs` that checks the menu, the pause card and the
  results layout against the contrast contract also opens the main-menu
  Controls panel (`#menu-help`) and checks it in both themes, with the same
  `check` helper (its text measured, every run meeting the contrast
  contract, a screenshot per theme, light and dark screenshots differing).
  It fails on the current white card in the dark theme.
- **A themed sky** (P-06): `css/tokens.css` declares the sky's colours as
  colour tokens — the horizon, zenith and below-horizon stops of the
  gradient — each with a light and a dark value. The light values are
  today's RGB(200,225,255), RGB(158,194,243) and RGB(222,236,255), so the
  light theme's scene is unchanged. The dark values are placeholders that
  sit with the dark backdrop tokens and keep the overlay bar over the 3D
  scene inside the contrast contract (its existing browser test passes in
  both themes).
- **Sky, fog and demo-scene background switch with the theme** (P-06): the
  3D renderer draws the sky gradient, the fog (which fades toward the sky
  colour) and the clear colour from one sky palette set on it, instead of
  the constants in `SKY_GLSL` and `setClearColor(0xc8e1ff)`. The shell
  (`js/main.js`) reads the sky tokens through the token reader at start-up
  and on every theme change, sets them on the renderer and requests a
  redraw, so the menu's demo board, the 3D game, the paused game and the
  replay viewer's 3D scene all follow a theme switch at once, with no
  reload. The renderer itself still reads no token and subscribes to no
  theme change; it is handed the colours.
- **Only sky, fog and background are themed**: the atlas tiles, number
  colours, selection outline and win/loss effects are identical in both
  themes.
- `tests/tokens.test.mjs` and `tests/theme.test.mjs` keep checking that
  `js/render.js` and `js/textures.js` read no token and subscribe to no
  theme change, renamed and worded for the rule that the scene's palette is
  handed in by the shell; a new check pins that every sky token has a light
  and a dark value and that the shell hands the renderer the active theme's
  sky on start-up and on a theme change.
- `.claude/domain/features/design-tokens-and-themes.md`,
  `.claude/domain/product-design.md` and
  `.claude/domain/features/screen-components.md` state the agreed design
  change — the 3D scene's sky, fog and background follow the theme through
  sky tokens the shell hands the renderer, while the cube tiles, number
  colours and effects keep one look in both themes — in every passage that
  stated the old one. Nothing else in those documents changes.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction — `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` P-06, approved without condition under
  § Approved scope and work plan (row W-02).
  - `design-tokens-and-themes.md` § Scope and non-goals says the 3D scene
    keeps one look — sky gradient, fog, cube tiles and number colours —
    under both themes, "a decision, not a deferral"; this task themes the
    sky, fog and background and leaves tiles and numbers as they are.
  - `design-tokens-and-themes.md` § Architecture, "Theme-independent
    scene", says the renderer's palette is not tokenised; this task
    tokenises the sky, handed to the renderer by the shell, which still
    never subscribes to theme changes.
  - `product-design.md` § Visual design system says the 3D scene keeps its
    one current look under both themes; it now does so except for the sky,
    fog and background.
  - `screen-components.md` § Architecture, "Overlay bar", says the bar stays
    readable over the 3D scene's light sky in both themes; the sky is now
    light only in the light theme, and the bar keeps its own themed surface.

  Those three documents are normally `/architect`'s and `/product-design`'s;
  this task updates them for exactly this and for nothing else. A further
  design decision met at implementation time is not covered: leave that
  passage untouched and name it under *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
- **The shell feeds the renderer; the renderer stays token-free.** The token
  reader is a shell-side module, and `js/render.js` already takes its
  per-board settings from `js/main.js` (pixel ratio, game). Handing it a sky
  palette keeps the renderer free of CSS and keeps the existing
  no-token / no-subscription guard meaningful.
- **Scrims go with the backdrop.** P-06's "dark card floats on a light page"
  covers every light-blue tint laid over the scene, so the pause, ready,
  results and context-lost scrims take a themed token alongside the
  Controls modal's backdrop. No other hard-coded colour in `css/style.css`
  is in scope.
- **Boundary with task 119** (row W-01, Mode-aware help): 119 rebuilds the
  Controls panel's content — its rows, the Classic 2D and 3D tabs and the
  bindings component that fills it — and also edits `css/style.css` and
  `index.html`. This task owns only the panel's surface, border and
  backdrop tokens and its contrast check; it does not edit the panel's rows
  or markup content, so either task can land first.
- **Out of scope** (dropped under `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` decision 17,
  accessibility settings not now): reduced motion for the demo board
  (P-56) and colour-blind-safe number colours (P-57). No new setting: the
  existing theme preference drives everything.
