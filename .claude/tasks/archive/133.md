## Goal
Tell a player what each board is and where they stand on it, and let a
returning player pick up where they left off in one click. This is row W-06
of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan (Area: Shell, Records,
Size: Small, Phase 2: preset detail lines, menu session strip, quick play).
It closes P-08 and P-54 of that document. P-08 (Minor, newcomer to 3D and
returning player): the 3D preset picker says only "6 × 6 × 6 · 10 mines",
nothing about length, that Expert is a 500-click board, that no-guess splits
records, or the player's best, and the 2D picker is the same; its approved
solution is "One detail line per preset: density, typical 3BV, the player's
best and streak ("Best 48.2 s · 3 in a row", or a dash); the no-guess switch
labelled "separate records"." P-54 (Minor, returning players): the main menu
tells a returning player only "Last played", with no last result, today's
best, streak, or a way to start the last board in one step (two clicks for
2D, three for 3D); its approved solution is "A one-line session strip under
the entries ("Expert · best 13.34 s · today 7 games · streak 2") and a "Play
again: Expert · no-guess" action that starts the last board directly (in 3D
that click is the pointer-lock gesture)." Every figure comes from the
records store; nothing new is stored.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Typical 3BV table** (P-08): one constant table of typical 3BV per named
  board (Classic 2D's three standard boards, the 3D cube presets), keyed by
  the board's size and mine count, so a preset whose size or mines change
  has no entry until it is measured again. A dev script (for example
  `dev/measure-typical-bbbv.mjs`, in the style of
  `dev/measure-noguess-3d.mjs`) generates boards for every named board
  through the generation worker's `generate` with no-guess on (the
  default), and prints the median 3BV per board in the table's form; the
  table holds the values that script printed at implementation time, and
  its head comment names the script. The review's measurements (medians 16,
  65, 174 for 2D; 24, 124, 502 for the cubes before their re-tune, row
  W-17) are the order of magnitude to expect, not values to copy.
- **Preset detail line** (P-08): on both board choice screens (Classic 2D
  `#c2d-choice`, 3D `#board-choice`) every preset item keeps its size line
  ("9 × 9 · 10 mines") and its "Last played" line, and gains one detail line:
  density, typical 3BV, then the player's best time and current streak on
  that board, e.g. "7.8 % mines · ~124 3BV · Best 48.213 s · 3 in a row".
  Density is mines ÷ cells as a whole percent (one decimal below 10 %);
  the 3BV part is omitted when the table has no entry for the board. The
  records part names the board the item would start, so it follows the
  presets' no-guess switch (a board and its no-guess twin are separate
  records) and updates when the switch changes. With no won game on that
  board the records part is a dash ("—"); with a best, "Best <time>",
  followed by " · <n> in a row" when the board's current streak is at
  least 1. The time is `formatTime` of `js/results/view.js`, the one format
  outside the live bar (task 128, to the thousandth). The line is filled
  from the records store each time the screen shows; the binders stay
  DOM-only and receive the text from a DOM-free helper, never importing the
  store.
- **"Separate records" on the no-guess switch** (P-08): each no-guess switch
  on both board choice screens says that no-guess boards keep separate
  records, stated once per switch. Task 121's helper line under the presets'
  switch ("Solvable by logic alone, no 50/50 guesses. Separate records.")
  satisfies it where present; where a switch has no such line, its label
  reads "No-guess board · separate records". No switch states it twice.
- **Session strip** (P-54): the main menu shows one line under the entries,
  for the last board played (the last mode played, `shell.lastMode`, and
  that mode's last board choice, only once it was played): the board's
  label (`boardLabel`, e.g. "Expert · no-guess", "Cube Expert"), the
  board's best time, the number of games on that board that ended today,
  and the board's current streak — "Expert · best 13.343 s · today 7 games ·
  streak 2". A missing best shows "best —"; "today 1 game" is singular;
  "today" is the player's local calendar day, from the history entries'
  `endedAt`. With no last board played the strip is not shown. The strip is
  rebuilt when the menu shows and when the records store reports a newly
  recorded game (`onChange`), and after `LAST_MODE.load()` settles at
  start-up.
- **Play again from the menu** (P-54): the menu's header (the title row)
  carries a "Play again: <board label>" button, e.g. "Play again: Expert ·
  no-guess", shown only when there is a last board played. One activation
  starts that board in its mode: Classic 2D opens straight onto the closed
  board (`MODES.start('classic-2d', …)` with the last choice); 3D starts the
  board and enters play the way the results screen's Play again enters a 3D
  game (`MODES.start('3d', …)` then `enterPlay3D(source)`): with a pointer,
  the click is the pointer-lock gesture, so the ready card is not shown when
  lock is granted and remains the fallback (with its message) when lock is
  refused or fails; with a controller, lockless play. The audio is unlocked
  as on a board choice start. Starting this way saves nothing new and
  leaves the last-choice memory, the "Last played" marks and the menu's
  default focus (the last mode played) as they are.
- **No 3D route without WebGL 2** (decision 13; task 132): the 3D Play again
  reads the same WebGL 2 availability flag as every other route into 3D.
  Without WebGL 2 and with a 3D last board, the Play again button is not
  shown (the 3D entry already carries the reason); the session strip still
  shows. A Classic 2D last board is unaffected.
- **Navigation**: the Play again button is reachable by keyboard and
  controller in the menu's focus order, activated by Enter, Space, a click
  or the controller's A, and needs no new binding.
- **Tests first**: a DOM-free helper module's tests pin the preset detail
  text (density rounding, the 3BV part present and omitted, the dash, best
  with and without a streak, the no-guess twin read separately), the session
  strip text (singular and plural games, "best —", only today's games
  counted across a day boundary in local time, no strip without a last
  board) and the Play again label, over a records model built in the test.
  A test pins that every current named board (`STANDARD_BOARDS`,
  `PRESETS_3D`) has a typical 3BV entry. Board choice binder tests pin the
  detail line's markup and its update on the no-guess switch;
  `tests/shell-menu.test.mjs` or the helper's tests pin which board Play
  again starts. A Playwright run checks, with SwiftShader: the detail lines
  on both screens before and after a won game, the strip after games, Play
  again starting a 2D board in one click, and a 3D board entering play with
  lock granted and showing the ready card when lock is refused; and with
  WebGL 2 removed (as in task 132's check) a 3D last board showing no Play
  again.
- **What not to break** (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Decisions taken): no-guess
  boards as separate records, the last-choice memory, release-to-act input,
  the honest empty states and storage notices, and the confirmations that
  say "It will count as a loss".
- The owned documents state the agreed design change, in every passage that
  stated the old one — the main menu gains the session strip and the Play
  again action over the last board played; each preset on both board choice
  screens gains a detail line, and the no-guess switch says it keeps
  separate records — and settle the points listed under Decisions. The
  documents are `.claude/domain/features/game-shell.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/3d-play-flow.md` and
  `.claude/domain/features/classic-2d-square-play.md`. Nothing else in those
  documents changes.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction, P-08 and P-54 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md`, approved without
  condition under § Approved scope and work plan (row W-06).
  - `game-shell.md` § Architecture, "Main menu" ("The entry list composed
    from `screen-components`, each entry a route"): the menu also carries
    the session strip under the entries and the Play again action in its
    header, which starts the last board played without a route through its
    board choice.
  - `game-shell.md` § Data and state, "Last mode played" (kept "so later
    milestones' *Continue* can resume it"): it also names the board Play
    again starts and the strip describes.
  - `3d-play-flow.md` § Scope and non-goals ("Board choice: three
    presets …") and § Architecture, "3D board choice": each preset shows a
    detail line (density, typical 3BV, best and streak) and the no-guess
    switch says it keeps separate records.
  - `classic-2d-square-play.md` § Scope and non-goals ("Board choice:
    Beginner …, plus the no-guess switch"): the same detail line and
    switch wording.

  Settled here, without conflict: a 3D board started from the menu's Play
  again enters play as the results screen's Play again does, the ready card
  the fallback when pointer lock is not granted (`3d-play-flow.md` §
  Architecture, "Mode adapter", which keeps today's pointer-lock and
  lockless behaviour). These documents are normally `/architect`'s; this
  task updates them for exactly this and for nothing else. A further design
  decision met at implementation time is not covered: leave that passage
  untouched and name it under *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
- **"best" in the strip is the board's personal best**, not today's best:
  the strip's example puts "today" on the games count only, and the
  personal best is what the records store holds per board. "today" counts
  games on the last board, not on every board, so every figure on the line
  is about the board it names.
- **The streak shown is the reference current streak** (`counters(board)
  .currentStreak`), in which an abandoned game is a loss. Task 130's
  completed-games streak sits beside the reference figures on the Records
  screen; these two compact surfaces show only the reference one.
- **The typical 3BV table is measured, never hand-typed**: it is keyed by
  size and mines so a preset change (row W-17, P-20 and I-02, not yet in the
  backlog) leaves the changed preset without an entry, and the table test
  fails until the script is re-run. Whoever implements W-17 re-runs the
  script and updates the table; the detail line omits the 3BV part for a
  board with no entry rather than show a stale figure.
- **Size line kept, one detail line added**: the size line is the board's
  identity and the custom panel's reference; P-08's "one detail line" is the
  one added line, not a replacement.
- **Play again is hidden, not disabled, without WebGL 2**: the menu's 3D
  entry already shows the reason (task 132), and a disabled header button
  would repeat it.
- **Menu default focus unchanged**: the last mode played's entry keeps
  default focus (`game-shell` focus rules, task 122's navigation); Play
  again is one click or one move away.
- **Out of scope**: crash durability and resume (P-49, P-50, P-18; decision
  9); the daily board (I-03, row W-33); the Records screen's layout and lists
  (W-30, W-31); the cube preset re-tune itself (W-17); the no-guess default
  and its helper line (task 121); "Start / Continue" (`game-shell`
  non-goal).
