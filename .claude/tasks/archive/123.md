## Goal
Give the shell one vocabulary across its screens, make the mines counter
show the real count on large boards, and turn a refused pointer lock from a
dead end into a way to play. This is row W-07 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` §
Approved scope and work plan (Area: Shell, Size: Small, Phase 1). It closes
P-10, P-12 and P-13 of that document. Today the wording drifts: Settings has
a Theme heading over a Theme label; audio is "Sound: on" then Volume on the
main menu, Volume then Mute in Settings, Volume then "Sound: on" on the pause
card; the 3D board choice lists "Beginner" under a Cube heading while results
and Records say "Cube Beginner"; Back, Main menu and Back to menu name the
same move (P-10, Minor, everyone). When the browser refuses pointer lock, the
3D ready card says "Could not capture the mouse. Click once more." on every
click and offers nothing else (P-12, Minor, players on embedded pages or with
strict privacy settings). The overlay bar's mines counter stops at 999 while
the pause card says 2480 mines left, so the count is wrong until 999 flags
are placed (P-13, Minor, custom-board players). P-09 (the landing tagline and
menu subtitles) is not fixed, per the Approved scope paragraph.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Sound** (P-10): the audio on/off control reads "Sound" on the main menu,
  the pause card and the Settings page, and comes first, followed by the
  Volume slider, in all three places. In Settings the `muted` setting's
  toggle is labelled "Sound" and shows on when sound plays (on = not muted);
  the stored key `muted`, its default and the legacy carry-over are
  unchanged. The menu and pause card buttons keep their "Sound: on" /
  "Sound: off" text, and M still toggles it. No visible "Mute" label
  remains.
- **Back and Main menu** (P-10): a control that returns one step reads
  "Back"; a control that goes to the main menu reads "Main menu". The
  results screen's `#r-menu` and the context-lost screen's
  `#ctx-lost-menu` read "Main menu"; so does any other shell control that
  goes to the menu, including the No board found offer's menu button when
  task 121 has landed. No visible "Back to menu" label remains anywhere in
  the shell. The pause card's Main menu and its confirmation ("Leave this
  game for the main menu? It will count as a loss.", confirm "Main menu")
  are unchanged.
- **Restart and Play again** (P-10): "Restart" labels only the action that
  discards an unfinished game (the pause card while a game is unfinished,
  its confirmation "Restart this game? It will count as a loss."), and
  "Play again" only the action that starts the same board choice after a
  game ended (the pause card once ended, the results screen). No screen
  shows both for the same moment; every help, hint and bindings line that
  names either follows the same rule.
- **Cube presets** (P-10): the 3D board choice's preset items read "Cube
  Beginner", "Cube Intermediate" and "Cube Expert", matching results and
  Records; the "Cube" heading above them goes, so the word is not said
  twice, and the presets menu keeps an accessible name. The preset data
  (`PRESETS` ids, names, sizes) and the Records and results labels are
  unchanged.
- **Theme** (P-10): the Settings page's Theme section no longer says
  "Theme" twice: the section heading stays, the theme control shows no
  separate visible label and takes its accessible name from the heading.
  Every other section's heading and labels are unchanged.
- **Mines counter** (P-13): `formatMineCount` no longer clamps the upper
  end, so the overlay bar and the replay viewer show the real mines left
  (2480 shows as 2480); the lower bound −99 is kept. When a board is set,
  the overlay bar sizes `#hud-mines` to the digits of the board's mine
  count (at least three), with tabular figures, so the bar does not shift
  as the count falls. Standard boards look as they do today.
- **Pointer-lock fallback** (P-12): a pointer-lock refusal on the 3D ready
  card is counted; a refusal inside the existing re-lock cooldown (the
  "needs a moment" case, 1500 ms after a release) is not, and an acquired
  lock resets the count. The first counted refusal keeps today's message.
  From the second, the ready card explains that the browser is blocking
  mouse capture on this page, and offers a "Play with drag-to-look" button
  outside the click-to-play card plus a line naming the controller (connect
  one and press A). The ready card's Back stays visible and works as today.
  For the rest of the page session, every later 3D ready card shows the
  explanation and the offer at once; nothing is persisted.
- **Drag-to-look** (P-12): "Play with drag-to-look" enters play without
  pointer lock (the existing lockless play: losing lock does not pause and
  Esc pauses directly), with the system cursor visible. Holding a mouse
  button and moving it past a small threshold turns the camera with the
  look sensitivity and invert-Y settings; a press released before the
  threshold acts on the crosshair cube exactly as a locked press and
  release does, and a drag that passed the threshold acts on nothing, so
  release-to-act with drag-to-cancel holds. The wheel, keyboard and
  controller work as in locked play. Resume from pause returns to
  drag-to-look play; leaving the game ends it. Locked play is unchanged.
- **What not to break** (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § What not to break): the
  confirmations that say "It will count as a loss" keep that wording, and
  release-to-act with drag-to-cancel holds in both modes.
- Tests come first: the new counted-refusal logic and the drag-to-look
  gesture are pure units with their own tests; `formatMineCount`, the
  Settings page labels and the 3D board choice markup tests are updated to
  the new wording; the full suite passes.
- `.claude/domain/features/screen-components.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/game-shell.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/results-screen.md` and
  `.claude/domain/features/3d-play-flow.md` state the agreed design change
  — the mine counter grows to the board's digits with −99 kept as its lower
  bound; the menu action is labelled Main menu, not Back to menu; after a
  second pointer-lock refusal the 3D ready card explains the block and
  offers drag-to-look or the controller — in every passage that stated the
  old one, and `.claude/domain/features/settings.md` settles the Sound
  label for the audio on/off setting. Nothing else in those documents
  changes.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction — `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` P-10, P-12 and P-13, approved without
  condition under § Approved scope and work plan (row W-07).
  - `screen-components.md` § Architecture, "Overlay bar", says the mine
    counter is clamped to −99 … 999; the upper clamp goes (P-13), −99 stays.
  - `game-shell.md` § Interfaces and contracts, "Back to menu", and
    `results-screen.md` (Scope, "Actions"; Interfaces, "Back to menu") name
    the menu action Back to menu; it is labelled Main menu (P-10).
  - `3d-play-flow.md` § Architecture, "Mode adapter", keeps today's
    pointer-lock and lockless behaviour; after a second refusal the ready
    card now offers drag-to-look or the controller (P-12).

  Settled here, without conflict: the audio on/off control is labelled
  Sound and the volume control Volume (`settings.md` § Scope, "Audio");
  the preset labels read Cube Beginner, Intermediate, Expert
  (`3d-play-flow.md` § Scope, "Board choice"). These documents are
  normally `/architect`'s; this task updates them for exactly this and for
  nothing else. A further design decision met at implementation time is
  not covered: leave that passage untouched and name it under *Follow-ups*
  as a precise follow-up.
- **"Cube Beginner" on the preset, "Cube" heading dropped.** P-10's approved
  solution names Cube Beginner on the preset; dropping the heading avoids
  the same doubled word as Theme, and matches the Classic 2D board choice,
  which has no heading over its presets. The label is display only:
  `PRESETS` keeps `name: 'Beginner'`, since `js/records/board.js` composes
  the Records label from family and name.
- **Restart and Play again stay two words for two moments.** The shell
  already swaps the pause card's Restart for Play again once a game ends,
  and `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` itself uses Restart for abandoning a game
  (decision 8, I-06) and Play again after it (I-07); the fix is that every
  surface follows that rule, not a merge.
- **Theme heading kept, control label dropped.** The Settings sections
  (Controls, Graphics, Theme, Audio) are the settings feature's structure;
  removing the repeated label changes nothing else. Audio keeps its
  heading, since its two controls, Sound and Volume, do not repeat it.
- **Counter lower bound −99 kept.** P-13 is about the upper cap; the −99
  floor matches the reference game's display and no board needs more.
- **Second refusal, cooldown not counted.** The cooldown refusal is the
  browser's re-lock delay after Esc, not a block, so it must not trigger
  the explanation; remembering the block for the page session saves the
  player two failed clicks on every new game, while a reload retries.
- **Drag-to-look aims with the crosshair.** Picking stays at the screen
  centre, so a click acts on the cube under the crosshair as in locked
  play; the drag threshold is what keeps drag-to-cancel.
