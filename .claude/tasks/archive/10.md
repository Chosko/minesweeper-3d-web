## Goal
Build the solver of feature `board-generation`: given a cell graph, a mine
set and a first-click cell, it plays the board by deduction alone and reports
whether the board clears without a guess. Its correctness is the no-guess
promise, so it is tested on hand-built boards with known answers.

## Acceptance criteria
- Works over any cell graph; never reads a mine it has not deduced.
- Deduction in order: single-cell rules (a number satisfied by its flags
  opens the rest; a number equal to its closed neighbours flags them), then
  subset and overlapping-constraint reasoning between neighbouring numbers,
  then the remaining-mine count over the frontier.
- Returns whether the board clears from the first-click cell, and the cells
  it could not resolve when it does not.
- Hand-built test boards: one solvable by single-cell rules, one needing
  subset reasoning, one needing the global mine count, one needing exactly
  one guess, and one needing a guess only at the end; each gives the known
  answer.
- An Expert board is solved well within a second in Node.
