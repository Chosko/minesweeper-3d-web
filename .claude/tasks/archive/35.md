# Task 35 — Classic 2D keyboard and controller cursor

Status: [DONE]

Feature: classic-2d-square-play

## Goal
Make Classic 2D fully playable without a mouse, as feature
`classic-2d-square-play` requires: a visible cell cursor moved by arrow
keys, d-pad or stick with auto-repeat, and reveal, flag and chord on
buttons, ending in the same engine actions as pointer input. Settles the
feature document's open questions on the controller mapping and held-button
feedback.

## Acceptance criteria
- A cell cursor drawn as a focus ring over the board, moved by arrow keys,
  d-pad and left stick, with auto-repeat for held directions (reusing
  `Repeat` and `stickCurve` from `js/gamepad.js`); the board scrolls to keep
  it visible.
- Controller: A or RT reveals, X or LT flags, Y or both triggers chords; B
  and Start keep their shell meanings (back / pause) and never act on the
  board. Keyboard: Space or Enter reveals, F flags, D chords.
- Holding a reveal or chord button shows the same pressed feedback as a held
  mouse button, and acts on release.
- Every action available to the mouse is available on the controller.
- The cursor logic is DOM-free and Node-tested with `fakePad`-style input; a
  Playwright check plays a Beginner game to a win by keyboard.
- `.claude/domain/features/classic-2d-square-play.md` records the mapping and
  the held-button feedback as settled in place of its two open questions.
  Nothing else in the document changes.

## Decisions
- Settled here: the controller mapping (A / RT reveal, X / LT flag, Y / both
  triggers chord, no single "smart" button) — the triggers match the 3D
  mode's trigger-as-mouse-button feel and the face buttons avoid B and Start,
  which the shell owns; and held buttons show pressed feedback, matching the
  mouse. `.claude/domain/features/classic-2d-square-play.md` is normally
  `/architect`'s; this task updates it for exactly this and for nothing
  else.
