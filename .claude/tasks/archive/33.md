## Goal
Build the board view of feature `classic-2d-square-play`: a Canvas 2D board
fitted to the available area at the largest tile size that fits, down to the
skin's minimum tile size, with scrolling or panning beyond that, pointer
hit-testing, and per-cell redraw from the engine's change lists through the
tile skin.

## Acceptance criteria
- The view sizes the canvas to the available area and device pixel ratio and
  picks the largest tile size that fits the board, never below
  `MIN_TILE_SIZE`; below it the board keeps the minimum size and scrolls or
  pans (wheel, drag with a modifier, and keyboard / controller cursor
  follow).
- `cellAt(x, y)` maps a pointer position to a cell index or none, including
  in a scrolled board; the mapping is DOM-free and Node-tested.
- After each action only the changed cells are repainted through
  `paintTile`; a full repaint happens on resize, pixel-ratio change and
  theme change (the skin's redraw callback).
- The board background uses design tokens; the board is hidden while the
  game is paused.
- Playwright screenshots of Beginner and Expert in both themes, and of a
  large custom board scrolled, show the board drawn correctly.
