# Task 115 — Three cube 3D presets and no no-guess on a side of 2

## Goal
Bring the 3D board choice of feature `3d-play-flow` in line with its
amended design: the double-layer presets (8 × 8 × 2, 14 × 14 × 2,
25 × 16 × 2) are dropped, leaving the three cube presets — Beginner
(6 × 6 × 6, 10), Intermediate (8 × 8 × 8, 40) and Expert (12 × 12 × 8, 130)
— plus Custom and Random; the custom no-guess switch is disabled on any box
with a side of exactly 2 cells, because such a box is never a no-guess
board; and the records' 3D preset list follows, so labels, the board listing
and the Records 3D picker name only the cube presets.

## Acceptance criteria
- `PRESETS` in `js/mode3d/board-choice.js` holds the three cube presets in
  the order Beginner, Intermediate, Expert, and `FAMILIES` holds only the
  cube family; the 3D board choice screen shows those three presets plus
  Custom and the Random fill, with no double-layer group left in the markup
  or styles.
- `DEFAULT_CHOICE` is cube Beginner. A remembered 3D choice naming a preset
  that is no longer offered (any `double-*` id) opens on cube Beginner.
- `noGuessAvailable` is false for a custom board with any side of exactly 2
  cells, at every size and density; a side of 1 keeps no-guess, as does every
  preset.
- The reason line reads "No-guess isn't available when a side is 2 cells"
  when a side is 2 — taking precedence over "No-guess is available up to N
  cells" when both apply — and the cell-limit line otherwise.
- `PRESETS_3D` in `js/records/board.js` holds the same three cube presets,
  and the test that keeps it equal to the 3D mode's `PRESETS` still holds.
- 3D labels name a cube preset as before ("Cube Expert",
  "Cube Beginner · no-guess"); a record or replay made on a former
  double-layer size is labelled and listed as a custom board
  ("8 × 8 × 2 · 10 mines"), and stays in the history and the library.
- The 3D board listing orders cube Beginner, Intermediate, Expert, each
  without then with no-guess, then custom boards by last played; the Records
  3D picker offers the same three presets.
- Node tests cover the preset values, the default choice, the fallback from
  a remembered double-layer choice, no-guess availability and the reason
  line for a side of 2 (including its precedence over the cell limit), the
  labels and listing of a former double-layer board as custom, and the
  picker's presets.

## Decisions
- The records' preset list changes in this task, not in a separate
  `3d-game-records` task: labels, listing order and the Records picker all
  derive from `PRESETS_3D`, and a test pins it equal to the 3D mode's
  `PRESETS`, so the two lists change together in one commit.
  `3d-game-records`' document was amended as wording only.
- No records or replays are migrated: an old double-layer record keeps its
  board key, and is simply no longer recognised as a preset.
