# Task 124 — Custom form: at least one mine

Archived: 2026-10-14
Status: [DONE]

Preconditions: none

Target: claude

## Goal
Make Classic 2D's custom board form require at least one mine, as the 3D
form already does, so the smallest custom board is 1 × 2 (or 2 × 1) with 1
mine. This is row W-12 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan
(Area: Classic 2D, Size: Small, Phase 1). It closes P-16 of that document,
under its decision 14 ("Zero-mine boards: Require at least one mine — P-16
proceeds"). Today the 2D form accepts a 1 × 1 board and 0 mines: one click
wins in 0.0 s, records 3BV/s 1000.00, shows "New best" three times, keeps an
auto-pinned replay and adds a win and a streak to the overall totals, while
the 3D form refuses the same board with good copy; the 2D info line also
reads "1 cells" and "from 0 to 0" (P-16, Minor, curious players and records
integrity). The work serves curious players who try the extreme board and
every player whose records and totals it would otherwise distort.

## Acceptance criteria
- **At least one mine** (P-16, decision 14): `validateCustom` in
  `js/classic2d/board-setup.js` refuses a custom board with fewer than 1
  mine on the `mines` field, with the reason "Mines must be a whole number
  from 1 to <max> on a <width> × <height> board" (the max formatted as the
  info line formats numbers). The upper bound is unchanged: the smaller of
  the site's cap and the cell count minus one (`maxMines`).
- **No room on a one-cell board** (P-16): a 1 × 1 board is refused on the
  `mines` field, whatever the mine count, with the 3D form's wording adapted
  to two sides: "A 1 × 1 board has no room for a mine beside the safe first
  click — make it at least 2 cells". The check runs after the width and
  height checks, as in `js/mode3d/board-choice.js` `validateCustom`. The
  smallest board accepted is 1 × 2 or 2 × 1 with 1 mine. "from 0 to 0"
  never appears.
- **Info line copy** (P-16): `customStatus` in `js/classic2d/board-choice.js`
  shows the refusals above as its sentence-cased text with a full stop, and
  its valid line pluralises both counts — "2 cells · up to 1 mine", never
  "1 mines" or "1 cells". The `max` it returns, and the mines input's
  `max` attribute it drives, are unchanged; the mines input in
  `index.html` (`#c2d-mines`) carries `min="1"`.
- **Stored and requested choices follow the rule**: `normaliseChoice`
  returns null for a custom choice with 0 mines or a 1 × 1 board, so a
  stored last choice like that reads as `DEFAULT_CHOICE`, exactly as any
  invalid stored choice already does, and is never saved; `boardSetup` and
  `createSession` throw a `RangeError` for it. The Start button stays
  disabled while the form is invalid, through the existing live validation.
- **Existing records left as they are**: records, history entries and
  replays already earned on 0-mine boards are not migrated, purged or
  hidden, and no records format step is added. The records board identity
  (`createBoardIdentity`, the board key parser and label in
  `js/records/board.js`) keeps accepting a 2D board with 0 mines, so those
  entries keep loading, showing on the Records screen and playing back in
  the replay viewer.
- **Tests first**: `tests/classic2d-session.test.mjs` pins `validateCustom`
  refusing 0 mines on a valid board, refusing 1 × 1 with the no-room
  reason, accepting 1 × 2 and 2 × 1 with 1 mine, `maxMines` unchanged,
  `normaliseChoice` and a stored last choice falling back to the default
  for a 0-mine or 1 × 1 custom choice; `tests/classic2d-mode.test.mjs` pins
  the `customStatus` texts (the no-room line, the 1-to-max range line, "2
  cells · up to 1 mine"); `tests/classic2d-fidelity.test.mjs`'s "Largest
  custom board" test checks the side limits with a valid mine count
  (1 × 1 now refused on the mines field, not on width or height). Existing
  tests that used 0 mines on a valid custom board are updated to state at
  least 1. A records test confirms a 0-mine 2D board identity still builds
  and round-trips through its key.
- **What not to break** (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Decisions taken): the live
  custom-form validation; the last-choice memory; no-guess boards as
  separate records; the 3D form, which is unchanged.
- `.claude/domain/features/classic-2d-square-play.md` states the agreed
  design change — a custom board takes at least one mine, so a 1 × 1 board
  is refused and the smallest is 1 × 2 — in every passage that stated the
  old one, and settles the refusal copy as the 3D form's. Nothing else in
  the document changes.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction — `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` decision 14 and P-16, approved without
  condition under § Approved scope and work plan (row W-12).
  - `classic-2d-square-play.md` § Architecture, "Board setup", gives the
    custom mine count only an upper bound (the site's cap and the cell count
    minus one, "the same rule as 3D custom boards"), and § Scope and
    non-goals says custom mines are "within the limit Board setup states";
    the lower bound becomes 1, so a 1 × 1 board is refused and the smallest
    custom board is 1 × 2, which also makes the "same rule as 3D" claim
    true.

  Settled here, without conflict: the refusal copy, taken from the 3D form
  ("no room for a mine beside the safe first click — make it at least 2
  cells") (`classic-2d-square-play.md` § Architecture, "Board setup"). That
  document is normally `/architect`'s; this task updates it for exactly this
  and for nothing else. A further design decision met at implementation time
  is not covered: leave that passage untouched and name it under
  *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
- **Sides stay 1 to 100.** The 1 × 1 refusal is a mines-field refusal, as
  in 3D, so `CUSTOM_LIMITS.minSide` and the fidelity entry "Largest custom
  board" stay as measured; a 1 × 100 board remains valid.
- **Existing 0-mine entries stay.** The review asks only that the form
  refuse the board; touching stored records would need a records format
  step and a history rewrite the review does not approve, and decision 9
  puts records durability work out of this stage. The identity stays
  permissive so old entries load.
- **Pluralisation is the 2D info line's.** P-16 names the 2D copy; the 3D
  info line, which can read "up to 1 mines" on a two-cell box, is out of
  scope (the 3D form is unchanged).
- **Boundaries.** Task 121 (row W-04) also edits `board-setup.js`
  (`DEFAULT_CHOICE`) and `board-choice.js` (the switch and a density hint
  under the info line); neither depends on the other, so either can land
  first and the second merges around the first.
