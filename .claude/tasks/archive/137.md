## Goal
Give a Classic 2D game an end-of-game moment, and make the loop from one
game to the next as short as the player wants it in both modes. This is row
W-11 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan (Area: Classic
2D, shell, Size: Small, Phase 2). It closes P-64 and I-07 of that document,
both approved without condition. P-64 (Minor, every Classic 2D player): a
2D game has no end-of-game moment — no celebration on a win, no reveal
effect on a loss, and the results card appears within about 30 ms of the
last click; its approved solution is "The 3D win confetti and loss wave at
board scale, then the results card after a short delay that any click or
key skips (I-07)." I-07 (Faster loop, enthusiasts on short boards): per
ten-second game the loop costs the end delay, the results card, Play again
and in 3D another Click to play; its approved solution is "Any click or key
during the end delay ends it; Enter or R on results is Play again; Play
again's click is the pointer-lock gesture so 3D skips the ready card, which
stays as the fallback when lock is refused." The results flow stays record
before show.

## Acceptance criteria
- **2D end delay** (P-64): at a Classic 2D win or loss the mode reports
  `canPause(false)` at once and reports `finished(summary, replay)` once,
  after a short end delay (a named constant in the 2D mode, about one
  second, the same order as the 3D `END_DELAY_MS`) run on the mode's clock
  whether or not the tab draws frames. During the delay the mode is
  `ending`: a pause is ignored, the board takes no action, `restart` and
  `leave` cancel the delay, and a `finishNow()` reports the game finished
  at once — the page going away (`pagehide`) calls it, so a 2D game in its
  end delay is recorded finished, never abandoned, exactly as a 3D one. The
  summary and replay are the game's at its end; the timer, the overlay's
  frozen time and mines left, 3BV, click and efficiency accounting are
  unchanged. The leave-page guard treats a 2D game in its delay as a 3D one
  (nothing to lose).
- **2D win effect** (P-64): during the delay a won board plays the 3D win
  re-expressed at board scale on the 2D canvas: a pulse wave spreading over
  the tiles from the last action's cell, and confetti falling over the
  board's own rectangle (not the window), drawn on the board canvas. The
  end-state tiles (unflagged mines shown as flags) are the skin's existing
  static tiles.
- **2D loss effect** (P-64): during the delay a lost board plays the 3D loss
  reveal wave at board scale: starting at the exploded tile, each tile whose
  state the loss changed (mines shown, wrong flags marked) keeps its
  pre-loss tile until the wave reaches it, at a delay proportional to its
  distance from the exploded tile, scaled so the wave reaches the board's
  farthest tile within the 2D end delay on every board up to 100 × 100. The
  loss's existing explode sound and screen flash stay as they are.
- **The board ends in its end state**: when the delay ends — by its timer or
  cut short — the board is drawn in its full end state with no effect left
  running, and the results card shows over it as today. A theme change, a
  resize or a scroll during the effect redraws correctly. No effect state
  touches the game; the view keeps reading every tile from the game.
- **Any click or key ends the delay, both modes** (I-07): during the end
  delay of a Classic 2D or a 3D game, a mouse button press, a key press or a
  controller button press ends the delay at once: the mode reports finished
  immediately and the results flow records, then shows, the results card.
  The input that ends the delay does nothing else — no board action, no
  pause, no Back, no `F2` restart, no fullscreen or sound toggle — and its
  release, a key's auto-repeat and a held button do not reach the results
  card (in particular an `Enter` or `R` that ended the delay does not also
  activate Play again; a fresh press does). Inputs already held when the
  game ended (the release that ended it) do not end the delay. Where the 3D
  end sequence is concerned, everything else stays as
  `.claude/context/app-shell.md` describes it: no camera movement or view
  mode change during the delay, and the results screen's takeover.
- **Enter or R on results is Play again** (I-07): on the results screen,
  `Enter` and `R` start a new board of the same choice, in both modes, the
  way the Play again button does. Task 130 binds `R` on the results screen
  to Play again: this task uses that one binding where task 130 has landed
  and adds it in the same place and form where it has not, so exactly one
  handler binds `R` on results. `Enter` is Play again while focus is on
  Play again (its default focus) or on no action; `Enter` on another
  focused action (Watch replay, Records, Back to menu) still activates that
  action. Key repeats are ignored. Neither key acts on any other screen.
- **3D Play again takes pointer lock** (I-07): Play again on the results
  screen of a 3D game — by click, `Enter` or `R` — starts the board and
  enters play through the same `enterPlay3D(source)` path task 133 uses for
  the main menu's Play again: with a pointer or keyboard, the activation is
  the pointer-lock gesture, so the click-to-play ready card is not shown
  when lock is granted (no visible flash of it), and the ready card stays
  the fallback, with its message, when lock is refused or fails (including
  the browser's re-lock cooldown); a controller's A enters lockless play as
  today. No second path into 3D play is added.
- **Tests first**: a DOM-free end-effect module's tests pin the loss
  wave's per-tile reach time (zero at the exploded tile, growing with
  distance, the farthest tile reached within the 2D end delay on Beginner,
  Expert and a 100 × 100 board), the win pulse's origin, the confetti
  bounded to the board rectangle, and the effect reporting itself done at
  the end of the delay. `tests/classic2d-mode.test.mjs` pins `finished`
  only after the delay on the mode's clock, `canPause(false)` at the end,
  pause ignored during the delay, `restart` and `leave` cancelling it,
  `finishNow()` reporting finished once, and the summary unchanged.
  `tests/classic2d-view.test.mjs` pins tiles drawn in their pre-loss state
  until reached and the full end state at the delay's end.
  `tests/mode3d-end.test.mjs` (or `tests/shell-mode-3d.test.mjs`) pins a
  press during the 3D delay reporting finished at once and nothing else
  acting. `tests/results.test.mjs` pins `Enter` and `R` as Play again, a
  key that ended the delay not activating Play again, and `Enter` on
  another focused action keeping its meaning. A Playwright run through
  `__ms`, with SwiftShader, checks: a 2D win and a 2D loss showing their
  effect and then the results card after the delay; a click during the
  delay showing the card at once in both modes; `R` on results starting a
  new board in both modes; and 3D Play again entering play with lock
  granted without the ready card, and showing the ready card when lock is
  refused.
- **What not to break** (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Decisions taken):
  release-to-act with drag-to-cancel in both modes; the reference input
  conventions; 3BV, click and efficiency accounting; the three-way best
  comparison; auto-pinned best replays; the confirmations that say "It
  will count as a loss"; the last-choice memory.
- The owned documents state the agreed design change — a Classic 2D game
  ends with a win confetti or loss reveal wave on the board during a short
  end delay before the results screen; in both modes any click, key or
  controller button during the end delay ends it at once; `Enter` or `R` on
  results is Play again; 3D Play again's activation is the pointer-lock
  gesture, the ready card the fallback — in every passage that stated the
  old one, and settle the points listed under Decisions:
  `.claude/domain/features/classic-2d-square-play.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/3d-results-records-screens.md`,
  `.claude/domain/features/3d-play-flow.md` and
  `.claude/domain/features/results-screen.md`. Nothing else in those
  documents changes.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction — P-64 and I-07 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md`, approved without
  condition under § Approved scope and work plan (row W-11).
  - `classic-2d-square-play.md` § Interfaces and contracts, "Game finished"
    ("one event per won or lost game with its summary, sent to the game
    shell, which opens `results-screen`"): sent after a short end delay in
    which the win confetti or loss reveal wave plays on the board, cut short
    by any click, key or controller button; a game in its delay is finished,
    never abandoned. § Scope and non-goals gains the end-of-game effect.
  - `3d-results-records-screens.md` § Scope and non-goals ("input frozen
    while the end effect plays"), § Architecture, "3D end sequence", and §
    Interfaces and contracts, "Input during the delay" ("board actions,
    camera movement and view-mode keys are ignored … Esc and the
    controller's Start are ignored until the results screen shows"): any
    click, key or controller button ends the delay at once and shows the
    results screen; that input does nothing else. "Game finished, 3D"
    ("Nothing during the delay can restart or leave the game") still holds.
  - `3d-results-records-screens.md` § Interfaces and contracts, "Play
    again" ("the 3D mode re-acquires pointer lock … as its start does
    today"): the activation is the pointer-lock gesture, so the ready card
    is skipped when lock is granted and remains the fallback.
  - `3d-play-flow.md` § Interfaces and contracts, "Mode contract" ("`game
    finished(summary, replay)` at a win or loss, once the end effect has
    played"): or once the player cut the end delay short.
  - `results-screen.md` § Interfaces and contracts, "Play again": also
    `Enter` and `R` (task 130 adds `R`; whichever lands second states both
    once).

  Settled here, without conflict: the 2D end delay's length (about one
  second, a named 2D constant); the 2D effects are drawn by the Classic 2D
  board view over the skin's static end-state tiles, so
  `square-tile-skin.md`'s non-goal ("Win and loss animations beyond the
  static end-state tiles") stays true of the skin and that document is not
  edited. These documents are normally `/architect`'s; this task updates
  them for exactly this and for nothing else. A further design decision met
  at implementation time is not covered: leave that passage untouched and
  name it under *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
- **"Any click or key" includes controller buttons**: a controller-only
  player has neither, and the shell treats the controller as a first-class
  input everywhere else; one rule for all three keeps the modes and devices
  agreeing.
- **The skipping input is consumed**: it ends the delay and nothing else,
  so a press meant as "skip" can never reveal, pause, restart or activate
  Play again on the card it reveals. With task 130 landed, `F2` during the
  end delay ends the delay (it is a key) and does not restart; task 130's
  "do nothing during the 3D end delay" becomes "ends the delay".
- **`Enter` keeps button semantics**: Play again has default focus, so
  `Enter` right after the card shows is Play again, as I-07 asks; once the
  player moves focus to another action, `Enter` activates that action
  rather than overriding keyboard navigation.
- **One end-delay shape for both modes**: the 2D mode exposes `ending` and
  `finishNow()` as the 3D adapter does, so `js/main.js` treats the two
  alike (skip, `pagehide`, leave-page guard, pause) instead of special
  casing 3D.
- **Win origin**: the 2D win pulse starts at the last action's cell, as the
  3D one starts at the aimed cell.
- **Out of scope**: reduced-motion handling for the new effects (P-56,
  dropped by decision 17 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md`); scaling the 3D end delay
  to the board's diagonal, capped at 2.5 s, which is row W-16 (P-22) — the
  3D `END_DELAY_MS` stays 1000 ms here, and the open question at the end of
  `3d-results-records-screens.md` stays open for W-16; the results card's
  hierarchy and new-best emphasis (P-65, row W-27, task 128); playing the
  2D end effect in the replay viewer; 2D win and loss sounds (row W-09,
  task 135).
- **Boundaries**: task 130 (row W-29) binds `R` on results and `F2` in
  play, task 133 (row W-06) the menu's 3D Play again through
  `enterPlay3D`, task 128 (row W-27) reworks the results view, task 134
  (row W-08) the 2D board view and task 136 (row W-10) the tile skin.
  None depends on this task nor this on them; whichever lands second
  merges around the first.
