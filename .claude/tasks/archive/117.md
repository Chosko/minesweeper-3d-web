# Task 117 — Movement playback holds each sample, then interpolates

Status: [DONE]

Preconditions: none
Feature: replay-playback

Target: claude

## Goal
Bring movement playback of feature `replay-playback` in line with its
amended design. The recorder stores no sample equal to the previous one, so
a gap longer than the sampling interval is a still stretch; playback
currently interpolates across the whole gap, drifting the pointer or camera
through a stretch where it did not move. Playback holds the earlier sample
until one sampling interval before the later one, then interpolates, with
the decoder supplying the interval for the replay's mode and format
version.

## Acceptance criteria
- The replay decoder gives each decoded replay its movement sampling
  interval: the recording constant for the replay's mode under its format
  version (2D 50 ms, 3D 100 ms, as `replay-recording` § Recording constants
  records), since the header does not store it.
- Between two stored samples, the 2D pointer (`pointerAt`) and the 3D camera
  (`cameraAt`) hold the earlier sample until one interval before the later
  one, then interpolate linearly to it; across a gap of one interval or
  less they interpolate throughout.
- The 2D cursor cell and the 3D view mode stay events that switch at their
  recorded times; after the movement cap the 3D camera still holds its last
  recorded position; before the first and after the last sample the
  existing behaviour stands.
- Node tests cover the decoder's interval per mode, a held stretch and its
  final interval of interpolation, a gap of exactly one interval and a
  shorter one, for both the pointer and the camera.
