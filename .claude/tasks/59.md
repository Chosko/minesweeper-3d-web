# Task 59 — 3D mode adapter on the session

Target: claude

## Goal
Rebuild the 3D mode adapter of feature `3d-play-flow` over the 3D session:
the game shell's mode contract — start, pause, resume, restart, leave — with
today's pointer-lock and lockless behaviour, and the started, finished and
abandoned reports at the points the feature document fixes.

## Acceptance criteria
- Start opens the 3D board choice's game in a new session; resume
  re-acquires pointer lock, or enters lockless play when resumed from a
  controller, as today.
- Pause stops the timer and hides the board; during generation it keeps the
  request running.
- `game started` is reported at the first applied reveal; `game
  finished(summary)` at a win or loss; `game abandoned(summary)` when a
  started, unfinished game is restarted or left. A game left before its first
  reveal reports nothing.
- The summary is built by `game-summary`'s builder from the engine's summary
  or counts, the elapsed time, the board choice, the seed and the generator
  version.
- A lost graphics context is reported as a start or play failure and
  cancels a pending generation.
- Node tests cover the reports with a fake session; Playwright drives
  start, pause, resume, restart and leave via `window.__ms`.

## Decisions
- Until `3d-game-records` gives the summary builder its 3D board form, the
  adapter reports the summary fields task 25 reports; `3d-game-records`
  switches it to the builder. Reporting points and the abandoned rule land
  here.
