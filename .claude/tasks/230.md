# Task 230 — Board choice screens to the canvas

Target: claude

## Goal
Bring the Classic 2D and 3D board choice screens in line with the
*Joyful tiles* canvas (Claude Design artifact
https://claude.ai/artifact/LeP32jxNxEnHLUtRwJ3FNC, page "Joyful tiles").
A design review found their foot, switch row, bests tiles, buttons,
density, options, Custom form, Learn 3D panel, notes and window position
drift. Part of the canvas drift fixes; intended feature:
`shared-screen-layout`. Drift ids: E1-E5, E7-E14, E21-E24, the
board-choice parts of K9, K11, K19, the use of K15, and E17, E19.

Sizes: xl 1920×1080; l 1366 and m 1024 are compact; p 390 is touch.

  18px, 10px 20px 14px at l/m, summary 16px/700). Phone: the fixed 92px
  white bar; Records (md, chart icon) and Play (lg compact: 21px, 54px tall,
  play icon) right-aligned.
- **No-guess (E3, E20).** Task 219's switch row: "No-guess" 18px/700 with
  its 14px muted help line stacked left, switch right; label stays ink when
  the switch is locked (3D Custom).
- **Bests (E4).** Task 222's plain stat tiles: label 14px/700 sentence case
  ("Time"), note 13px sans, value 32px (26px at l and m).
- **Icons (E5).** Arrow-left before "Main menu", chart on Records, play
  triangle (24px) on Play, shuffle on Random.
- **Compact (E8).** Head per task 223; Play 21px, min-height 54, padding
  12px 22px; stat value 26px, stat padding 10px 14px; frame 506 tall at
  top 131 at l. Split the `max-width:1439px` dense rule so it does not
  reach phones.
- **Phone (E9, E10).** Back reads "← Back" (aria-label "Back to Main
  menu"), 44px tall; options min-height 56, padding 9px 14px, name 19px,
  meta 14px, check 28px; column padding 16px 18px 112px, sections gap 18;
  an hr between the options and Board settings; the "Last played" chip
  wraps in a `flex-wrap` name row instead of running into the check.
- **Options and columns (K11, E24).** Option padding 12px 18px, min-height
  64, gap 14; the Last played chip at the right just before the check.
  Columns: xl padding 24px 26px, option-column gap 14, options gap 12,

- **Custom form (E11).** Task 222's muted panel (padding 14px 16px, gap
  10); fields fill a `repeat(3|4, minmax(0,1fr))` grid (gap 12 in 2D, 10 in
  3D), 48px text inputs with number spinners hidden; info line left and
  Random (sm, shuffle icon) right on one row.
- **Learn 3D panel (E21).** Muted panel, padding 14px 16px, gap 8 between
  the label and three 16px lines; outro 15px muted.
- **Notes (E12-E14).** Task 221's warn and err notes for refusals, the 3D
  no-guess reason, the density hint (inside the settings block, under the

- **3D window position (E22, E23).** The 3D window centres like Classic 2D

- Board-choice tests and `[slow]` focus walks pass; renders match the
  artboards.
- `.claude/context/app-shell-2d.md`, `app-shell-3d.md` (board choice
  passages) reflect the layout.

## Decisions
- Canvas artboards to check against: J-C2D-xl, J-C2D-l, J-C2D-m, J-C2D-p,
  J-C2D-states ("Custom · fields, info line, Random", refusals, board code
  states), J-C3D-xl, J-C3D-l, J-C3D-m, J-C3D-p, J-C3D-states ("Learn 3D
  selected", "Phone · Learn 3D", custom side of 2, over 8,000 cells).
- Owner decision 1 (E17): keep the Classic 2D custom mine cap at 168 (a
  measured product rule); the canvas's 99 cap and its "up to 99 mines" /
  "up to 56 mines" lines are not adopted. The closing report asks the
  design side to redraw.
- Owner decision 2 (E19): keep dropping "· no-guess" from the bests title
  and summary when the custom board cannot be no-guess.
- Copy changes on these screens (board code lines, valid-code summary,
  refusal periods) are task 242's.
- Depends on tasks 219, 221, 222 and 223.
