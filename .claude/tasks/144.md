# Task 144 — Controller walk, pad-aware Records and viewer, haptics

Target: claude

## Goal
Put every control on the controller's D-pad walk, make the Records screen
and the replay viewer show and use their pad routes, and give the
release-to-act triggers a press cue by rumble. This is row W-25 of
`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan (Area: Input, Size:
Small, Phase 4). It closes two rows of that document. **P-41** (Major for
controller-only players): the D-pad walk skips controls. On the 2D board
choice DOWN from Beginner goes Intermediate, Expert, Start, Mines, Back and
never visits Width, Height or the No-guess switch; in Records DOWN skips
every Watch and Pin button in the tables, and RIGHT from "Pinned first"
lands on Back first. The 3D form walks fine. Approved solution: when the
spatial search finds nothing in the pressed direction, fall back to the next
focusable in DOM order; lay the 2D form out so DOWN from the last preset
reaches the fields and the switch; make each table row's Watch a stop.
**I-18**: Records and the viewer work with a pad but the routes are hidden,
and release-to-act needs a press cue. Approved solution: LB and RB switch
boards and LT and RT switch 2D and 3D in Records; the seek bar shows the
pad's hint; a 10 ms rumble pulse on trigger press and a double pulse on a
flag; vibration on a long-press flag on touch (that last item is task 142's,
see Decisions). The work serves controller-only players setting a custom 2D
board or rewatching a game, and controller players in Records and the
replay viewer.

## Acceptance criteria
- **DOM-order fallback** (P-41): the shell's spatial focus move — the one
  `padNav` makes, and the one the arrow keys make once task 122 routes them
  through the same path — considers only candidates inside the pressed
  direction's cone (see Decisions); when none is there, focus moves to the
  next focusable of the topmost layer in DOM order for DOWN and RIGHT, and
  the previous one for UP and LEFT. At the first or last focusable of the
  layer the move does nothing (no wrap). A range slider keeps LEFT / RIGHT
  as its value step, as today. The fallback lives in one DOM-free helper
  (in `js/shell/navigation.js`, beside `resolveFocus`) that the controller
  path and task 122's arrow path both call, so the two never disagree.
- **2D board choice walk** (P-41): from Beginner, DOWN goes Intermediate,
  Expert, then the custom form's Width; LEFT / RIGHT walk Width, Height,
  Mines and Start; DOWN from the custom row reaches the No-guess switch,
  then Back; UP retraces the walk. Every focusable on the screen is reached
  by the D-pad alone, and the layout changes no more than the walk needs.
  LB / RB still step a focused number field, the live custom-form
  validation still runs on every step, and the last-choice memory is
  unchanged.
- **3D board choice walk unchanged**: the 3D form's walk is pinned before
  the change (the expected sequence written before the code changes) and is
  the same after it.
- **Records walk** (P-41): every Watch and Pin button in the Records tables
  is a stop. Inside a table, DOWN and UP step to the next / previous button
  of the table in DOM order — row by row, a replays row's Pin / Unpin before
  its Watch — so repeated DOWN from the control above a table visits each
  one before leaving the table, and UP retraces it. RIGHT from "Pinned
  first" goes to the first button of the replays table when it has rows
  (the DOM-order fallback), never to Back. Pin / Unpin and Watch, pressed
  with A, act as a click does.
- **Records triggers and bumpers** (I-18): on the Records screen, with no
  modal or confirmation open, LB and RB select the previous / next board of
  the board picker (in picker order, stopping at the ends), and LT and RT
  select 2D and 3D on the mode switch, exactly as choosing them by click
  does (`select`, `setMode`). Focus stays where it was when that control
  still exists, else it goes to the picker's chosen option. Held-button
  suppression applies as everywhere else, so a trigger held through
  entering Records does nothing until released. Elsewhere, LB / RB keep
  stepping a focused number field.
- **Records shows its pad routes** (I-18, "the routes are hidden"): while
  the controller drives navigation (`body.pad-nav`), the picker shows the
  active pad's LB / RB glyphs at its ends and the mode switch its LT / RT
  glyphs (`padGlyphs`, redrawn through `ui.setPad` when the pad changes);
  with a mouse or keyboard they are hidden and the layout does not shift.
- **Seek bar hint** (I-18): while the controller drives navigation, the
  replay viewer's seek bar shows the pad's left / right hint (D-pad ◀ ▶,
  seek); it is hidden otherwise. The seek bar is on the viewer's D-pad walk
  and, when focused, D-pad left / right step it by the viewer's seek step,
  as today. `REPLAY_PAD` (Start / Y, LB / RB, LT / RT) is unchanged.
- **Trigger press pulse** (I-18): on a board in play — 3D play and the
  Classic 2D board, not during the 3D end delay, on a failed board's offer,
  paused, in menus, Records or the replay viewer — pressing RT or LT plays
  a 10 ms rumble pulse on the active pad, the cue that the action comes on
  release. Pressing A or X does not pulse.
- **Flag double pulse** (I-18): whenever a controller action places or
  removes a flag — LT or X, in either mode, including a 3D batch flag —
  the active pad plays a short double pulse (two pulses, the second
  starting after the first ends), replacing the single 40 ms tick 3D plays
  today (`PAD_TICK`). A 2D LT press that flags plays the double pulse in
  place of the press pulse. The explosion rumble (`PAD_BOOM`) is
  unchanged. Mouse and keyboard actions never rumble.
- **Rumble never interrupts play**: every pulse goes through one helper
  that does nothing when the pad has no `vibrationActuator` or no
  `playEffect`, and swallows a rejected or thrown effect; the pulse
  constants are named in `js/gamepad.js`.
- **Tests first**:
  - `tests/shell-router.test.mjs` pins the fallback helper: next / previous
    in DOM order for each direction, no wrap at either end, a found spatial
    candidate taking precedence, and the in-table DOWN / UP rule.
  - `tests/gamepad.test.mjs` pins the cone in `pickInDirection` (a
    candidate outside the cone is not picked; its existing cases still hold)
    and the pulse helper against a fake actuator: the 10 ms press pulse,
    the two pulses of a flag in order with the second after the first ends,
    and silence with no actuator, no `playEffect` or a rejecting one.
  - `tests/records-screen.test.mjs` pins stepping the board picker forward
    and back with the ends held, and the mode change by trigger, through
    the screen's API.
  - `tests/classic2d-cursor.test.mjs` pins that a pad flag and a pad
    trigger press are reported to the shell (for the rumble), and a keyboard
    `F` is not.
  - A new Playwright check `tests/shell-pad-walk.test.mjs` (skipped when
    Playwright or chromium is unavailable) drives a faked controller
    through the 2D board choice walk, the 3D board choice walk, the Records
    walk over a seeded history with replays (each Watch and Pin a stop,
    RIGHT from "Pinned first"), LB / RB / LT / RT in Records, and the seek
    bar hint showing under pad navigation and hidden after a mouse move.
- **What not to break** (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Decisions taken):
  release-to-act with drag-to-cancel in both modes; held-button suppression
  across screen changes; the reference input conventions; the live
  custom-form validation and the last-choice memory. Every existing walk
  other than those this task fixes — main menu, settings, pause and ready
  cards, results, the controls modal — reaches the same controls as before.
- `README.md` § Game controller says that in Records `LB` / `RB` change the
  board and `LT` / `RT` switch 2D and 3D, that the controller pulses on a
  trigger press and double-pulses on a flag besides rumbling on an
  explosion, and the replay table's seek line says the seek bar steps with
  the D-pad left / right when focused.

## Decisions
- **The spatial search gets a cone.** `pickInDirection` today accepts any
  candidate whose centre lies past the current control's in the pressed
  direction (a half-plane), so it almost never finds nothing and the
  approved DOM-order fallback would never fire; that is how RIGHT from
  "Pinned first" reaches Back. A candidate counts only when it overlaps the
  current control on the perpendicular axis or its centre lies within 45°
  of the pressed direction; the scoring inside the cone is unchanged.
  Source: P-41's "when the spatial search finds nothing in the pressed
  direction".
- **Tables walk in DOM order on DOWN / UP.** P-41 asks that each table
  row's Watch be a stop; the Watch and Pin buttons sit at the right of their
  rows, so a spatial DOWN from the picker or the paging buttons misses them.
  Inside a table DOWN / UP take the next / previous button in DOM order, so
  every Watch and Pin is a DOWN stop; LEFT / RIGHT stay spatial.
- **Rumble on a board only, for both modes.** I-18's press cue is for
  release-to-act, which only board actions have; the replay viewer and
  Records use LT / RT for speed and mode and stay silent. The flag double
  pulse applies to Classic 2D as well as 3D, since I-18 names no mode.
- **Pad hints in Records.** I-18 says the routes are hidden; LB / RB and
  LT / RT glyphs beside the picker and the switch, shown only under pad
  navigation, make them visible without a new help surface.
- **Touch long-press vibration lives in task 142.** I-18 lists vibration
  on a long-press flag on touch; task 142 (Touch Classic 2D and touch-safe
  3D entry) adds the 400 ms long-press flag and specifies its single
  `navigator.vibrate` pulse, so this task adds no touch vibration.
- **Pad glyphs on the HUD strip and pause card are task 119's.** P-42
  (keyboard hints shown to controller players in 3D: the HUD strip, the
  bottom hint and the pause cards) belongs to W-01, task 119 (Mode-aware
  help and first-use hints); this task changes none of those surfaces.
- **Related, not preconditions:** task 122 maps the arrow keys to the same
  spatial moves, so the fallback applies to the arrows through the shared
  helper whichever lands first; task 119 owns `js/settings/bindings.js` and
  the help copy, which this task leaves alone; task 142 owns touch
  vibration.
- **Out of scope:** rebinding (P-44, waits for the `input-rebinding`
  feature); accessibility settings (decision 17); focus containment in
  overlays (P-43, W-26).
