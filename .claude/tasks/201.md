# Task 201 — Pause card in two columns at 1024 px and wider, fitting a 1024 × 768 window

Target: claude

## Goal
At 1024 × 768 with a mouse the pause card runs past the bottom of the
window in both modes, so a player has to scroll to reach the lower controls
and settings; a 3D game's card, with its longer controls list and the look
settings, overflows far more than the Classic 2D one. Task 199 left this
open (its criterion put the card's height out of scope). Lay the card out
with the kit's column composition, so that at the kit's one breakpoint and
wider the card is two columns side by side and fits the window with no
scrolling, without shrinking any text and without dropping any of its
contents.

## Acceptance criteria
- At 1024 px wide and wider, the pause card is composed with the kit's
  column composition (`ui-screen--columns` on the card, a
  `ui-columns--two` inside it): the first column holds the title, the board
  and status lines, the warning note, the actions (or the confirmation in
  their place) and the Settings panel; the second, wider column holds the
  controls list panel. Below that width the card stays one column in its
  current order.
- At 1024 × 768 with a fine pointer, in both themes, the whole pause card
  lies inside the viewport — top, bottom, left and right — with no
  scrolling of the card or the page, for a Classic 2D game in play and for
  a 3D game in play (look settings showing), and with the restart
  confirmation showing in place of the actions. Every key cap row of the
  controls list stays on one line and unclipped, as task 199's test
  requires.
- No text shrinks: every font size on the card (title, status lines,
  buttons, slider and switch labels, controls list text, `.ui-stat__label`
  and `kbd` sizes) is the same as before the change, at every pointer.
- The card keeps every control it has today in both modes: the look
  sensitivity and invert Y shortcuts on a 3D game's card only, Sound and
  volume in both. Default focus, Esc, Back and the controller's focus walk
  over the card behave as before.
- **Tests first**, before the markup and CSS change: a browser test at
  1024 × 768, fine pointer, both themes, opens the pause card for a Classic
  2D game and for a 3D game and asserts the card's rectangle is inside the
  viewport, the card and the document do not scroll
  (`scrollHeight <= clientHeight` for both), and the key cap rows are whole.
  It fails until the layout changes. Extend or sit beside the 1024 × 768
  test in `tests/classic2d-mode.test.mjs` added by task 199, whose
  "pause card inside the viewport's width" assertion this one subsumes for
  the height.
- `css/components.css`'s screen-composition comment for the pause card and
  the `ui-screen--columns` line (which names the screens laid out with
  `ui-columns`) describe the two-column card.
  `.claude/domain/features/screen-components.md` names the pause card
  beside the Records and Settings screens as laid out with the column
  composition (§ Scope and non-goals, the column composition bullet). Nothing else in
  that document changes.
- `.claude/context/app-shell-markup.md` (the pause card bullet) describes
  the new layout.

## Decisions
- **Two columns, not hiding settings.** The look settings are already
  hidden on a Classic 2D card, which still overflows, and
  `.claude/domain/features/settings.md` § Pause-card shortcuts requires
  them on a 3D game's card; hiding more would contradict the design, while
  the kit's column composition is the documented way a screen uses the
  width at the 1024 px breakpoint (`screen-components.md` § Scope and non-goals). A
  1024 × 768 window is at that breakpoint, so it gets the two columns.
- **The controls list alone in the wider column.** It is the tallest part
  of the card (by far in 3D); putting the title, actions and settings
  beside it, rather than a full-width title above both columns, keeps the
  second column's height to the list itself so the 3D card fits.
- **No text shrinks** — task 199's Decisions forbid trading text size for
  fit. If the two columns do not fit the 3D card at 1024 × 768 with every
  size intact, that is a finding to report under *Follow-ups*, not a
  reason to shrink text or drop content.
- Settled here, without conflict: `.claude/domain/features/screen-components.md`
  § Scope and non-goals leaves which screens beyond Records and Settings use the column
  composition open; this task adds the pause card to that list.
  `screen-components.md` is normally `/architect`'s; this task updates it
  for exactly this and for nothing else. A further design decision met at
  implementation time is not covered: leave that passage untouched and
  name it under *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
