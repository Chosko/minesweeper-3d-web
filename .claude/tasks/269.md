# Task 269 — Play in Hexagon and Triangle

Target: claude

## Goal
Part of feature `flat-grid-modes`: a Hexagon or Triangle game plays on the
Classic 2D session in the Classic 2D game window, by mouse, keyboard,
controller and touch, with Classic 2D's help, sound, rumble, end delay and
end effect, and a finished game goes to the shared results screen.

## Acceptance criteria
- Play: safe first click; standard boards placed at once; no-guess boards
  from the mode's generation client with the generating card and failure
  offer (retry or play a standard board); pre-generation for Play again;
  the timer from the first reveal; pause hiding the board; restart and leave
  counted as losses after the first click. A generation worker failure gets
  the same failure offer.
- The game window is Classic 2D's: head (Swap clicks, "<Mode> · <board>",
  Pause), counter strip with Restart, the board in its board frame over the
  mode's geometry and tiles, the live figures panel against the board's
  bests, the foot line, and the narrow and phone layouts. Only one flat game
  is open at a time.
- Inputs: the mouse with its button swap, the keyboard and controller
  cursor moving by the grid's geometry, touch with the Tap choice,
  long-press flag, chord on a number, pan and pinch zoom, all with Classic
  2D's bindings.
- Help: the pause card's controls list, the H panel and the HUD strip follow
  the active device. The first-use hint strip and the one-time scroll hint
  share Classic 2D's first-use record.
- Sound and rumble per action as Classic 2D's. The win wave, confetti and
  the loss wave from the exploded tile spread by distance between tile
  centres.
- Results: the shared results screen with time, 3BV, 3BV/s, efficiency and
  the three-best comparison; its board line leads with the mode's name; Play
  again, Records and Main menu work as after a Classic 2D game. The
  game-in-progress marker covers these modes.
- Browser tests play a Hexagon and a Triangle game to a win and a loss on
  fixed boards, by mouse, keyboard and touch.

## Decisions
- **Replays for these modes are `flat-grid-records-replays`' (tasks
  274-275).** Until they land the replay recorder refuses the `hex` and
  `tri` graphs; recorder failure never interrupts play, so the game ends
  without a replay and Watch replay is not offered. This task asserts
  nothing about replays or per-mode records counters beyond the results
  screen showing.
