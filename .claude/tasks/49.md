# Task 49 — Records history chart and recent games list

Target: claude

## Goal
Add the stats history of feature `records-screen`: a small canvas line chart
of 3BV/s and efficiency per won game over time for the chosen board, drawn
from design tokens in both themes, and a paged list of recent games with
outcome, time, 3BV/s and efficiency.

## Acceptance criteria
- The chart draws 3BV/s and efficiency of the board's won games in play
  order on a canvas, with colours from the token reader, and redraws on
  theme change and on a recorded game; no external chart library.
- The chart has an accessible text alternative; the figures panel and the
  games list carry the same numbers in text.
- The recent games list shows newest first with outcome, time, 3BV/s and
  efficiency, paged so a long history stays responsive; paging is
  reachable by keyboard and controller.
- Each game's time is shown to the tenth of a second, truncated (47.38 s
  shows as `47.3 s`), through the results screen's time formatter
  (task 46) rather than a second copy; a Node test checks a row's time.
- Chart scaling and paging logic are DOM-free and Node-tested; a Playwright
  screenshot of the chart in both themes over a seeded history is checked.

## Decisions
- The list's time format is `results-screen`'s, per `records-screen.md`
  § Architecture (Recent games list).
