# Task 175 — Counter readout and figures panel kit components

Target: claude

## Goal
Add the in-game components of feature `in-game-layout` to the kit: the counter
readout (digits and icon as parts, so a theme can draw plain figures, LED
digits on black or anything between) and the figures panel (figure rows with
label, figure, detail line and an optional progress bar, and a
controls-reminder foot), each catalogued, structured in the base stylesheet,
styled by *Joyful tiles* as J-Components draws them (its counter strip,
counter, live figure and live figures panel) and shown in the gallery.

## Acceptance criteria
- Both components are declared in `js/ui/kit-catalogue.js` with their parts
  (optional ones marked, the counter's icon among them) and documented as
  markup patterns in the head comment of `css/components.css`.
- The base stylesheet gives them layout only; *Joyful tiles* styles every part
  as J-Components draws it and still passes the conformance suite. The
  figures panel has an optional head bar (title and an action) for the 3D
  game's panel (task 177).
- The figures panel is a polite live region whose announcements are throttled
  to at most once a second; the counter readouts are not announced on every
  change.
- The gallery shows both under any registered theme, the panel with and
  without its progress bar and pace row.
- `tests/components.test.mjs` covers the markup patterns, roles and the
  live-region attributes; contrast checks pass under *Joyful tiles*.

## Decisions
- **The canvas is the source** (owner, DESIGN-CHANGES §1): J-Components,
  not the earlier S3D-Game artboard.
- **Settled design detail** (`.claude/domain/features/in-game-layout.md` §
  Scope, *Counter readouts*): the component and part names. The feature
  document is not edited here; task 179 records what holds at ship time.
