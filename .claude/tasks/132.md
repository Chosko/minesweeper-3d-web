# Task 132 — Shell without WebGL 2

Target: claude

## Goal
Let a browser without WebGL 2 play Classic 2D, read Records and change
Settings, with only the 3D game turned away. This is row W-03 of
`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Approved scope and work plan (Area: Shell, Size:
Small, Phase 2). It closes P-04 of that document (Major): without WebGL 2
the whole game refuses, Classic 2D, Records and Settings included, though
the 2D board is Canvas 2D. Today `js/main.js` creates the 3D renderer at
start-up and, when that throws, shows the full-page "Cannot start
Minesweeper 3D" card and stops. P-04's approved solution: "Start the shell
without the demo scene; keep Classic 2D, Records and Settings working;
disable the 3D entry with the reason under it and show the current card
only when 3D is picked." Decision 13 of § Decisions taken settles that
WebGL 2 is not a hard requirement: "Classic 2D, Records and Settings run
without it". The work serves players on locked-down browsers, remote
desktops and blocklisted GPUs.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Start-up without WebGL 2** (P-04, decision 13): when the 3D renderer
  cannot be created, the page no longer shows the "Cannot start" card and
  no longer stops. The shell starts as usual (settings, records, replay
  library, last choices, main menu) with no renderer. One availability
  flag (for example `WEBGL2`, true when the renderer was created) is the
  single place every 3D-only path reads.
- **Static background in place of the demo scene** (P-04): the menu
  screens (menu, both board choices, coming soon, Records, Settings) show
  a static background built from the theme's tokens, correct in the light
  and dark themes, instead of the decorative 3D demo board. No demo board
  is generated and the `#scene` canvas is hidden. With WebGL 2 present the
  demo scene stays exactly as today.
- **Nothing calls the renderer without one**: the frame loop keeps
  running (controller polling, `mode2d.tick()`, the replay viewer's tick)
  and skips every renderer step (backdrop orbit, toggles, spacing, `sync`,
  picking, `render`). Resize, `applyPixelRatio` (including the render
  resolution setting), the menu backdrop and the 3D replay scene do
  nothing. No error reaches the console during a session.
- **Classic 2D works end to end** (P-04): board choice, generation, play,
  pause, restart, results, Play again, Records from the results screen,
  back to the menu, with the same input and records behaviour as with
  WebGL 2.
- **Records and Settings work** (P-04): the Records screen opens on
  either side of its 2D | 3D switch; the 3D side's board picker, figures,
  history chart and kept replays can be picked and read. Every Settings
  control works and is saved; the graphics settings are saved and take
  effect on a later visit with WebGL 2.
- **2D replays play**: Watch on a 2D replay, from the results screen and
  from the Records library view, opens the replay viewer and plays it as
  today.
- **3D replays say why they cannot play**: Watch on a 3D replay stays
  offered on the Records screen and opens the replay viewer, which mounts
  no 3D viewer and shows its failure message instead, for example "This 3D
  replay needs WebGL 2, which is not available in this browser.", with its
  Back working as for the other failure lines. The line is a new entry in
  `FAILURES` of `js/replay/viewer.js`, chosen when the replay's graph kind
  is `box` and the shell has no 3D viewer to offer.
- **The 3D entry is disabled with its reason** (P-04): the main menu's 3D
  entry is a disabled kit menu item (`.ui-menu__item:disabled`) with the
  line "Needs WebGL 2 — not available in this browser" under its label,
  in place of its detail line. It cannot be activated by mouse, keyboard
  or controller, and shell navigation skips it. When the last mode played
  is 3D its "Last played" mark still shows, and the menu's default focus
  falls to the first enabled entry instead of the disabled one. The menu
  model in `js/shell/menu.js` carries the unavailable state and its
  reason, so an entry marked unavailable routes nowhere when opened.
- **No route runs 3D without a renderer**: the 3D mode's board choice,
  `MODES.start('3d', …)`, Play again on a 3D result and the `__ms` start
  hook do nothing without WebGL 2 (the hook reports the reason as an
  error). See Decisions for the "Cannot start" card.
- **Context loss mid-session stays as today**: with WebGL 2 present, a
  `webglcontextlost` during a 3D game, a 3D replay or on a menu screen
  behaves exactly as it does now (the `ctxlost` screen, Reload and Back to
  menu).
- **Copy**: the `<noscript>` line in `index.html` says the game needs
  JavaScript, without WebGL 2. `README.md`'s requirement line says 3D needs
  WebGL 2 and Classic 2D, Records and Settings run without it. The
  `index.html` `onerror` card for game files that fail to load is
  unchanged.
- **Tests first**: `tests/shell-menu.test.mjs` pins an unavailable entry
  (its reason, routing nowhere, the other entries unaffected).
  `tests/replay-viewer.test.mjs` pins a box replay with no 3D viewer
  showing the WebGL 2 failure line, and a square replay still playing. A
  Playwright run with WebGL 2 removed (for example an init script making
  `HTMLCanvasElement.prototype.getContext('webgl2')` return null, or
  chromium's `--disable-webgl2`) checks: no "Cannot start" card and no
  console error; the static background in both themes; the disabled 3D
  entry and its reason; a Classic 2D game to its results; the Records 3D
  side; a Settings change; a 2D replay playing; a 3D fixture replay from
  `tests/fixtures/replays/` (through `__ms.watch(data)`) showing the WebGL
  2 line. The same run with SwiftShader shows the demo scene and an
  enabled 3D entry as today.
- **What not to break** (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md` § Decisions taken): the
  release-to-act input in both modes, the confirmations that say "It will
  count as a loss", the last-choice memory, and the honest empty states
  and storage notices.
- The owned documents state the agreed design change, in every passage
  that stated the old one: without WebGL 2 the menu screens show a static
  background in place of the demo board, the 3D entry is disabled with its
  reason, and a 3D replay is not played and the viewer says why. They also
  settle the points listed under Decisions. The documents are
  `.claude/domain/features/game-shell.md` and
  `.claude/domain/features/replay-playback.md`. Nothing else in those
  documents changes.

## Decisions
- **Design change agreed (user decision, 2026-10-12):** settled by prior
  instruction, P-04 and decision 13 of `docs/UX_REVIEW.md`, approved
  without condition under § Approved scope and work plan (row W-03).
  - `game-shell.md` § Scope and non-goals ("3D opens the existing 3D
    presets and custom board") and § Architecture, "Main menu" ("The
    decorative 3D demo board behind the current menu stays as the menu's
    backdrop"): without WebGL 2 the 3D entry is disabled with its reason,
    and the menu screens show a static background instead of the demo
    board.

  Settled here, without conflict: `replay-playback.md` § Interfaces,
  "Failure", gains the case of a 3D replay in a browser without WebGL 2,
  which is not played and says why, Back still working. These documents
  are normally `/architect`'s; this task updates them for exactly this and
  for nothing else. A further design decision met at implementation time
  is not covered: leave that passage untouched and name it under
  *Follow-ups* as a precise follow-up.
- **No "Cannot start" card for a missing WebGL 2**: P-04 shows the card
  "only when 3D is picked", and the same solution disables the 3D entry,
  so the player can no longer pick 3D. No other player route reaches 3D
  without the entry: Play again on a 3D result needs a finished 3D game,
  and Records has no play action. The disabled entry and its reason line
  carry the card's message, and a full-page card would end a session
  that otherwise works. The full-page card stays only for game files that
  fail to load (`index.html`'s `onerror`).
- **Watch on a 3D replay opens the viewer, which says why**: the viewer's
  failure lines are the place a replay that cannot play says so, with Back
  already handled. Hiding Watch on the Records screen would leave the
  player guessing why a kept replay has no Watch button.
- **Detection is the renderer's creation**, not a separate probe: three.js
  r186's `WebGLRenderer` needs WebGL 2 and throws without it, and that is
  the check `js/main.js` already makes.
- **Out of scope**: any other P or I row of the review; a software or
  WebGL 1 fallback for 3D; any change to the context-lost flow. Entries
  into 3D that later rows add (W-06 quick play, W-21 Learn 3D) read the
  same availability flag when they land.
