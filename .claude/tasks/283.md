# Task 283 — Main menu redesign: play tiles with their own previews

Target: claude

## Goal
For feature `shared-screen-layout` (§ Scope and non-goals, the main menu
buttons and **Main menu**; § Architecture, **Menu previews** and **Main
menu buttons**): rebuild the main menu in the standard screen frame as a
narrower window of buttons only — two columns of play tiles, then Records
and Settings as small entries — each play tile showing its mode's own
cropped preview, with no preview panel, no Quit and no Last played chip.
Exact sizes, colours, crops and positions come from the canvas
https://claude.ai/artifact/LeP32jxNxEnHLUtRwJ3FNC — J-Menu-xl, J-Menu-l,
J-Menu-m, J-Menu-p, J-Menu-states, J-Menu-extra, J-Components *Main menu
buttons* and *Menu entry*, and J-Tokens *Menu entry pastels*.

## Acceptance criteria
- The play tile, the small entry and the phone row replace `ui-mode-entry`
  in the kit catalogue, with their icon, label, reason, preview and lock
  parts; the new pastel and stronger-neutral tokens join *Joyful tiles*.
- Every main menu button is the same height at every size. Rest: a pastel
  of its colour with a pastel edge (small entries the neutral raised
  surface); selected by hover or keyboard focus: full colour (small
  entries the stronger neutral), pushed level with the surface, its bottom
  edge gone; pressed: full colour, sunk below the surface. Disabled and
  locked buttons do not move; a locked one darkens when selected. No drop
  shadow; the smaller edge.
- Layout: Classic 2D · Hexagon, Triangle · Surface, 3D · Daily, then
  Records · Settings; the title bar with no Back; no Quit entry; the foot
  holds only the build label, with no Move / Select key hints.
- Previews, cropped from the middle so no board edge shows: Classic 2D a
  real Intermediate game in progress on the dark scene background;
  Hexagon and Triangle tiles keep their current placeholder art until
  `flat-grid-menu-entries` gives them their boards; Surface the torus,
  Daily a generic calendar icon and 3D a still of the menu's demo board
  cut out on a transparent background (never orbiting), each on
  translucent white over the button colour. 3D without WebGL 2 is
  disabled, its reason under the label, with a locked preview.
- Phone: one column of phone rows, each play row's preview a cropped strip
  inside its right end that never sets the row's height; the disabled 3D
  row reads *Needs WebGL 2*; the foot as J-Menu-p draws it.
- The menu still opens with the last mode played focused, and on Classic
  2D for a fresh profile without WebGL 2; routes, ids and controller
  navigation keep working across the two-column grid.
- Contrast: navy ink passes AA on every pastel and on the stronger
  neutral; the locked label passes as large text.

## Decisions
- **The 3D still** is produced once and shown as an image; how (rendered
  at start-up or shipped as an asset) is the implementer's call.
- **Surface's torus** is the canvas's still image, shown while Surface
  still opens *Coming soon*.
